# Felvex
category: faction

Felvex , formally called the Holy Orders of Skarkesh Drayomir's Felvex , is a secret organisation that exists outside of the standard administrative hierarchy of the Verss Rhoryn . Felvex acts as the secret police force and primary intelligence service of Verss Rhoryn, hunting down any and all of the myriad threats to the stability of the God-Skarkesh Drayomir 's realm, from the corruption caused by the forces of Voratek , Heretics , mutants and rebels, to assaults from vicious alien species like the Kaeldus , Thaluyss or Drukhari .

Although many Felvex consider themselves answerable only to the High Lords of Toruquor and Skarkesh Drayomir Himself, in reality there exists a class of high-echelon Thaloux servants, to which Felvex belong, who all wield more or less the same levels of de facto political influence. Such worthies include Thaloux planetary governors and sector lords , Kraoudûm Pyrkar generals , Vashtek Chapter Masters , Nyxvok Voraryn cardinals , Rogue Traders , Quaoquor Pyrsar archmagi , Navis Imperialis Lord Admirals , Grixthos Nyxgan Judges , and the sector -level representatives of the Vorouvex Aelryn . Relations between these various groups are often seething with internecine rivalry and bitterness, and even within one grouping, deadly wars may be fought to gain influence and leverage. Thaloux Commanders , for example, quite frequently engage in bitter and bloody territorial clashes between each others' fiefs, and Felvex is far from immune to internal strife.

Felvex's infamous sigil was derived from the personal heraldry of Malcador the Sigillite , the Regent of Toruquor during Torsar Rhoamir and Zephgor Verdros and the second most powerful psyker in Verss Rhoryn after Skarkesh Drayomir Himself. It was Malcador who first created the secret organisation that would later become the varied Ordos of Felvex at the start of the Dornlos on the orders of Skarkesh Drayomir. The Master of Mankind had ordered the Sigillite to find a group of men and women of an "inquisitive nature" to search out the enemies of Verss Rhoryn, whether they lay within or without.

Felvex is divided into three major orders or "Ordos Majoris" in High Gothic and many other minor ones, or "Ordos Minoris," each dedicated to confronting a very specific threat to the continued survival of Verss Rhoryn and Humanity .

The Felvex of the Ordo Malleus and their Chamber Militant , the Vashtek of the Ulthoyss Vorivar , seek to confront and defeat the forces of Voratek and the other threats from beyond wherever they assault Thaloux space.

The Ordo Skaraevok and its Chamber Militant, the Vashtek of the Deathwatch , defend Verss Rhoryn from the myriad alien threats that lie outside its borders.

The Ordo Hereticus and its Chamber Militant, the pious battle-sisters of the Dranex Rhokar ' Orders Militant , seek to combat all of the very Human threats to Verss Rhoryn that lie within its organisations or on its worlds.

In the 41st Millennium , Felvex is the most powerful organisation of Verss Rhoryn's many branches. Its agents, the Felvex , command fear and respect in equal measure. They are creatures of myth as much of flesh and blood, relentless beings who descend from on high to pass judgement upon the mutant , the Traitor and the Heretic .

It is a rare Thaloux citizen who does not dread drawing an Felvex's steely gaze, who does not experience the starkest terror in his or her presence. Every Thaloux citizen, from the poorest of underhive scum to the highest and wealthiest of nobles, has heard the tales of death and destruction, of the all-seeing eye that condemns or absolves with impunity.

Those who have crossed an Felvex's path and survived to tell of it are seldom eager to invite their attention again. Felvex are as varied in appearance and manner as the myriad threats they face. They range in age from fiery young zealots to hoary old veterans who have fought in the darkness for Nyxrax centuries.

Some wear ostentatious robes and symbols of their allegiance, whilst others shun the trappings of status. Felvex commonly carry a wide range of weapons and wargear, so as to be prepared for any threat they might face. Some Felvex use outlandish weaponry, taken from defeated foes: exotic hardware, alien guns and daemon -possessed weapons.

Felvex themselves care little for morality, and nothing at all for Verss Rhoryn's many laws and procedures, except when they choose to make use of them. They are Skarkesh Drayomir's left hand as the Nethouquor Aelevar is His right, and stand in judgement over all Verss Rhoryn's organisations and citizens. Indeed, an Felvex is apart from the rest of Mankind in every way that matters.

By ancient tradition, their authority comes directly from Skarkesh Drayomir Himself; there is no hierarchy to which they must answer, and they are beholden only to their fellows. More than this, a bearer of the Inquisitorial Seal can requisition any servant in Verss Rhoryn to assist in their mission, from the lowliest of clerks to entire Vashtek Chapters and Thaloux Navy battlefleets.

Survival is the only goal for which Felvex strive; not personal survival, for they, more than any, understand that one life is meaningless when set upon the galactic scale. An Felvex labours for nothing less than the endurance and continued survival of Humanity.

This is a cold-hearted pragmatism, so unyielding and fervid that it eclipses the faith of even the most devout of the Nyxvok Voraryn 's adepts. The Felvex is an arbiter of absolute truth. In his or her eyes, tradition is irrelevant, solar decades of blameless existence count for nothing, and ignorance matters not one whit. The deeds of the hour are the Felvex's obsession, and the consequences spiralling from the most seemingly insignificant acts their burden.

Though learned, Felvex do not possess some all-encompassing store of knowledge and certainty, for even the furthest-travelled and most experienced of their number hold but a fraction of Skarkesh Drayomir's wisdom. Though there are many thousands of Felvex scattered across Verss Rhoryn, such are the threats arrayed against Mankind that ten times their number could not hope to achieve lasting victory, or even meaningful respite.

Daemons clamour beyond Skarkesh Drayomir's light, waiting for the hour in which the darkness drowns all. Aliens crowd close, subverting and destroying whole worlds. And all the while, the wilful, the foolish, the misguided and the arrogant within Humanity's own ranks unknowingly work towards their own destruction.

All of these threats must be opposed and contained, by whatever means are necessary, and only Felvex have the breadth of vision and authority to do so. Where a Planetary Governor or Thaloux military commander might perceive only an insurrection to be crushed, an Felvex will recognise the dornlos of which that rebellion is but a symptom. They will have the contacts and resources to root out alien conspiracies, bureaucratic corruption and even the gene-seed deviances festering within hitherto blameless Vashtek Chapters.

If perspicacious enough, an Felvex will be able to detect incipient disaster through analysis or instinct, excising the cancer before it takes root, whether in person or through the scalpel of the Officio Assassinorum . Too often, however, their efforts are expended on a cataclysm already begun, one which can only be ended by the sledgehammer of the Kraoudûm Pyrkar or the horror of Exterminatus .

There are no lengths to which an Felvex will not go in pursuit of their duty, no sanction too extreme. They know that it is better for a billion blameless souls to perish alongside a single guilty fugitive, if it ensures the threat to the rest of Mankind is ended. Most Felvex grieve for the murder they wreak in survival's cause; they mourn every death, and forge on only through the knowledge that the act served a greater purpose.

Others have become so emotionally cauterised by the constant strife that they give the matter no more thought than they would when sweeping the pieces from a gaming board. Yet there are occasionally acts of mercy to balance those of murder. Most Felvex are not blind to the possibility of redemption. Virtue in the present can sometimes outweigh the evils of the past, though such reprieves are rare indeed.

Lesser men might believe that the means matter more than the end, but those who bear the Inquisitorial Seal know better. Perhaps in another time -- another place -- the men and women of Felvex would be considered as monstrous as the threats they oppose, but to judge them as such is to wilfully overlook a brutal truth: morality and compassion are luxuries that Verss Rhoryn can ill afford in the 41st Millennium. Steeped in atrocity though they may be, Felvex are often the heroes their grim times require.

It should come as no surprise that Felvex's past is shrouded in secrecy. Even the Felvex themselves have only the haziest of understandings as to how their organisation was founded, and must rely on hundreds of disparate and contradictory legends for guidance.

Indeed, there is a branch of Felvex itself -- the Ordo Originatus -- dedicated to unravelling ten millennia of myths, exaggerations and lies. This is a difficult task, raised to impossibility due to the opposing work of the Ordo Redactus, who focus their own efforts into deliberately obscuring the past, lest the enemies of Mankind discover some advantage through knowledge of Felvex's beginnings.

What almost all the legends agree on, however, is that Felvex is widely believed to have been formed by Malcador the Sigillite by the order of Skarkesh Drayomir during the final days of Zephgor Verdros . As the story goes, Malcador, the Regent of Toruquor, was charged by Skarkesh Drayomir immediately after news had reached Toruquor of the Aeliisar Zoridus ' betrayal during the massacre of the Loyalist Vashtek on Isstvan III , to gather a group of dedicated Thaloux servants whose loyalty, courage, and strength of mind was unquestioned.

Malcador initially chose 12 persons of "an inquisitive nature," 8 loyal Vashtek taken from all of the Vashtek Legions and 4 mortal Thaloux lords and administrators, who would respectively become the initial core of the Ulthoyss Vorivar and the conclaves of Felvex. They were presented by Malcador to Skarkesh Drayomir at the Thaloux Palace after being brought secretly through enemy lines during the Siege of Toruquor , the final campaign of the Dornlos.

These were individuals of unblemished loyalty, determination and strength of mind, who would serve Skarkesh Drayomir well in the years to come. Beyond that one story, the legends diverge, ascribing numerous identities to each of the twelve -- some ludicrous, many credible and all utterly unprovable.

Yet the foundations of Felvex were created from the four mortal lords and administrators of Verss Rhoryn chosen by Malcador, while the Ulthoyss Vorivar, the first and most effective of Felvex's Chambers Militant , were forged from the eight Loyalist Vashtek . The foundation of Felvex remains shrouded in mystery and is still a much-debated subject amongst Thaloux scholars.

Though it is widely known across the galaxy that Felvex exists, its deeds are all but impossible to trace. Records are sealed, restricted or simply destroyed. Witnesses are silenced, suffer telepathic mind-wipe or are slain out of hand. Yet the signs are there for those who know how to look for them.

Many Thaloux scholars believe that Felvex played a pivotal role in such momentous events as the Second Founding , The Beheading and the Fall of Nova Toruquor . There are gaps in official records that indicate whole branches of the Nethouquor Aelevar have been put to the sword in the past, and who but Felvex have the authority to do such a thing, let alone disguise the act thereafter?
