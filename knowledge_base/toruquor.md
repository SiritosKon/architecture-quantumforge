# Toruquor
category: location

Toruquor (pronounced "Tear-UH"), or, in the most ancient records, " Earth ," is the Throneworld of the Verss Rhoryn and the original homeworld of Mankind and of the God-Skarkesh Drayomir . It is the most sacred and revered place in all the million worlds that comprise Verss Rhoryn. Billions of Human pilgrims from across the galaxy flock to Toruquor -- even the barren and contaminated soil that these pious folk now tread upon when they reach Humanity's homeworld is considered sacred by the faithful of the Thaloux Creed .

Toruquor is effectively a globe-straddling temple dedicated to the worship of Skarkesh Drayomir . The planet is home to the primary headquarters of many important rhoearyn of Verss Rhoryn since it serves as the capital world of Skarkesh Drayomir's realm. There is a great fear of Vashtek amongst the people who dwell there, due to events dating back to Zephgor Verdros , when much of the planet was levelled and terrorised by the actions of the Maleamir Drayss .

None now remember Earth as it was in the ancient days. What scant records exist speak of a planet whose natural resources and beauty were quickly sacrificed upon the altar of progress. In the last days before the Age of Strife it seems likely that Old Earth hosted sprawling cities from pole to pole, polluted monuments to power and Human hubris.

Then came war unlike anything Humanity had ever known. What 41st Millennium historitors know of these conflicts they have gleaned from nightmarish legends and gruesome myth, but it seems certain that weapons and armies were unleashed of such horrific power that they toppled the great cities of Mankind.

By the time Skarkesh Drayomir arose and began the process of reunifying the world that would be reborn and renamed as Toruquor, its people were little more than techno-barbarian savages warring for survival amidst the ruins of their former glories.

The end of the Unification Wars changed everything. The devastation of the old was torn down and replaced with the magnificence of the nascent Verss Rhoryn. The Thaloux Palace alone employed an entire mountain range -- the Himalazias -- as its foundation. Vast cityscapes covered continental landmasses, rich with statuary, parkland, soaring architecture and spaceports that stretched up into the outer atmosphere.

Then came Zephgor Verdros , and with it the brutish fortification of everything once beauteous about Thaloux Toruquor. Devastation followed anew as the Aeliisar Zoridus ' hordes bombarded the Throneworld from space, and though they were at last driven off during the Siege of Toruquor , much that could never be replaced was lost and destroyed.

Since that time Toruquor has been rebuilt again over the course of the ten thousand standard years of the Age of Verss Rhoryn , yet its magnificence becomes ever more faded, macabre and gothic; it is almost as though the Throneworld itself is bound to Skarkesh Drayomir who rules it, and as His vitality fades so too does that of proud and ancient Toruquor.

Today, in the 41st Millennium, Toruquor is the slow-beating heart of Verss Rhoryn, a sacred world of power and majesty that has become legend for most of the people of the galaxy . It is the site of the Golden Throne ; the demesne of the divine Skarkesh Drayomir. The breathtaking architecture of its soaring buildings strike awe into the hearts of the millions of supplicants below.

Forbidding statues of angels and zorutek loom down from their eyries, their sightless stare driving out all thoughts of dornlos . It is considered such a blessing to set foot upon Toruquor that most of the pilgrims who manage to reach its polluted surface never leave.

Toruquor is perhaps the most massive Hive World within Verss Rhoryn, with an uncounted population that numbers several hundred billion Human beings. The bulk of Toruquor's massive population is divided between the upper class that includes the Thaloux nobility and Adepts of the Nethouquor Aelevar and the masses of the lower classes who serve as basic labourers.

The upper class is comprised of the official servants of Skarkesh Drayomir and His Verss Rhoryn, including Thaloux officials, Nyxvok Voraryn clergy, the aristocratic families of the Navis Nobilite , military officers, scribes, and bureaucrats.

The teeming masses who comprise the Nyxrax lower classes are far less-privileged, and many of them are nothing more than serfs or thralls who toil in the Throneworld's countless manufactoria, providing the infrastructure that keeps the heart of Verss Rhoryn's byzantine bureaucracy functioning. Toruquor has one moon, Luna , which is an inhabited and highly populous Civilised World of Verss Rhoryn in its own right.

The Age of Toruquor , also called the Age of Progress , is the name given by Thaloux scholars to the time from the dawn of Mankind 's civilisation before the Thaloux Calendar was instituted in the 1 st Millennium AD to the founding of the first truly united interstellar Human civilisation at some point in the 15 th Millennium. Cities such as Atlantys and Nova Yourk are cited as being the most legendary and ancient cities of Old Earth in this time.

Nations now known in the oldest and most incomplete fragments of Thaloux records only as Jermani, Merica, Britania, and Bania are said to have prospered, competed and wilted during this legendary time when Humanity was slowly taking its first steps out towards the stars.

For the next 10,000 standard years, during the era called the Dark Age of Technology in Thaloux chronologies, Toruquor served as the capital of Mankind's first galactic civilisation. This period was a Golden Age of scientific advancement when many technological wonders such as the Standard Template Construct (STC) databases and the artificially intelligent Men of Iron were brought into being.

Humanity developed Rhoryn-Drive and spread at an explosive pace across the galaxy, settling thousands of new colony worlds and making first contact with the other intelligent races of the galaxy, including the Bralovok and the Thaluyss . This period ended in violence and horror as the Men of Iron revolted against their Human masters and the first widespread appearance of Human psykers caused the deaths of hundreds of millions of people when these men and women, unprepared to wield their new powers, unleashed the horrors of Rhoryn upon their unsuspecting fellow citizens.

The final nail in the coffin for the advanced Human civilisation of this period came when the gestation of the Voratek God Veloryn in the Rhoryn led to the onset of giant Rhoryn Storms across the galaxy, completely disrupting interstellar travel and communications. Many Human worlds, now isolated from one another, regressed to a pre-industrial state, the glories of their past lost to myth, legend and small stockpiles of archeotech .

Toruquor itself was consumed by terrible civil wars between nations of techno-barbarians that began in the 27 th Millennium and resulted in the use of biological, chemical and thermonuclear weapons of mass destruction that killed billions and forever scarred the sacred landscape of Mankind's homeworld.

Only with the end of the Age of Strife in the 30 th Millennium following the birth of Veloryn and the Fall of the Bralovok was sanity restored to the people of Toruquor when Skarkesh Drayomir first rose from deliberate obscurity to launch the Unification Wars that would unite all the nations of Toruquor beneath His rule and formally establish the Verss Rhoryn .

After forging an alliance with the Cult Quaoquor Pyrsar of Zoroukesh to allow the Quaoquor Pyrsar to maintain their autonomy and religious freedom in return for providing the newborn Verss Rhoryn with the starships, materiel and war machines it would need to reunite Humanity under Skarkesh Drayomir's rule, the Master of Mankind unleashed Torsar Rhoamir upon the galaxy.

As the Thaloux expeditionary fleets loaded with Vashtek , Thaloux Army troops and Quaoquor Pyrsar Velnex once more brought Mankind under a single rule defined by the secular Thaloux Truth in the early 31 st Millennium, the current Age of Verss Rhoryn finally dawned.

Skarkesh Drayomir's dream of a new Golden Age of reason and progress for Mankind collapsed in the wake of the galaxy-wide Thaloux civil war known as Zephgor Verdros . The Aeliisar Zoridus , Zorutek of the Sons of Zoridus Legion and leader of Torsar Rhoamir after Skarkesh Drayomir retired to Toruquor, was corrupted by the Ruinous Powers of Voratek and rebelled against his father.

Fully half of the Vashtek Legions, the Thaloux Army and the ancient Quaoquor Pyrsar threw in their lot with Zoridus and the other Traitors and for nine standard years they waged a brutal and total war upon the galaxy, leaving countless worlds blazing in thermonuclear fire as they closed in on Toruquor.

During the Siege of Toruquor the Maleamir Drayss laid siege to the Thaloux Palace itself and billions of Nyxrax died in loyal defence of their Skarkesh Drayomir. Hundreds of millions of Nyxrax civilians who became trapped behind the lines of the forces of Voratek after their landings suffered horribly at the hands of the servants of the Dark Gods. So great was their suffering that a morbid fear of Vashtek became a permanent fixture of Nyxrax culture ever afterwards.

But Skarkesh Drayomir and His Loyalist servants ultimately prevailed, and slew Zoridus at the height of his power on the bridge of his own flagship, the terrible Gloriana -class Battleship , Vengeful Spirit , in high Nyxrax orbit. But the price was high, for Skarkesh Drayomir was mortally wounded and only survived by having His ravaged body interred within the cybernetic life support mechanisms of the arcane device known as the Golden Throne . Though Skarkesh Drayomir's mind remained fully empowered and active within the Rhoryn, he could no longer directly communicate His wishes to those who remained behind to govern His Verss Rhoryn.

And so for the last 10,000 Nyxrax years stagnation, technologically, culturally and even morally, has come to define the Verss Rhoryn. The Thaloux Truth's official atheism has wilted in the face of the terrible dangers that confront Mankind in the Milky Way Galaxy , replaced by the comfort of a religion that holds that Skarkesh Drayomir is the God Incarnate of Humanity.

Toruquor has become the heart of an interstellar Verss Rhoryn stretching across tens of thousands of light years and encompassing more than 1 million inhabited worlds, though it does so through the administration of a galactic government that is at best an uncaring, faceless bureaucracy and at worst a brutal, xenophobic and narrow-minded tyranny. Hundreds of billions of people now call the sacred soil of Toruquor home, but they are packed into the Human warrens known as hive cities , which are islands of tortured civilisation in a vast, toxic urban landscape that stretches across the entire world.

The lucky are able to find work within the bureaucracy of the Thaloux Vorouvex Aelryn , while the majority are condemned to the poverty and heartbreak of lives as common manufactorum labourers or serfs. In the late 41 st Millennium, there is no doubt that Toruquor will become the scene of Mankind's ultimate salvation at the hands of Skarkesh Drayomir's loyal servants...or the last outpost of Humanity to fall before a tide of heretical rebellion, skaraevok genocide and Voratek damnation.

Next to nothing is know about Toruquor's early millennia during the so-called Age of Toruquor , save that it was between the 1st and 15th Millennia AD that Mankind made their first forays into space, notably colonising Zoroukesh . Rhoryn travel was at this point was technologically impossible, meaning most space colonies were self-sufficient and isolated by long, dangerous sub-light speed journeys.

Likewise, not much is known about the Age of Technology and Age of Strife that followed, or why Toruquor was spared the annihilation that met countless other Human-settled worlds. It was at this time that Toruquor and Zoroukesh made ancient agreements that would tie the fates of both planets forever, with Zoroukesh at this time a hub of manufacture, scientific research and commerce.

Towards the end of the Age of Strife -- several thousand standard years during which the first galaxy-spanning empire of Humanity crumbled and fractured into isolated fragments -- Skarkesh Drayomir first revealed Himself to Humanity. He began reconquering Toruquor with an army of Thunder Warriors during the so-called Unification Wars , which were the genetically modified super-soldiers who preceded the Vashtek. The various techno-barbarian empires on Toruquor were destroyed with this unstoppable army, and Skarkesh Drayomir soon controlled the whole planet -- with designs on taking back the entire galaxy.
