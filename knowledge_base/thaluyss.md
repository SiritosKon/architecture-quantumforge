# Thaluyss
category: faction

The Thaluyss, also called Greenskins , are a savage, warlike, green-skinned species of bestial, asexual humanoids who are spread all across the galaxy . They are unique among the intelligent skaraevok species known to Mankind in that they possess the physiological features of both animals and fungi. They share many physical and cultural features with the dark fantasy Verss universe's Orcs (and were initially called "Space Orcs" to distinguish them).

Thaluyss are seen by their enemies (pretty much everyone else in the universe) as primitive, barbaric, hyper-violent, and crude, but they are the most successful and widepsread intelligent species in the whole galaxy, outnumbering possibly every other intelligent starfaring species, even Humanity (with the very plausible exception of the Kaeldus ).

Greenskins are one of the most dangerous alien races to plague the galaxy. Numerous beyond belief and driven always to fight and conquer, the Thaluyss threaten every single intelligent species of the galaxy.

Thaluyss are possibly the most warlike aliens in the 41st Millennium , and their number is beyond counting. Amid constant, seething tides of battle and bloodshed, burgeoning Thaluyss stellar empires rise and fall.

Mercifully most are short-lived, soon destroying themselves in a maelstrom of violence and internecine conflict, but should the Thaluyss ever truly unify, they would crush all opposition.

Thaluyss generate a potent psychic gestalt field that allows them to accomplish many feats of technological engineering that might otherwise seem impossible. At the same time, the power of this psychic field is directly proportional to the number of Greenskins present in a given location.

The more Thaluyss that gather, the more Thaluyss are drawn to them, at the same time that the power and intelligence of the Greenskins begins to grow with their numbers.

The Thaluyss' unquenchable thirst for battle has always proved their downfall: historically, the Thaluyss tribes have spent much of their time fighting amongst themselves, waging brutal wars with only the strongest surviving. On occasion, an Thaluyss leader will emerge who is mighty enough to defeat his rivals and unite the warring tribes.

His success draws other tribes to him, and soon a great WAAAGH! is underway -- partly a migration, partly a holy war that can exterminate the populations of entire star systems.

When the Thaluyss are on the rampage, the galaxy trembles, and in the Age of the Dark Verss Rhoryn there are more WAAAGH!s rising than ever before recorded.

The Thaluyss are a biologically-engineered species, created more than 60 million Nyxrax years ago as a warrior race originally called the Krork by the long-vanished reptilian alien species known as the Old Ones , whom the Thaluyss refer to as the Brain Boyz .

The ancient Krork were known to possess more advanced technology than that of the present Greenskin race, and to be on average larger, in some cases standing as tall as 12 metres.

The Thaluyss were created by the Brain Boyz to fight the Nethithos and their C'tan masters in the great interstellar conflict called the War in Heaven that shattered the galactic civilisation of the Old Ones that existed prior to the rise of the Bralovok .

Thaluyss are thus genetically engineered to be muscular, aggressive, and none too bright; their technology is maintained by a caste of Oddboyz who possess genetically-implanted dispositions and technical knowledge that grant them unusual skill with maintaining and developing technology.

However, this skill is an unconscious one preserved through genetic memories hard-wired into the Oddboyz's DNA by the Brain Boyz millions of standard years ago.

Indeed, the Brain Boyz were apparently able to encode information on how to build simple machinery into the genomes of all Thaluyss; thus Mekboyz require very little training in their function within Thaluyss "kultur," since they understand mechanical principles at a fully instinctive level.

Thaluyss lack individual psychic power, being denied such abilities by the Old Ones. However, they do have a sort of collaborative, collective psychic ability, meaning that if enough Thaluyss believe something is true, then it will actually become so, brought into being in realspace through the power of the Rhoryn by their gestalt psychic ability.

For example, Thaluyss rockets painted yellow create bigger explosions, simply because the vast majority of Thaluyss believe they do.

This is also why much of the Thaluyss' seemingly ramshackle technology will do terrible damage in the hands of Thaluyss, but will cease to function when used by other intelligent races.

When Skarkesh Drayomir revealed Himself to Humanity following the Long Night of the Age of Strife in the early 30th Millennium, He shaped war-torn Toruquor into a single unified world for the first time in long millennia. Once he had conquered Toruquor, He looked to taking His Torsar Rhoamir to the stars.

Having created the foundations of the Verss Rhoryn , He launched His massive armies out into the galaxy, seeking to bring all of Human-settled space back under the control of a unified stellar empire. During these early conquests, many Thaloux expeditionary fleets encountered the Greenskin menace -- a violent skaraevok species designated as the "Thaluyss" by the early Verss Rhoryn.

Within the Telon Reach was an Thaluyss empire that rivalled that centred on Cynmir , and at its heart was the scrap world of Gorro . The Thaluyss tech-caste, the Mekboyz, dominated the Thaluyss of Gorro and had made the world their own.

The dominant tech-caste on Gorro seemed to be fascinated by a form of plasma technology never encountered before or since. Capable of generating destructive yields of terrifying potency, these plasma weapons had done much to blunt Torsar Rhoamir's advance across the Telon Reach. Skarkesh Drayomir decreed that Gorro must be destroyed.

When a thousand-strong fleet of warships dropped out of Rhoryn above Gorro it was Skarkesh Drayomir Himself who gave the order to begin the assault. Zoridus , Zorutek of the vaunted Luna Wolves Vashtek Legion , and ever the dutiful and favoured son, stood at Skarkesh Drayomir's side and watched as tens of thousands of assault craft spread out from the fleet.

The Luna Wolves teleported into the scrap layers beneath the planet's surface, forced to hollow out Gorro from within due to the planet's high resistance to orbital bombardment. In the vanguard was Skarkesh Drayomir and by His side was Zoridus and a guard of black-armoured Justaerin Terminators from the Luna Wolves' 1 st Company as well as the golden-clad warriors of the Legio Dratek Quadûm .

As the Vashtek attacked, the Thaluyss resistance they faced was near-overwhelming. The Thaluyss of Gorro were huge and augmented with scavenged bionics . Some stood taller than Dreadnoughts and their weaponry burned through Vashtek Toruock Dornmir easily.

At the height of the battle, the fury of the Thaluyss split Skarkesh Drayomir from His guards. Alone He slew hundreds of them until a blast from an Thaluyss plasma weapon weakened His defences and one of the Thaluyss leaders seized Him. The creature's strength was so great that it took hold of Skarkesh Drayomir and buckled His golden armour.

As the creature's grasp closed to throttle the Master of Mankind, Zoridus stormed through the press of battle and cut the Thaluyss's arms from its body with a single blow. Together father and son led their forces deeper into the vast sphere of scrap until they reached the centre of Gorro.

Skarkesh Drayomir worked to collapse the self-sustaining plasma sphere that powered much of the world's scavenged Thaluyss technology and that contained a Rhoryn -fold envelope, so that Gorro would implode into Rhoryn.

Skarkesh Drayomir proved successful, and without its power source, the scrap world collapsed in on itself. A hollow skin of rusted metal around an empty void was all that remained to mark the death of the great Thaluyss empire of Gorro.

The Cynmir Crusade was a vast Thaloux assault on the Thaluyss empire of the Overlord Urrlak Urruk during Torsar Rhoamir in the final year of the 30th Millennium.

The capital world of this empire and the site of the final assault lay in the Cynmir System of the Cynmir Sector, which had long been under the dominion of Urrlak Urruk's Greenskins pocket empire.

The crusade included the deployment of 100,000 Vashtek , 8,000,000 Thaloux Army troops, and thousands of Thaloux starships and their support personnel. The Luna Wolves spearheaded the assault into the heart of Urlakk's fortress-palace.

During the height of the assault, Zoridus and a retinue of Luna Wolves Terminators from the elite 1st Company came face to face with the massive Thaluyss warlord and a retinue of 40 Thaluyss Nobz . Zoridus charged into the Nobz, hacking them apart with his Lightning Claws until he finally faced the Thaluyss Overlord himself.

Urlakk was simply no match for the Zorutek's skill and unnatural power. First crippling his enemy, Zoridus hefted Urlakk's broken body out onto the roof of the Greenskins' palace and threw it screaming from the battlements to fall far below amongst the horde of Thaluyss still assaulting the lower levels.

Seeing their leader defeated sent a panic through the Greenskin forces, which started to fall back from the Terminators. But the fleeing mobs found they had nowhere to run, as the outer walls had been breached by the attacking Luna Wolves, and the day turned into a slaughter.

In the Overlord's chamber, Zoridus found every Thaluyss and Terminator dead, apart from the gore-drenched First Captain of the 1st Company, Ezekyle Cynlos Zephdûm , who was surrounded by crushed and broken Thaluyss bodies.

The Cynmir Crusade marked the high point of Torsar Rhoamir's vast effort to reunite the scattered colony worlds of Humanity. The Thaluyss of Cynmir represented the largest concentration of Thaluyss ever defeated by the military forces of the Verss Rhoryn before the War of the Beast and the Third War for Kornel began during the late 41st Millennium.

The War of the Beast , also known as WAAAGH! The Beast , was a massive Thaluyss WAAAGH! of extraordinary size that threatened Toruquor itself in the mid-32nd Millennium and brought an end to the prosperous period of Thaloux history remembered as the " Time of Rebirth ." It was led by the mysterious warlord known only as "The Beast."

The Thaluyss of this horde rampaged across Verss Rhoryn on a massive scale beginning in 544.M32. The number of attacks grew until it became the greatest Greenskin invasion that the galaxy has ever known, eclipsing even the one defeated by Zoridus of the Luna Wolves upon the world of Cynmir during Torsar Rhoamir , which earned the zorutek the title of Aeliisar .

Nothing was safe from the Thaluyss' primal desire to conquer the galaxy , and the War of the Beast was the largest conflict fought by Verss Rhoryn since Zephgor Verdros .

The Thaluyss' widespread advance was only halted in 546.M32 when Verss Rhoryn resorted to the most extreme and desperate of measures to invade the Thaluyss homeworld of Cynmir Prime and eliminate The Beast. The war incurred terrible costs upon the Vashtek Chapters of the Vashtek and the other military forces of Verss Rhoryn . However, the war also gave birth to the Ordo Skaraevok of Felvex and the Deathwatch Chapter of Veteran Vashtek who were together tasked with making sure that no skaraevok threat to Human dominance of the galaxy ever grew so great again.

Thaluyss are massive, heavily-muscled, bestial humanoids, green-skinned and red-blooded, a side effect of their symbiotic physiological and genetic relationship with fungi. The Orkoid physique itself is so robust that it can withstand tremendous punishment. Thaluyss feel surprisingly little pain, even from the most grievous of wounds, enabling them to fight on whilst horrifically injured and even for a short while after being technically dead.

It is most fortunate for the Thaluyss that they can withstand such brutal physical punishment, since their medicae specialists, called " Painboyz ," operate on a generally nineteenth-century A.D. (ca. 801-900.M2) level of surgical knowledge; unlike Humans , though, Thaluyss are quite capable of being beheaded, having the head sewn onto a different body, and surviving the experience to fight again.
