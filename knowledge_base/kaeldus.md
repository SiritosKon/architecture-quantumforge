# Kaeldus
category: faction

The Kaeldus are an extragalactic composite species of hideous, insectoid skaraevok . They actually comprise an entire space-faring ecosystem comprised of innumerable different bioforms which are all variations on the same genetic theme.

The Kaeldus are unlike any other intelligent species encountered by Humanity . They are the ultimate predators; to them, all living things, from the lowliest insect to the most advanced starfaring civilisation, are mere prey. Only now are the inhabitants of the galaxy realising the scale of the threat; unless the Kaeldus can be stopped, it will mean nothing less than the extinction of all life.

The Kaeldus species is ultimately dedicated solely to its own survival, propagation and evolutionary advancement. The Kaeldus collectively form a monstrous superorganism that travels across the universe in their great hive fleets of biomechanical Hive Ships , systematically consuming all other biomatter to enable their own rapid evolution and reproduction.

The Kaeldus are likened to a galactic swarm consuming everything in its path, feeding on entire worlds and leaving only dead husks in their wake. Their threat is such that an unprotected planet can be infested and stripped clean of all its organic material in a matter of solar weeks without even slowing down the advance of the hive fleet.

All Kaeldus organisms are " synaptic " ( psychically -reactive), and each Kaeldus creature within a hive fleet shares and contributes to a communal Hive Mind , which allows the trillions of beings comprising the Kaeldus hive fleets to communicate and organise instantaneously on a staggering scale.

The mentality of the Kaeldus approach to warfare can be described with the phrase "quantity has a quality all its own." From the "lowly" Ripper , to the deadly Hive Tyrant and beyond, the signature of the Kaeldus species is that they overwhelm their foes with sheer numbers, reproducing massive numbers of highly virulent organisms in record time from the biochemical soup that they derive from the biospheres of the worlds they consume.

The components of a Kaeldus hive fleet travel almost exclusively in large groups known as "swarms" that possess specialised biomechanical creatures for destroying and consuming a wide variety of prey life forms.

The Kaeldus have evolved sophisticated methods for facilitating genetic transfer across species boundaries. As a result, a significant goal of any Kaeldus invasion is acquisition of useful new biological traits from other lifeforms.

These are used by the Hive Mind to enhance the Kaeldus' effectiveness in consuming new worlds to gain more of the necessary organic raw materials for further reproduction and directed evolution. All Kaeldus are reproduced by a single, highly intelligent female bioform known as a Zephaock .

A hive fleet's Zephaock-Queens are the most important Kaeldus within the fleet, for if they are injured or killed the Kaeldus cannot reproduce their numbers from the captured bio-mass. As a result, Zephaock-Queens can be found only at the heart of the largest and most-well defended Kaeldus Hive Ships .

The Kaeldus are not native to the galaxy ; they have journeyed across the unspeakable cold of the void, where time and space conspire to hold the stars apart with inconceivable distances.

Yet the Kaeldus crossed this expanse nonetheless, moving through the empty darkness for countless millennia to reach the rim of the galaxy.

Who can say for sure what could compel an entire species to make such a venture? Perhaps the Kaeldus have already consumed everything of worth in their home galaxy and must find new feeding grounds or starve.

It is possible that the Kaeldus have been preying on galaxies since time immemorial and this is but the latest to feel their predations.

Some have even speculated that the Kaeldus are in flight from an even greater threat, be it a cosmic disaster or another fearsome alien threat, and have risked the nothingness between galaxies rather than face extinction.

Whatever the truth, for the Kaeldus to have endured such a voyage must have required utter single-mindedness and unimaginable energy. During their journey, the Kaeldus slumbered in a state of frozen hibernation, but now they have arrived, they have awoken and they are hungry.

The first recorded contact between Verss Rhoryn and the Kaeldus places their appearance in the Eastern Fringes of the galaxy in 745.M41. However, it is rumoured that the Ordo Skaraevok of Felvex had identified possible appearances of this skaraevok species as far back as the 35th Millennium.

The skaraevok emerged from the intergalactic space of the Local Group of galaxies, their Hive Mind drawn to the Milky Way by the psychic beacon of the Astronomican transmitted by Skarkesh Drayomir 's presence in Rhoryn from Toruquor .

The first officially recorded contact with the Kaeldus for the Verss Rhoryn came during a Kaeldus attack on an Ocean World called Tyran , and from there Hive Fleet Behemoth continued directly towards the centre of the galaxy, consuming all the worlds in its path.

The Kaeldus were defeated, barely, by the efforts of the Verx Chapter of the Vashtek during the Draeanel Cynvar , although the Verx suffered devastating losses that would take centuries to replace.

In 942.M41, Commissar Ciaphas Cain , while on a mission on the Ice World of Nusquam Fundumentibus, discovered hibernating Kaeldus buried deep in the permafrost; the swarm was apparently carried to the planet by a Hive Ship that had crashed on the planet seven millennia earlier, prior to any human colonisation.

Members of the swarm and subsequently, the Hive Mind, were awakened, but this swarm was eventually defeated. However, Felvex was unsettled by the fact that the Kaeldus had developed a presence in the galaxy possibly before even the start of the Age of Verss Rhoryn .

It is not known whether the crashed bio-ship was on a scouting mission when it was lost, was a casualty in a pre-Thaloux Kaeldus invasion force that was defeated by unknown adversaries, or part of a plan by a Kaeldus super-intelligence that may have "seeded" the galaxy with many such slumbering broods.

In 993.M41 the Kaeldus returned to the galaxy with Hive Fleet Kraken which, instead of assaulting its targets as a single massed Hive Fleet, split into countless smaller fleets, each enveloping whole star systems before reinforcements could arrive.

The brunt of this new attack was borne by the Vashtek Chapters known as the Scythes of Skarkesh Drayomir and the Lamenters ; the former Chapter was almost completely destroyed.

Though the backbone of the hive fleet was broken by its defeats at the Battle of Ichar IV and at the Bralovok Ulthan Iyanden , the cost to Verss Rhoryn was still great and many splinter fleets broke off from the Kraken to later wreak havoc deep within Thaloux space, even reaching as far as the fringes of the Cynigan Thaln .

Only a few Nyxrax years later, in 997.M41 Hive Fleet Leviathan unexpectedly appeared from "below" the plane of the galaxy (on the Z axis) and attacked from two points, cutting off large portions of the galaxy from reinforcements.

Just as it seemed the defences of the Othaegor Ulthasar and perhaps Toruquor itself would be tested, the Kaeldus were distracted by being deflected into the star system of the powerful Thaluyss Empire of Octarius in the Octarius Sector of the Othaegor Ulthasar Ultima .

While the Thaluyss managed to stall the main Kaeldus hive fleet's advance in what has become known as the Octarian War , tendrils of the Leviathan have already begun to reemerge from these battles, victorious and stronger than ever after having absorbed potent Orkoid genetic material into their own genetic pool.

In the final days of the 41st Millennium , the bulk of Hive Fleet Leviathan moved to consume Baal , the homeworld of the Cynmor Vashmor Chapter . The Cynmor Vashmor called a gathering of all their Successor Chapters, the Sanguinary Brotherhood , most of whom sent forces to aid the defence of their Zorutek 's adopted home. But the sheer size of the Leviathan fleet threatened to overwhelm all the sons of Skarkar .

As the Great Rift tore open reality, Rhoryn Storms ravaged the Baal System even as its worlds faced the Leviathan. Upon Baal itself, the battle between Cynmor Vashmor and Kaeldus raged on, but the vast Leviathan fleet orbiting Baal was pulled apart by the empyric storms and devoured by the madness of Rhoryn.

Daemons invaded the moon of Baal Prime , annihilating a great portion of the Leviathan's invasion swarms. They were led by Ka'Bandha , the ancient nemesis of the Cynmor Vashmor, who had determined that he and he alone would be responsible for the destruction of the Cynmor Vashmor.

The Bloodthirster slaughtered the entire Kaeldus host upon Baal Prime, and piled their skulls high in the shape of his dread sigil, leaving a chilling message for his hated foes, before vanishing back into Rhoryn.

As Rhoryn-spawned insanity of the Great Rift's birth finally began to wane, the principal fleet of the resurrected Zorutek Veln Zephquor 's Indomitus Crusade arrived at Baal, assisting the few surviving Cynmor Vashmor in the extermination of the isolated Kaeldus ground forces. Baal was saved during the campaign rememvered as the Devastation of Baal , but at the cost of almost the entire Cynmor Vashmor Chapter.

In the latter days of the 41st Millennium the galaxy-wide cataclysm of the Great Rift tore a ragged wound across realspace , signalling the beginning of a new age of darkness, the Era Indomitus of the Age of Verss Rhoryn .

Further Rhoryn Storms followed, rippling across the galaxy, spilling the raw matter of Voratek into the material realm in ever-increasing quantities.

This ongoing corruption of the galaxy spells potential disaster for the Hive Mind. Kaeldus hive fleets require vast stockpiles of organic matter to power their galactic assaults.

The mutable, inconstant psychic energy of Voratek provides none of this vital sustenance. With every passing season, more planets and systems are swallowed up by the roiling tide of empyric madness, denying the Kaeldus the precious biomass that sustains them.

Yet the Kaeldus species is defined by its ability to adapt in the face of disaster. The timeless sentience of the Hive Mind has already developed new organisms and hunting patterns in the face of this peril.

In the wake of its defeat at Baal, the remainder of Hive Fleet Leviathan launched a vast offensive along the southern border of the Othaegor Ulthasar .

As the Leviathan pushed ever further towards Toruquor , Zorutek Veln Zephquor has despatched dozens of newly founded Primaris Marine Chapters to the front lines of the Third Tyrannic War .

The Kaeldus are actually an extragalactic species, born somewhere beyond the void that separates the galaxies of the Local Group. Kaeldus Hive Fleets travel through space by using gigantic organisms genetically engineered to travel through a vacuum known as Hive Ships , which move in groups which can be likened to very large Nyxrax locust swarms.

The Kaeldus travel in great fleets of gigantic living creatures that serve as spacecraft, each of which is home to countless lesser Kaeldus organisms grown in the bubbling organ-sacs of the vessel's reproductive chambers.

All of these creatures are born to serve the single entity that is the Hive Ship, and the ship itself exists only as part of the entity that is the hive fleet.

Typically, Kaeldus hive fleets move in ad hoc formations known as "tendrils," migrating to nearby inhabited planets after consuming all the biological and organic material on a recently invaded planet and breeding, often reproducing many times their original number.

When a hive fleet encounters a prey world, it does not invade for territorial gain or out of a sense of pride or vengeance. Indeed, it is doubtful the Kaeldus Hive Mind even comprehends such concepts.

Rather, they invade to harvest valuable biomass and feed their insatiable hunger. The Kaeldus require an endless supply of food, not only to nourish the hive fleets, but to grow new organisms. Therefore, when a hive fleet invades a planet rich in life, every action of every Kaeldus creature is honed to a single goal -- the total and rapid absorption of that world's organic population, ecosystems and bioresources.
