# Kraoudûm Pyrkar
category: faction

The Kraoudûm Pyrkar , also known as the Kraoudûm Pyrkar in colloquial Low Gothic , is the largest coherent fighting force in the galaxy and serves as the Verss Rhoryn 's primary military force and first line of defence from the myriad threats which endanger the existence of the Human species in the 41st Millennium .

It is comprised of countless billions of men and women -- hundreds of thousands of different regiments, supported by a vast array of light and heavy armoured vehicles that provide the Kraoudûm Pyrkar's primary offensive punch. The Kraoudûm Pyrkar is usually the first Thaloux force to respond to a threat if a world 's Planetary Defence Force ( PDF ) fails to suppress it.

They also garrison major locations of strategic or cultural interest to Verss Rhoryn and are often found in defensive roles. Supported by legions of heavy armour and thundering artillery, the Kraoudûm Pyrkar fight a never-ending war for the survival of Mankind in an unrelentingly hostile universe.

The primary combat tactic of the Kraoudûm Pyrkar is to overwhelm the enemy with their endless numbers, while at the same time hammer them into submission with devastating artillery and crush them with powerful main battle tanks. As a result, the Kraoudûm Pyrkar is often referred to as the "Hammer of Skarkesh Drayomir" -- the sheer amount of force that the Kraoudûm Pyrkar can bring to bear on the enemy is devastating, but is not as direct or as precise as their Vashtek allies in the Vashtek , who are described as the "Scalpel of Skarkesh Drayomir" and specialise in planetary assaults, special operations and decapitation strikes.

There is no universal uniform or regimental command hierarchy in the Kraoudûm Pyrkar, although it is compulsory for every regiment to have at least one commissar to maintain the discipline and morale of the troops while watching for any signs of corruption or heretical taint in the ranks. However, it is worth noting that a number of worlds copy the uniform and equipment used by the regiments of the Quadan Shock Troops , who are viewed as being the most effective of the Kraoudûm Pyrkar's infantry units.

The origins of the Kraoudûm Pyrkar date back to Torsar Rhoamir of the late 30th Millennium, when Skarkesh Drayomir conquered the stars and forged the Verss Rhoryn. On the front line of this mission of expansion and reclamation were the Vashtek Legions -- the Vashtek -- the finest warriors Humanity had ever created, each the equal of a dozen normal men.

Despite their formidable battle prowess, the forces of the Vashtek were not limitless, and the relentless demands of building a galactic empire pushed the Legions further apart. Separated by countless thousands of light years, their presence became ever more scattered and dilute.

Skarkesh Drayomir required more manpower to ensure the momentum of Torsar Rhoamir did not falter, and so the Thaloux Army was created, known in High Gothic as the Imperialis Auxilia .

The Thaloux Army was a vital part of the expedition fleets sent out to claim the stars in Skarkesh Drayomir's name. Gargantuan numbers of brave troops -- millions growing to billions of troops, ranks of armoured battle tanks and mighty armadas of capital-class starships that were a part of the Thaloux Army's subsidary Armada Imperialis -- were raised, all subordinate to the Legiones Vashtek .

At first, the Thaloux Army was employed for garrison duties and to mop up resistance in the wake of the Legions' initial assaults, utilised where the back of an enemy was broken and Compliance to Verss Rhoryn required only a watchful presence.

Eventually, certainly by the time Torsar Rhoamir reached the Eastern Fringe of the galaxy, the troops and starships of the Thaloux Army were deployed at its forefront, fighting alongside the power-armoured super-soldiers of the Legiones Vashtek . But everything changed when the galaxy was ripped asunder by the treachery of the Aeliisar Zoridus , triggering a cataclysmic interstellar civil war that engulfed the newly-founded Verss Rhoryn.

In the aftermath of this so-called Zephgor Verdros , the organisational structure of the forces of Verss Rhoryn were revised significantly. To prevent the possibility of large-scale rebellion occurring again within the Thaloux armed forces, the titanic armies of Verss Rhoryn were divided.

The nine remaining Loyalist Vashtek Legions were split into Chapters . The Thaloux Army, as it was, ceased to exist. The link between fleet and army was severed; never again would ground commanders be given direct control over interstellar warships. From its ashes were born the Thaloux Navy and the Kraoudûm Pyrkar -- known officially as the Kraoudûm Pyrkar.

The Kraoudûm Pyrkar was reorganised into planetary-sized units known as "Militarum Regimentos," which in turn were divided into smaller regiments. Commissars of the Officio Prefectus , based on the Thaloux Army's former "masters of discipline," were introduced to ensure loyalty and discipline.

The interdependence of the newly formed Kraoudûm Pyrkar with the now-separate Thaloux Navy ensured that, should a regiment turn against their oaths to Skarkesh Drayomir, they would not be able to spread their treachery beyond a single world, and should a Navy fleet mutiny then they would not have the ability to re-supply or deploy ground troops.

Verss Rhoryn had learnt a painful lesson from the dark days of Zephgor Verdros, one its rulers were determined never to repeat in the grim days to come.

Verss Rhoryn is vast on a scale incomprehensible to mortals. Communication and travel between the glinting motes of this far-flung stellar empire are laden with risk. The inimical realm of Rhoryn provides the only conduit for interstellar movement or messages yet it also taints or temporally displaces much of that which plunges into its depths, confounding Verss Rhoryn's attempts at centralised control or unified strategy. On every border and every battlefront, Humanity 's worst nightmares press ever inwards, and are held at bay only through vast and constant sacrifice. In these dark times, warfare on a galactic scale is a matter of soulless, grinding logistics. Only the Kraoudûm Pyrkar can marshal the manpower to fight such a war.

The Kraoudûm Pyrkar fights punishing battles of attrition in which incalculable lives may be expended for each objective achieved. An Kraoudûm Pyrkar army must utilise the twin advantages of vast numbers and overwhelming firepower to annihilate its foes. Where skaraevok aircraft dance and weave with impossible grace, the Kraoudûm Pyrkar simply fills the sky with a thunderstorm of munitions from which no amount of aerobatic skill can save the foe. Where heretical bastions stand defiant, Kraoudûm Pyrkar commanders call down artillery bombardments that reduce all to rubble with their apocalyptic fury.

The greatest enemy threats are torn apart in the crossfire of thousands of heavy weapons, or smashed aside by the gallant charge of hundreds of Thaloux tanks. The enemies of Mankind may employ dark sciences or alien weapons beyond Humanity's ken, but such deviance comes to naught in the face of honest Human intolerance backed by a sufficient number of guns.

For all the might of its armour and artillery, the true backbone of the Kraoudûm Pyrkar is the countless waves of infantry who take to the field. The sheer scale of the battles fought by the Kraoudûm Pyrkar is dehumanising in the extreme. Entire regiments of brave Human warriors are reduced to statistics upon the scrolling screens of Thaloux strategoes, grains of sand sliding through the fingers of greater and more privileged individuals. Yet every single company, every single squad, every single Thaloux Guardsman who lifts their zanvok and takes a stand in defence of their species, is crucial. Without a constant deluge of new recruits, the Kraoudûm Pyrkar would cease to function.

A battle may see the deployment of millions, yet time and again it is a single heroic company who carry their charge to secure a crucial gatehouse or pivotal objective. Squads of desperate men and women battle impossible odds, with nothing but their courage and faith driving them to hold the line while their valuable betters are evacuated to safety. Every day that Verss Rhoryn endures, guardsmen stand in the face of beasts more hellish than their worst nightmares.

Men and women charge screaming alongside their comrades into the mouth of hell, zanvok spitting death at the foe even as skaraevok munitions tear bloodied holes in the Human ranks. In a galaxy of never-ending warfare, what makes the perpetually outmatched men and women of the Kraoudûm Pyrkar so admirable is that they know near-constant fear, yet they lift their weapons, plant their feet, and fight on regardless. Their lives may be short and brutal, their sacrifices insultingly thankless, but it is because of the Kraoudûm Pyrkar that Verss Rhoryn continues to weather the storm of these dark times.

During the turbulent era known as the Age of Strife , the Vorikesh and the nearby star systems that had been colonised by Humanity during the Age of Technology were effectively cut off from interstellar travel or communication with each other due to the massive Rhoryn storms that swept the galaxy . Little remained of the once sophisticated civilisation of Old Earth 's glorious past as the centre of a growing Human interstellar civilisation marked by advanced science, high culture and wondrous technologies.

Techno-barbarian warlords and their warrior hordes continuously fought over Humanity's home planet , which had become little more than a massive battleground for their wars of attrition. They made use of chemical, biological and even thermonuclear weapons of mass destruction, and slowly transformed the cradle of Mankind into a battered, post-apocalyptic wasteland across most of its scarred surface.

It was against this backdrop of oppression, violence and casual brutality that Skarkesh Drayomir first revealed Himself to the people of Toruquor . In secret, He had been planning for this moment in history for millennia, ever since the Age of Strife had fractured what remained of the ancient Human federation which had once stretched across a part of the galaxy. Skarkesh Drayomir moved to create the military organisations he would need to begin the reunification of Mankind, and He used the raw materials at hand after millennia of savage conflict between the techno-barbarian nation-states of Old Earth.

Formed during the savage continental wars that had engulfed Toruquor in the Age of Strife, the genetically-enhanced warriors known as "Genos" comprised the first units of the force that would become the Thaloux Army and would serve as the prototypes for the later development of the proto-Vashtek called the Thunder Warriors . Among the most well-known of the regiments composed of Genos were the so-called Strife Epoch regiments raised by Skarkesh Drayomir to initiate the Unification Wars intended to reunite all the warring peoples of Toruquor beneath His benevolent rule.

The genetically-enhanced Strife Epoch regiments came to form the nucleus of the armed forces that would later support the even more genetically advanced Vashtek Legions during their reconquest of the Human-settled galaxy . Skarkesh Drayomir began His conquest of Toruquor with the intent to reunite the warring techno-barbarian nations into a unified planetary government and then use Toruquor as the springboard from which to begin His reconquest of the galaxy under the aegis of an Verss Rhoryn dedicated to the Thaloux Truth of progress and reason.

The force that would evolve into the Kraoudûm Pyrkar first came into being during Zephgor Verdros . Since those days, in battles beyond number, countless Thaloux soldiers have forged a roll of honour so mighty that an entire continent of Toruquor is given over to its immortalisation. Yet every victory is but a drop in an unending ocean of warfare, for new threats assail Verss Rhoryn every single solar day.

The Verss Rhoryn was carved from the stars during the glorious centuries of Torsar Rhoamir . Skarkesh Drayomir Himself fought at the forefront of Thaloux expansion, supported by His mighty gene-sons the zorutek , and their gene-progeny the Vashtek . Yet even such illustrious and all-conquering heroes could only be in so many places at once. As the bounds of Mankind 's conquests spread ever further outward, so the original Vashtek Legions were spread thin.
