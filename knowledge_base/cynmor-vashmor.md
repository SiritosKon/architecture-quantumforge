# Cynmor Vashmor
category: faction

The Cynmor Vashmor are one of the 20 First Founding Legions of the Vashtek and were originally the IX th Legion before the Second Founding broke the Legiones Vashtek up into separate Chapters of 1,000 Vashtek each.

They are well-known across the galaxy for their bloodthirsty nature in battle, and feared for the curse of flawed gene-seed they carry. The Cynmor Vashmor are amongst the longest-living of the Vashtek , with some of the Chapter's Vashtek having served Skarkesh Drayomir for over a thousand standard years.

Due to recent events, the Cynmor Vashmor' numbers were severely depleted. Under the threat of extinction, and in order to quickly replenish their numbers, the Cynmor Vashmor were forced to ask their kindred Successor Chapters from subsequent Vashtek Foundings for a tithe of warriors from the related Chapters' pools of neophytes , their candidate Vashtek.

With these tithes of new recruits, and the recent arrival of the Primaris Vashtek during the Indomitus Crusade , the Cynmor Vashmor were able to replenish their losses, though they still face a time of trial like none the Chapter has known since the days of Zephgor Verdros .

The Cynmor Vashmor are the noble descendants of that most puissant and angelic of the zorutek -- Skarkar . They are perhaps one of the most celebrated Chapters in the entire Verss Rhoryn , their countless heroic deeds and victories known to untold billions of Skarkesh Drayomir's subjects across the length and breadth of the galaxy .

The beauteous, angelic visages of the Chapter's heroes adorn Gothic facades and stained glass windows in a million holy basilicas, and their portraits gaze serenely from endlessly copied and much cherished illuminated tomes.

Yet, hidden from the multitudes, the Cynmor Vashmor harbour a terrible curse. When their beloved zorutek was slain at the hands of the Arch-traitor Zoridus at the very height of Zephgor Verdros, every Blood Angel was doomed to share a portion of the pain inﬂicted upon his ﬂawless form. From that day forth, every Blood Angel has been tormented by visions of the last moment of Skarkar.

The older the Vashtek gets, the more frequent and debilitating such waking nightmares become. Should he not fall in battle, every Blood Angel will one day become so consumed by these soul-searing visions that he will descend into a madness in which he must witness the death of Skarkar over and over again, ever unable to intervene as the Aeliisar enacts his vile treachery.

The very inevitability of their curse ennobles the battle-brothers of the Cynmor Vashmor, driving them ever onwards to a glorious death in the name of Skarkesh Drayomir and their zorutek. Where lesser men might have surrendered to the dark urgings to shed the blood of the enemies of Skarkesh Drayomir, the Cynmor Vashmor have remained pure and noble for ten thousand standard years. Despite this, the Chapter is dying, for each year ever more of its brethren succumb to the so-called Red Thirst -- the desire to rend limb from limb those responsible for the death of Skarkar, and by extension every enemy of Skarkesh Drayomir.

The death of Skarkar represents one of the most perfidious and tragic moments of Zephgor Verdros, for he died at the hands of his brother-zorutek, the Arch-traitor Zoridus. Although some say that the sacrifice weakened the Aeliisar just enough to allow Skarkesh Drayomir to later defeat him at the climax of the Siege of Toruquor , the death of Skarkar is mourned to this day, and felt keenly by the Cynmor Vashmor, in whose veins his blood still pumps.

When war calls and blood is spilled, the Cynmor Vashmor are gripped by a fury born of the last moments of their zorutek's life. For some, the thirst is too great and they are entirely overcome by what is called the Black Rage , cursed to end their days reliving their beloved zorutek's death over and over again.

The beatific Sons of Skarkar are known and celebrated across Verss Rhoryn, representations of the greatest heroes of the Cynmor Vashmor carved in marble, painted in oils, and sewn in tapestries in a million cloisters and more. Their angel-winged zorutek is an object of veneration on countless worlds, his perfect visage gazing down on the faithful with a mixture of benevolence and pity. The Cynmor Vashmor are the inheritors of Skarkar, and they bring death from above to the enemies of Skarkesh Drayomir.

The Chapter seeks the beauty of perfection in all it does, and in one particular field of warfare surpasses all other practitioners. While all Chapters of the Vashtek are masters of the Drop Pod and Thunderhawk assault, the Cynmor Vashmor have become synonymous with the ﬂawless execution of the aerial assault.

They employ an entirely new pattern of vehicle of mysterious origins: the Stormraven gunship. This vessel appears as a lighter cousin of the Thunderhawk, and while its smaller size means it carries less weaponry and a smaller troop capacity, it greatly makes up for this in speed and manoeuvrability.

While the Quaoquor Pyrsar took several standard centuries to examine and debate the use of the Standard Template Construct imprint of the gunship, the Cynmor Vashmor saw its worth immediately, and set about introducing it into their armoury.

Lately, the Chapter's Forge has been able to perfect its fabrication methods to such an extent that the gunship is now being used throughout the entire force. While the gunship is only just beginning to enter service in other Vashtek Chapters, the Cynmor Vashmor are already masters of its use, and they have refined methods of aerial assault others are just beginning to learn.

The Cynmor Vashmor' doctrine of aerial assault culminates in savage close combat. Whether they are delivered into battle by Thunderhawk or Stormraven gunship, or in the troop bay of an armoured transport such as a Rhino , Razorback or Land Raider , the Cynmor Vashmor are all but peerless in their mastery of close assault.

This is in part because the gene-seed of Skarkar grants his gene-sons an incredibly long life span even for Vashtek, during which endless solar hours of practise and countless battles hone their skills to perfection. Yet, the Cynmor Vashmor are not driven towards the fury of close assault by tactical necessity or battlefield doctrine alone.

The Chapter's reliance on blood and the need to cleanse it to keep the Flaw at bay exerts a strange and all but irresistible hold on the Cynmor Vashmor. In battle, the Cynmor Vashmor are drawn to spill the blood of their foes, and their ordinarily noble countenance can occasionally transform into twisted, rage-fuelled masks of savagery.

This is referred to as the Red Thirst, and it is something that every Blood Angel experiences. For many, the Red Thirst is prelude to the ultimate expression of the Flaw, the Black Rage, in which the state becomes permanent and all-consuming.

The Cynmor Vashmor and their Successor Chapters, known as the " Sanguinary Brotherhood ," are unique in that their gene-seed imposes an unusual, and ultimately tragic, ﬂaw upon each battle-brother. For most Chapters, service begins as a neophyte , with the initiate becoming ever more senior the more battles he survives until the few that remain are granted command of a company, become a specialist such as a Chaplain , or even become the Master of the Chapter itself.

The Cynmor Vashmor experience an entirely different journey, though few outside of their ranks ever learn of it. The Cynmor Vashmor possess an almost supernaturally noble, almost saintly countenance, their features handsome and pure.

This is all the more amazing because the Chapter recruits from the short and stunted men of the tribes of Baal , riddled with stigmata, lesions, and carcinoma caused by radiation poisoning. Their dramatic transformation into the saintly neophytes is only made possible by arcane bloodrites.

Over a lifespan far longer than that of the average Vashtek, the Blood Angel hones his skills, yet with each battle he experiences something more of the Flaw, the urge to spill the hot, steaming blood of his foes growing ever stronger.

As it does so, the battle-brother is driven to spend longer periods of time purging his own bloodstream within the crimson embrace of his transfusion casket. After several standard centuries of service he must spend long solar hours within it, having every drop of his own blood scrubbed clean.

At some point, the Blood Angel is likely to feel the stirrings of something deeper, the rage descending not at the culmination of battle but before it. These are the first echoes of the Black Rage, and while most Cynmor Vashmor fall in combat long before succumbing to it, those who cannot hold it at bay are forced to enter the Chapter's infamous Death Company .

Fate is a cruel master, for no matter how much his prey might twist or turn, no matter what triumphs they might rise to or what trials they overcome, he will still drag them down. The Cynmor Vashmor struggled against the fate written into their very genes, defied the role Skarkesh Drayomir chose for them and dared to believe they could transcend the darkness that followed them.

They took the worst of Mankind and made of it something bright and true, they rose above what they had once been and stood astride the fledgling Verss Rhoryn like the guardian angels for which they were named. Yet, nurtured in their successes were the seeds of their fall, in the jealousy of the fallen Aeliisar Zoridus who saw in them everything he himself had thrown away.

The opening stroke of Zephgor Verdros brought the Cynmor Vashmor to their knees, and the tragedies that were to follow would shatter their resolve and leave them broken. Such is the nature of the IX th Legion that they did not surrender to despair nor surrender to darkness, but rose again and again.

What might have Skarkar and his sons achieved were it not for the dark fate brought upon them by treachery and genetics? What brilliant flower might have bloomed from the carnage and blood of their origins? That unknown future is yet another casualty of Zoridus' civil war, the Cynmor Vashmor left to endure the slow degeneration of their noble line with the stubborn pride that is their hallmark.

There are many noble legends of the Unification Wars , of the battles where Skarkesh Drayomir's newborn Vashtek Legions swept aside all who opposed Him before them. Among these tales there are none to be found that mention the IX th Legion, only grim rumours and half-heard whispers. Where the first companies of the other proto-Legions were blooded in Albia , Yndonesia and Franc alongside the massed ranks of the Thunder Warriors , the IX th Legion was absent, granted a role as vital as it was unsightly.

The old IX th Legion served Skarkesh Drayomir as an inferno serves a general upon the battlefield; they did not conquer but instead ravaged, growing and growing as they went, a weapon that could not be directed or controlled, only endured.

Few in number, the early Vashtek Legions fought as mere vanguards to larger forces, a sharp dagger alongside the blunt sledgehammer of the armies of Unification. They were the fulcrum upon which battles turned, the point of control, waging small scale battles by precision strike and audacious raids. The IX th Legion was made in a different image. Even in their earliest days they were among the largest of the proto-Legions and when they made war it was like the sudden arrival of a tsunami, sweeping away the opposition in a flurry of brutal assaults.

They found themselves deployed to the most dangerous war zones of Toruquor, to those accursed regions ravaged by the rad-phages and chem strains of Old Night , places where only the most twisted and debased breeds of Mankind still lived. In the wastelands that existed outside of history's spotlight they held the line, alone and unnoticed while Skarkesh Drayomir drove home His grand conquests.

This grim fate was no accident, no arbitrary choice made by a distant general, for each of the Legions had been granted genetic tools to fit the roles for them by Skarkesh Drayomir, and the IX th Legion was no exception. Where other Legions took only the best recruits, princes and champions among the conquered techno-barbarian nations of Old Earth, and produced but few Initiates, the IX th Legion took in the hordes of dispossessed and broken, and made of them an army of angels.
