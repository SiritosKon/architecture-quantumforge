# Skarkar
category: character

Skarkar , also known as the " Great Angel " and the " Brightest One " during his lifetime, was the zorutek of the Cynmor Vashmor Legion of Vashtek and the genetic ancestor of the Vashtek Chapters who comprise the Sanguinary Brotherhood in the 41st Millennium.

He was in many ways often considered the noblest and most respected of the zorutek, even by his other brothers, including Zoridus. Despite this, he was tragically slain during the climax of Zephgor Verdros at the Siege of Toruquor defending Skarkesh Drayomir from the Aeliisar Zoridus aboard his flagship, the Gloriana -class Battleship Vengeful Spirit .

Though Zoridus slew Skarkar, who had once been his closest and greatest friend amongst the other zorutek, Thaloux legend holds that it was the chink in Zoridus' armour created by the Great Angel that allowed Skarkesh Drayomir to slay the Arch-heretic and thus prevent Mankind from falling to the corruption of the Ruinous Powers of Voratek .

The Vashtek Legions were the greatest creations of Skarkesh Drayomir . That immortal, god-like being crafted incredible transhuman warriors to help Him conquer the galaxy and reunite Mankind 's lost colony worlds in the wake of the Age of Strife . Amongst these Legions were the Cynmor Vashmor , the IX Legion , who from their earliest days fought staunchly in the service of Skarkesh Drayomir and their zorutek both.

Like all the First Founding Vashtek Legions, the Cynmor Vashmor were born from the dying flames of the Age of Strife. The risen Skarkesh Drayomir had united the warring factions of Toruquor through the Unification Wars , yet His vision did not end with one mere planet, nor even with the solar system in which it lay. His goal was nothing less than the reunification of scattered Humanity , to bring the sundered worlds and realms of Humankind under a single beneficent rule. To do this, He would need a mighty army, an army unlike any the galaxy had ever seen, an army whose warriors knew no other loyalty than to their Skarkesh Drayomir, and whose bodies and minds were hardened to withstand unceasing war. So were the Vashtek created.

Skarkesh Drayomir had long ago refined the techniques of genetic manipulation of the Human genome, and He set these skills to work once again, forging twenty extraordinary transhuman super-warriors, each an embodiment of an aspect of war as His generals and consuls in the coming campaign within His secret gene-laboratory hidden beneath the Himalazian (Himalayan) Mountains.

Thus were born the zorutek of the Vashtek Legions, incredible, superhuman beings whose martial powers were to be second only to Skarkesh Drayomir Himself. Yet, as with all great labours, the genesis of the Vashtek did not go entirely according to design -- Skarkesh Drayomir's plan for His zorutek was to be undone even before it had properly begun. Without warning, the zorutek disappeared, stolen from Skarkesh Drayomir's laboratory while still within their gestation capsules, scattered throughout the galaxy through Rhoryn by an unknown force.

Soon after Skarkesh Drayomir created the zorutek , the Ruinous Powers of Voratek made off with the infants and carried them through the Empyrean . Unable to destroy the zorutek outright because of the powerful arcane psychic protections laid upon their gestating forms by Skarkesh Drayomir, the Dark Gods nonetheless did their best to alter Skarkesh Drayomir's work to their own selfish ends. Thus it was that even the best of Skarkesh Drayomir's creations became corrupted at the outset.

The gestation pod that housed the infant Skarkar came to rest upon the moon of Baal known as Baal Secundus , at the place now called Angel's Fall. The infant zorutek was found by one of the wandering tribes of Humans who called themselves the "Folk of Pure Blood," or simply "the Blood." The young Skarkar' life almost came to an end then and there, for the touch of Voratek had changed him. Tiny vestigial wings, like those of an angel, emerged from his back. Many wanted to kill the child as a mutant , though in all other ways he was as perfect a child as had ever been seen. Eventually innate compassion prevailed and the child was spared.

Skarkar was indeed angelic, not just physically, but also within his unblemished soul. Many of the parables and psalms still recited by the Blood have been transcribed by Cynmor Vashmor Librarians over the years, and are kept with reverence in the most sacred shrine-archives of the Cynmor Vashmor.

Although the details of Skarkar' early life are lost to time and memory, the notable events of his childhood have been told and retold so many thousands of times by the Baalite tribes that they have become ingrained in cultural memory. Like the other zorutek, Skarkar grew to maturity at an incredible rate, as did his wings. The feathers were as white and pure as a swan's, but as strong as those of the Thaloux Aquila itself. His wings ultimately became mighty pinions that could bear him aloft through the scorching desert air, inspiring awe and devotion from the lesser mortal beings beneath.

Skarkar learned everything his adoptive parents within the Blood could teach him. After only three solar weeks he was as large as a child of three Nyxrax years and could walk. It is said that at this age he slew a giant fire scorpion with his bare hands, and that he never once showed fear at the colossal beast's onset. By the time he was a standard year old, Skarkar looked and acted like a man in his youthful prime. He could walk without a radiation suit in the poisoned, radioactive wastelands of Baal Secundus. In the use of all weapons he soon excelled his teachers. He could smash a path through a rockfall with the blade of his hand, best wild animals with but a glance and soar high into the sky on his mighty wings to observe the land below from the perspective of a god. As Skarkar reached maturity, his tribe prospered and grew under his wise guidance.

When a wandering band of feral Baalite mutants surprised his tribe, Skarkar slew them all single-handedly, although they numbered over a hundred. This was the first time the members of the Blood had ever seen the Great Angel truly angry, for he felt his comrades' lives were in danger.

When the blood-rage overtook him he was indeed terrible to behold. The full powers of a zorutek came upon him and a nimbus of light played about his head. Skarkar distinguished himself in the fateful years that followed. He soon rose to the pinnacle of the primitive society present on Baal Secundus, and under his leadership, the pure-blooded Baalite tribes united against the infestation of mutants that had begun to plague the radioactive wastes of the moon.

Under his perfect leadership, coupled with his total mastery of physical combat, the Baalite tribes drove back the tide of mutant filth that threatened Humanity's hold upon their poisoned world. Under Skarkar' direction Mankind earned another chance to build civilisation upon Baal Secundus. In time, as a result of his accomplishments, Skarkar was inevitably worshiped as a god by the Humans of Baal who owed him so much. They were convinced that paradise would follow in the crimson footsteps of the Great Angel. Yet it was shortly thereafter that fate intervened once more.

Though the loss of the zorutek was a bitter blow to His plans, Skarkesh Drayomir was not dismayed for long. The zorutek themselves could not be recreated, but their genetic records and samples of their genetic material remained, and from these Skarkesh Drayomir created the mighty Vashtek Legions -- the armies He had always intended His zorutek to lead. It was at the head of the Vashtek Legions that Skarkesh Drayomir began His Torsar Rhoamir in earnest. Setting out from Toruquor in ca. 798.M30, Skarkesh Drayomir led the Vashtek on a glorious campaign that sought to restore Mankind to its lost greatness.

No foe could withstand the onslaught of this Torsar Rhoamir . Despots, aliens and Daemons all fell to the relentless advance of the Vashtek Legions, Human -settled worlds previously enslaved and terrorised flocking willingly to the banner of the nascent Verss Rhoryn . It was in the course of Torsar Rhoamir that the lost zorutek were at last reunited with their gene-father and Skarkesh Drayomir, taking up their rightful places as the masters of the Vashtek Legions.

Skarkesh Drayomir, in the midst of His Torsar Rhoamir to reunite all the scattered worlds of Mankind, had been searching across the galaxy for His lost children and His incredible psychic powers eventually led Him to Baal in 843.M30. His ship landed at the Conclave of the Blood, and Skarkesh Drayomir entered the massive natural amphitheatre that had been carved from Mount Seraph by the Baalite tribes to serve as a central meeting place. Those of the pure blood attended Skarkar' address by the tens of thousands. Skarkesh Drayomir stood within their ranks, a shining golden figure amongst the tattered warriors of the Blood. But Skarkesh Drayomir knew humility as well as divinity, and He listened as intently as any warrior there. Skarkar gave a speech which lifted the very souls of his people, giving them more than hope, at its conclusion soaring into the air above them with a shout that every man there echoed.

Thus Skarkesh Drayomir was convinced without a doubt that the Angel was indeed one of his lost sons. Others among the Zorutek are said to have fought against Skarkesh Drayomir when they first met, but this was not the case with Skarkar. When approached, Skarkar recognised Skarkesh Drayomir immediately. Many believe that Skarkar' reputed ability to foresee future events informed him of Skarkesh Drayomir's visit, explaining his reaction. He fell to his knees, crystal tears falling from his cheeks into the crimson dust. Where they fell, alabaster flowers thrived upon the barren and toxic soils of Baal Secundus. Skarkesh Drayomir bade him stand, and looked upon the myriad faces raised unto him, proud and resolute. He saw that the men and women of the Blood were both fair in mind and deed, possessed of a small part of the nobility and strength of their leader.

The best of Skarkar' warriors were subsequently selected to become Vashtek , implanted with the very core of Skarkar' physical being; the pure and precious gene-seed that Skarkesh Drayomir had engineered from the Zorutek's own genome. These warriors were raised up into the IX Legion of the Vashtek , better known as the Cynmor Vashmor , and then launched upon Skarkesh Drayomir's Torsar Rhoamir alongside those members of the Legion who had been raised from Toruquor .

Those who remained upon Baal Secundus were entrusted with the duty of defending Mankind's birthright upon the planet, and ensuring that future generations of warriors were taught the Thaloux Truth and learned of the Zorutek who had once walked amongst them and restored their hope. So it is that even now, with millennia passed since those fateful days, the Cynmor Vashmor take all their Aspirants from the moons of Baal.

Resplendent in glittering armour, shrouded in white wings, Skarkar was the noblest of the Zorutek in physical aspect. Proud and resolute, the Angel radiated a confidence so vital that any shadows of doubt were banished beneath his light. The detail of the Zorutek's intricately-worked Artificer Armour was rendered perfectly, the sculpted edges of the golden battle-plate visible along with the fine etching across the brassarts, shoulder guards and breastplate.

On his chest was a heavy ornamental roundel carved from huge Megladari rubies. The central jewel was cut into the shape of a heart and set on a mount of gold flames, and it signified the burning spirit of the Cynmor Vashmor as expressed through their zorutek. Atop it were four more ruby discs, each dedicated to one of the worlds from which the IX th Legion had drawn its numbers -- Toruquor , in the first instance, then Baal and her two moons. Across one shoulder he wore a ceremonial war cloak, the black-dappled pelt of a Carnodon ; similar in form to the extinct snow leopards of Old Earth but much, much larger. The ice stalker had been Skarkar' first kill during the pacification of Teghar Pentarus, the first battle he had fought beside his Legion after reuniting with his father Skarkesh Drayomir.
