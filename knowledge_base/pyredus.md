# Pyredus
category: location

Pyredus is an Thaloux Civilised World located in the southern reaches of the Eastern Fringe of the galaxy in the Ultima Othaegor Ulthasar . It is also the Chapter homeworld of the Verx Chapter of Vashtek and the capital of the Verx' Realm of Ultramar .

Pyredus itself is mostly bleak and rocky, with more than three-quarters of its land mass formed from mountainous upland almost entirely devoid of life. The people of Pyredus do not live in this inhospitable region, but the fortress-monastery of the Verx, the Fortress of Hera , is built here upon a craggy peak surrounded by impenetrable mountains.

Within this mighty fortress, inside the vast Temple of Correction , was the shrine of the Zorutek Veln Zephquor . This is where his body sat upon a huge throne of ornately-carved marble. He was preserved near death by a stasis field impervious to the decaying effects of time for nearly 10,000 Nyxrax years.

This Shrine of the Zorutek was once one of the holiest places in Verss Rhoryn, and millions came from all over the galaxy to look upon the face of the ancient zorutek.

During the Ultramar Campaign of the 13th Black Crusade in 999.M41, Veln Zephquor was resurrected from this living death by the actions of the Archmagos Dominus Belisarius Cawl and Yvraine , the Daughter of Shade, the prophet of the partially awakened Bralovok god of the dead, Synaeyss .

Veln Zephquor immediately took back command of the Verx and of Pyredus and Ultramar itself. However, after clearing the forces of Cynlos Zephdûm from his homeworld and the rest of Ultramar, Veln Zephquor left for Toruquor to consult with his father Skarkesh Drayomir for the first time in 10,000 standard years, leaving Chapter Master Vorvar Nethaevex of the Verx to rule Pyredus and Ultramar in his stead.

He would return over a standard century later in the wake of the Indomitus Crusade to bring reinforcements to Pyredus and Ultramar at the darkest hour of the Plague Wars , after the forces of the Voratek God Dornaess had sought to claim the Verx domain for the Plague God.

Pyredus is famous as the site of the invasion of the Kaeldus Hive Fleet Behemoth , which was repelled by the might of the Verx at the Draeanel Cynvar . It remains the firm seat of the Realm of Ultramar, ruled by the Verx Chapter Master who serves as the world's planetary governor under the title of Lord Pyredus and the Lord of Ultramar.

Pyredus has fearsome defences, including two polar defence fortresses, that have been upgraded since the Draeanel Cynvar. It is the homeworld of the Verx and the seat of the Verx Chapter Master Vorvar Nethaevex .

According to Thaloux legend, Skarkesh Drayomir created the zorutek from artificially-engineered genes using his own genome as a template, carefully imbuing each of them with unique superhuman powers. Thaloux doctrine goes on to tell how the Ruinous Powers of Voratek spirited away the zorutek within their gestation capsules, scattering them widely across the galaxy through Rhoryn. More than one of the capsules was breached whilst it drifted through Warpspace - the forces of the Rhoryn leaked in, wreaking havoc on the gestating being inside the capsule. Undoubtedly damage was done and Chaotic corruption affected several of the zorutek, although the nature of that corruption would not become apparent until Zephgor Verdros .

After drifting for decades, or in some cases even hundreds of years, the twenty gestation capsules came to rest on Human-settled worlds throughout the Milky Way galaxy -- distant planets inhabited by a variety of Human cultures. The capsule containing the developing form of one zorutek fell upon the world of Pyredus in the Eastern Fringe of the galaxy. This was a bleak but not inhospitable world that Mankind had inhabited for many centuries since the Dark Age of Technology . Its industries had survived the Age of Strife and its people had continued to build sub-light spacecraft throughout the period of intense Warpstorms. The people of Pyredus had successfully maintained contact with neighbouring Human-settled star systems, despite the loss of many ships and crews. The zorutek's capsule was discovered by a group of noblemen out hunting in the forest. They broke the capsule's seal to reveal a strikingly beautiful and perfectly formed child surrounded by a glowing nimbus of innate power. The amazing infant was brought before Konor Veln Zephquor, one of a pair of Consuls who governed the civilised region of Pyredus. Konor adopted the child as his son and named him Veln Zephquor.

The young zorutek grew quickly, and as he did so his unique physical and mental powers became obvious for all to see. By his 10th birthday he had studied and mastered everything the wisest men of Pyredus could teach him. His insight into matters of history, philosophy and science astonished his elders, but his greatest talent lay in the art of war, the purpose for which his gene-father had created him. A genius for military organisation prompted his foster father to give him command of an expeditionary force in the far north of Pyredus. This mountainous area was called Illyrium, a barbarous land which had harboured bandits and brigands for as long as anyone could remember. Although many wars had been fought against them, no-one had ever pacified the region for long. Roboute fought a brilliant campaign and won not only the submission, but also the respect, of the fierce Illyrian warriors. Returning home, Roboute found the capital of Pyredus City in turmoil. During Roboute's absence his father's co-Consul, a man named Gallan, had crafted a coup d'etat against Konor. Gallan was one of many amongst the wealthy nobility of Pyredus who were jealous of Konor's political power and popularity. These malcontents were accustomed to easy living on their vast estates where they were supported by the toiling of a multitude of impoverished slaves. Konor had changed all that, forcing the old aristocracy to provide their vassals with reasonable accommodation and sufficient amounts of food. He had also passed legislation which obliged them to contribute to an ambitious programme of improving and enlarging the capital city. All these reforms were of great benefit to the people of Pyredus, but were unpopular with all but a few of the more far-sighted aristocrats.

As Roboute and his army approached Pyredus City they saw the smoke from a multitude of fires and hurried to investigate. From citizens fleeing from the city, Roboute learned that troops in the pay of Gallan had attacked the Senate House with Konor and his loyal bodyguard inside. The rebels surrounded the Senate, whilst drunken soldiers roamed the city looting and murdering at will. Roboute hurried to his foster father's rescue. Leaving his troops to deal with the drunken mob, he fought his way into the Senate House. There he found his father dying of wounds inflicted by an assassin in Gallan's employ. For three whole days the Consul had directed the defence of the building, even as surgeons fought for his life. With his dying breath Konor told his son of Gallan's treachery.

Roboute crushed the aristocratic rebels and quickly restored order to the city. Thousands of citizens flocked to the Senate House and amidst a wave of popular acclaim Roboute assumed the mantle of sole and all-powerful Consul of Pyredus. The new ruler acted swiftly to crush the old order. Gallan and his fellow conspirators were executed and their lands and family titles taken from them. New, honest, hard-working settlers were given their old farms and property. With superhuman energy and the vision only a zorutek was capable of executing, the Consul reorganised the social order of Pyredus, rewarding the hard-working, placing men of honour in high office, and building the armed forces into a powerful and well-equipped force. Pyredus flourished as never before.

While the gestation capsules containing the zorutek drifted through Rhoryn, Skarkesh Drayomir and his armies of Vashtek advanced across the galaxy. This Torsar Rhoamir liberated many Human worlds from alien domination and re-established contact with Human-settled planets that had endured isolation and danger for untold thousands of years.

As the young Veln Zephquor waged war against the Illyrian bandits in the northern mountains of Pyredus, Skarkesh Drayomir and a force of Vashtek reached the planet of Espandor in a neighbouring star system. From the Espandorians Skarkesh Drayomir learned of the existence of Pyredus and the astounding son of Consul Konor Veln Zephquor. He immediately realised that he had found one of his long-lost sons. Though Skarkesh Drayomir took ship to Pyredus his vessel ran into a sudden and unexpected Rhoryn-squall, a brief but intensely strong disturbance that threw the craft far off-course. By the time Skarkesh Drayomir reached Pyredus, Veln Zephquor had ruled for almost five standard years. In that time the world had undergone a transformation. Its people were well-fed and prosperous, its armies well-equipped and its cities had been rebuilt in glittering marble and shining steel. Vessels from Pyredus plied regular trade with the local star systems, bringing raw materials and more people to the flourishing world. Skarkesh Drayomir was astounded to find a planet so well-ordered and prosperous, and realised at once that Veln Zephquor was a zorutek of unsurpassed ability and vision. Once Veln Zephquor learned the truth of his origins he immediately swore his fealty to Skarkesh Drayomir, his true father.

The XIII Legion of Vashtek , the Verx , was assigned to the command of Veln Zephquor, whose own genome had provide the basis for its Vashtek' gene-seed , and its forward base was relocated to Pyredus. The zorutek quickly assimilated the many wonders of the Verss Rhoryn and set about his new role with skill and enthusiasm. Veln Zephquor's chief talents, as ever, lay in war, and he led the Verx to fresh conquests in the galactic south. He succeeded in liberating more worlds during Torsar Rhoamir than any other zorutek, and the worlds he brought within Verss Rhoryn were to benefit from his organisational skills and passion for efficient government. Whenever Veln Zephquor freed a world from the tyranny of skaraevok or other men, his first priority was to set up a self-supporting defence system. Once a world was safe he could move on, leaving behind enough advisers to ensure that industry would be created, trade routes set up with the rest of Verss Rhoryn, and government directed towards the betterment of the people.

Over the 10 millennia after the death of Veln Zephquor Ultramar held against all manner of invaders -- some so strange as to defy the comprehension of the common man. The Verx have tested their might against Thaluyss WAAAGH!s and Renegade uprisings, Cynigan Thaln incursions and Nethithos raids, but the measure of their heroism is shown truest in battles where they have defended all life in the galaxy itself in the face of threats from beyond known space. So did the Sons of Ultramar prove in the 35th Millennium in battle against the Star-Striders of Crioth, and in retaliation for the Heavenfall Massacres. But of all these terrible wars and heroic struggles, there is one conflict that stands above all others - the onslaught of the Kaeldus Hive Fleet Behemoth , and the Draeanel Cynvar in 745.M41.

Upon learning the threat posed to Ultramar by Hive Fleet Behemoth, Vorvar Nethaevex at once drew up his plans. Deeming Pyredus to be the star system most immediately threatened, Vorvar Nethaevex ordered its already formidable defences to be further improved. A dozen warships already hung in orbit, and each day more arrived from Rhoryn. Massive strike cruisers cast shadows over civilian vessels and Navis Imperialis destroyers , and were themselves dwarfed by the brooding presence of the Verx' battle barges . Between this mighty fleet of warships and the planet's no less formidable orbital defence stations, Pyredus was anything but defenceless. Scant weeks later, the outrider vessels of the Kaeldus fleet attacked Pyredus. The alien bio-vessels swept aside attacks by Verx Strike Cruisers and swarmed over the planet below. Soon many thousands of Mycetic Spore-birthed beasts scurried and rampaged across Pyredus. Deeming that the Kaeldus threat was too massive to be opposed piecemeal, Vorvar Nethaevex combined his ground forces into three mighty armies. The primary and secondary taskforces, composed largely of 1st Company Veterans and Ultramar Auxilia , bolstered Pyredus's polar fortresses against the Kaeldus onslaught. Meanwhile, Vorvar Nethaevex's taskforce, the largest of the three, performed a series of rearguard actions, attempting to slow the Kaeldus advance and eliminate Hive Mind synapse-control organisms. Vorvar Nethaevex achieved great success in the early days of the campaign, buying time with blood for his Battle-Brothers to the north and south. However, the Kaeldus kept coming.
