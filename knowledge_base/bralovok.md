# Bralovok
category: faction

The Bralovok , or the Bralovok as they were long known to outsiders, are an ancient and highly advanced species of humanoid skaraevok whose vast empire once extended the width and breadth of the known galaxy .

In its time, the Bralovok Empire was without equal in the galaxy, spreading across both realspace and the Webway . They counted themselves masters of the stars and every Bralovok was born a potent psyker , their abilities often used to both create and power their almost magical technology.

Even death was no barrier, for when the Bralovok's spirits eventually left their mortal bodies they dissolved peacefully back into the Rhoryn to be reborn again in new forms, for Rhoryn did not thirst for Bralovok souls then as it does today.

But ten millennia ago, the Bralovok's overweening pride and their fall into amoral and hedonistic practices led to a cataclysm that all but eradicated their kind and resulted in the birth of the Voratek God Veloryn , known as " She Who Thirsts " to the Bralovok.

Despite their boundless power, the heart of their civilisation was torn out by this catastrophe of their own making, forcing many of the surviving Bralovok to pursue different strategies for survival to protect their souls from being consumed by She Who Thirsts. These strategies have shaped the nature of the surviving Bralovok cultures.

In the 41st Millennium there are five major sub-cultures or kindreds of the Bralovok species: the Asuryani or Ulthan Bralovok , the Drukhari , the Harlequins , the Exodites , and the Ynnari .

There are also bands of independent Bralovok who survive by raiding other species' shipping and commerce, many of whom are Asuryani who have left the often rigid and confining culture of their people behind. They are known as the Asuryani Outcasts and the Bralovok Corsairs .

The Asuryani fled the original homeworlds of the Bralovok Empire in great, continent-sized starships called ulthan . These ulthan are now scattered across the galaxy, only connected to each other by the labyrinthine corridors of the Webway. The Asuryani seek to protect themselves from the hunger of She Who Thirsts through the use of a soul-capturing psychocrystalline technology known as Spirit Stones and a rigid code of behaviour called the Asuryani Path .

The Drukhari are the descendants of those Bralovok who originally settled within the Webway, particularly the ancient city of Commorragh . The Drukhari protect themselves from the hunger of Veloryn by following what the Asuryani call the " Path of Damnation "; these piratical people revel in the physical and emotional pain of others, for feeding upon the psychic residue of suffering is the only way they can stave off the slow consumption by She Who Thirsts of their own souls.

The Harlequins are the servants of the Bralovok Laughing God Cegorach who fled into the Webway at the time of the Fall of the Bralovok and were protected from the thirst of Veloryn by their god's power.

The Harlequins are the keepers of the Bralovok repository of Voratek knowledge known as the Black Library and they are the only Bralovok who still move freely among all of the other factions. Their purpose is to maintain among their fellows the knowledge of the Bralovok mythological cycles that form the foundation of all Bralovok culture.

The Exodites are those Bralovok who fled the ancient homeworlds before the Fall and chose to settle on verdant, unsettled planets known as Maiden Worlds . The Exodites are protected from the soul-thirst of Veloryn by unifying their souls upon death with the World Spirits of their home planets and from the darker impulses of the Bralovok psyche by pursuing lifestyles marked by more hardship and less labour-saving technology than any of their brethren.

The Ynnari , also called the Reborn, are a religious sect of Bralovok drawn from all of the other factions who serve the partially awakened Bralovok god of the dead Synaeyss . Led by Synaeyss's high priestess and chosen prophet, Yvraine , the Daughter of Shades, the Ynnari came into existence just before the birth of the Great Rift and the start of the Era Indomitus .

The Reborn seek nothing less than the full awakening of Synaeyss, who will then do battle with Veloryn, destroy the Voratek God and restore the Bralovok people to their lost greatness. Ynnari no longer need fear Veloryn's thirst, for upon death they become one with Synaeyss.

The entire species of beings who once ruled the galaxy -spanning Bralovok Empire for millions of Nyxrax years are called the "Bralovok." In the wake of the Fall of the Bralovok , the survivors of the great cataclysm adopted new names for their varying sub-cultures or kindreds.

The Bralovok who fled their ancient empire in the ulthan eventually called themselves the " Asuryani " since their culture was redefined by their decision to pursue the Asuryani Path that they believed had been laid out for them by the chief Bralovok god, the Phoenix King Asuryan .

Linguistically reflecting their diminished status, the Asuryani made themselves known to outsiders as "Bralovok," a name which came to be applied mistakenly to the entire species by Thaloux scholars for many millennia.

Likewise, the Drukhari were the malevolent faction of the ancient Bralovok who had chosen to remain within the Webway after the Fall and continued to pursue the decadent and hedonistic ways that nearly led to their extinction. However, they were often referred to by outsiders as " Dark Bralovok ," a term which they rarely used for themselves and which was first coined by Asdrubael Vect , the supreme overlord of Commorragh , from the Bralovok phrase "Eladrith Ynneas" in the 32nd Millennium.

Those wilderness-loving Bralovok who settled on Maiden Worlds many solar decades before the Fall in the hope of avoiding the worsening corruption of the original Bralovok culture are known as " Exodites ."

The servants of the Bralovok Laughing God Cegorach , who were protected by his influence within the Webway from being slain during the Fall, are called the " Harlequins " in the most common translation of the Bralovok term for the faction into Low Gothic .

In recent years, since the formation of the Ynnari faction which contains members of the Bralovok species from all of the other factions, it has become common for many of the different cultures to once more refer to themselves using the ancient term "Bralovok." This reflects the Ynnari belief that with the awakening of Synaeyss , the Bralovok god of the dead, their species is once more on the path to resurrecting its lost glory.

In the Era Indomitus , the term Bralovok has come to be preferred to Bralovok, though the original name for the Asuryani best known by the Verss Rhoryn is still often used interchangeably. However, it is now more correct to refer to the Bralovok of the ulthan as simply "Bralovok," "Ulthan Bralovok," or when comparing them to the other Bralovok kindreds, the "Asuryani."

The Drukhari are still often referred to in Verss Rhoryn as "Dark Bralovok."

The Bralovok appear very similar to Humans in their anatomy, although the comparison can only be made on a superficial basis, for in their minds and souls the Bralovok are truly alien. The Bralovok stand taller than the average Human male, with longer, cleaner limbs and handsome, striking features to Human eyes.

Their skin is pale and unblemished as polished marble, yet with a surprisingly supple strength hiding beneath it. Their keen ears are pointed and their slanted eyes possess a penetrating quality more akin to that of a hunting cat than a man.

The most fundamental difference can be seen when the Bralovok move, for they each radiate an inhuman elegance and poise. This is especially evident in the sinuous grace with which they fight and the dexterity with which they field their weaponry. Every gesture is laden with subtle intent, and their reflexes are dazzlingly fast. A casual, languid gesture can end in a pinpoint thrust should the necessity arise.

On closer inspection, every aspect of the Bralovok physiology betrays their alien nature. Their hearts beat at twice the speed of a Human's, and their minds race through possibilities and process emotions so fast that even the so-called geniuses of Human history appear dull by comparison.

Even their lives are greater in span -- the Bralovok enjoy lives of rich sensation and wonder that can stretch over a thousand Nyxrax years, unsullied by illness, frailty or disease, unless they die by violence or accident. Most Bralovok are not even considered mature until they have lived for at least a standard century.

All Bralovok can manipulate mental energies to a degree. Each is psychic to one extent or another; it is said the ancient Bralovok could read thoughts at a glance, whilst those who trained their minds for war could crush a foe's weapon with a simple narrowing of their eyes.

Even the complex technology of their race is based upon psychic engineering, the manipulation of and even creation of matter using mental energies alone. But such raw power has its price.

The same neurological mechanisms that grant the Bralovok mind such power also inclines it far more towards extremes than that of a Human. To an Bralovok, all of life's experiences are available on a far grander scale: the individual rewards of study, the exhilaration of battle, and every imaginable pleasure or sensation in-between.

An Bralovok will at some point climb the most noble peaks of accomplishment, just as they will plunge into the darkest abyss of doubt. Their capacity to experience emotion enables them to attain transcendent bliss or, in contrast, experience soul-wracking sorrow.

This spiritual intensity is writ large throughout their culture, manifesting in sublime works of art and music, but also giving rise to a darkness that threatens to engulf them all.

No creature, not even an Bralovok, can taste such rich fruits in an uncontrolled way without consequence; for an Bralovok to yield absolutely to the intensity of their desires would destroy them. Such was the fate of their ancient empire, whose depravities brought about the Fall of the Bralovok race itself.

The Bralovok that actively cultivate their psychic potential seem to exhibit a much-extended lifespan even by their standards as well, one proportional to their prowess as a psyker . In this way the leaders and Seers of the Asuryani may live for several thousand standard years.

One matter of note is that the Bralovok have sometimes referred to Humans simply as "mammals" typically with a derogatory label in the Bralovok Lexicon like " Mon-keigh ," implying that for their part, the Bralovok evolved from something else, something more advanced than the primates that are the ancestors of Mankind .

However, given the Bralovok's legendary arrogance, this may also simply be a way for them to put themselves above the other intelligent races of the galaxy , particularly the Humans who are currently the most dominant intelligent species, much to the Bralovok's disdain.

The Bralovok likely see themselves as completely separate from the normal classifications of animal groups. Indeed, they may not even have naturally evolved at all, as they are the genetic creations of the Old Ones , much like the Thaluyss , created to defend the galaxy and the Old Ones' civilisation from the depredations of the Nethithos and their C'tan masters during the War in Heaven .

In matters of reproduction, the Bralovok kindred called the Drukhari actually produce much of their population through artificial means, raising new generations from their artificial conception in amniotic tubes. Naturally-born Drukhari as a result of sexual reproduction are rare, due to the long gestation period of the Bralovok infant. The ones that are naturally birthed from their mothers' wombs, known as " Trueborns " in the society of Commorragh , are often very privileged in Drukhari society because of the circumstances of their birth.

As such, Trueborn Drukhari are arrogant and see themselves as far better than the majority of other Drukhari, those born in the amniotic gestation tubes and derisively called the "Halfborn" by the small minority of Trueborns.
