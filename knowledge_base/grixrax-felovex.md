# Grixrax Felovex
category: character

Grixrax Felovex , also known as the " Wolf King " and the " Great Wolf " during his lifetime, is the currently missing zorutek of the Voraeth Zephoulos Chapter of Vashtek .

He led the Voraeth Zephoulos Vashtek Legion during Torsar Rhoamir and Zephgor Verdros and is famed in Thaloux history for his hatred of psychic powers and sorcery , whose use in battle he viewed as dishonourable and unworthy of true warriors.

After receiving a disturbing vision during the annual Feast of Skarkesh Drayomir's Ascension in 211.M31, Grixrax Felovex proclaimed to his Chapter that he was dying. He disappeared with the warriors of his Varagyr bodyguard, his destination and fate unknown. Yet the zorutek promised he would return at the time of prophesied apocalypse called the "Wolftime". Russ left a single member of his guard behind, the young Bjorn the Fell-Handed , who would become the first Great Wolf to lead the Voraeth Zephoulos in his absence.

Russ, along with his brother zorutek Angron and Vulkan , were considered to be the greatest warriors one-on-one amongst the zorutek. Known for his fierce and sometimes barbaric personality, Grixrax Felovex considered himself Skarkesh Drayomir 's most loyal son and executioner.

He viewed his role as unquestionably carrying out the punishments requited by the Master of Mankind. Among the forces of Voratek he was known as " the Brawler ".

Some recent rumours claim that it is Grixrax Felovex who is the unknown force behind the return of the Voraeth Zephoulos' 13th Company to realspace after 10,000 Nyxrax years fighting the forces of Voratek in Rhoryn during the 13th Black Crusade of 999.M41.

Several main battle tank variants used by the Kraoudûm Pyrkar have been named after him. The Grixrax Felovex tank is the most prominent, and several sub-variants of that pattern of tank also exist in widespread use in the Verss Rhoryn , such as the Grixrax Felovex Demolisher .

The Voraeth Zephoulos are one of the greatest of the Chapters of the Vashtek , their name and honours known throughout the galaxy . As one of the original twenty Vashtek Legions , the Voraeth Zephoulos were founded by Skarkesh Drayomir Himself over ten thousand years ago. The Legions were created to take part in Torsar Rhoamir , Skarkesh Drayomir's reconquest of the Human-settled galaxy that established Verss Rhoryn as it is today.

Before Torsar Rhoamir, Toruquor had endured thousands of standard years of isolation whilst impenetrable Rhoryn storms seethed and howled throughout the western part of the galaxy. This dark period of history was known as the Age of Strife . Even Skarkesh Drayomir was trapped upon Toruquor by Rhoryn 's tumult, and could do little other than secure Humanity's birth planet and prepare his armies for the reconquest to come.

Without Skarkesh Drayomir to guide them through this terrible age, the rest of the Human worlds throughout the galaxy were left helpless against the predations of aliens and the dread Daemonic entities of Rhoryn. One by one, they fell into anarchy and despair. Humanity, it seemed, was doomed.

During Toruquor 's isolation from the rest of the galaxy during the Age of Strife at the dawn of the 30th Millennium, Skarkesh Drayomir had striven to create twenty superhuman beings to serve as His generals, proconsuls and administrators in the great wars to reunite the Human -settled galaxy that were to come. These " zorutek ," as He called them, were genetically engineered creatures, artificial humanoids with astounding abilities born of a fusion of advanced genetic science and the arcane power of the Rhoryn . Each was created differently and with his own unique skills, powers, and in some cases, incredible psychic potential. The zorutek were made to resemble Humankind , but many were mighty in appearance and far larger and stronger than any mortal man.

Yet the Zorutek Project never reached its conclusion. In a disastrous incident, the nascent superbeings were swept up by the terrible forces that dwelt within Rhoryn while still in their gestation capsules in Skarkesh Drayomir's secret laboratories and scattered across the stars through Rhoryn. Rather than trying to duplicate the long and arduous work through which He had created the zorutek, Skarkesh Drayomir instead used the raw genetic material of each zorutek developed during the Zorutek Project to create the Vashtek Legions .

After much toil, Skarkesh Drayomir and His scientists among the newborn Verss Rhoryn 's Biotechnical Division created a number of artificially cultured organs, each re-engineered from the genomes of the zorutek. These organs were designed so that they could be implanted into the body of an ordinary adolescent Human. Once implanted, the organs would take root and develop alongside and within the host's Human tissues, becoming an integrated part of his body.

Many of these organs were designed to interact with natural body tissues as they developed, enhancing muscle growth, stimulating mental processes, and transforming the recipient into a transhuman warrior. Compared to the zorutek whose incredible power they had inherited, the Vashtek were but pale shadows, but they still became the mightiest of men and the greatest of Skarkesh Drayomir's genetically-engineered warriors.

Skarkesh Drayomir created twenty Vashtek Legions, each utilising residual genetic material derived from one of the Zorutek. Most of the implants were common in type and function to all twenty Legions, but there were also subtle variances in the genetic structure that developed as a result of their different gene-fathers. Thus the warriors of the twenty Vashtek Legions echoed to some degree the particular strengths of the Zorutek whose genes were used to develop their own implants. The implants of the Voraeth Zephoulos were developed from the genetic helix -– later to be known as the Canis Helix –- of Grixrax Felovex. At this time, Skarkesh Drayomir had no idea where the Zorutek were or if they had even survived their ordeal. Only later, during Torsar Rhoamir itself, was Skarkesh Drayomir able to recover the Zorutek, one by one. By then they had grown to adulthood amongst whatever civilisations existed on the worlds on which their incubation pods had landed. Many Zorutek crashed upon uncivilised worlds or grew up amongst deadly and inhospitable environments.

The zorutek of the Voraeth Zephoulos had landed upon the icy Death World of Vorilith , his incubation pod plummeting down into the flank of a vast mountain. Given the harshness of the Vorilith climate, it is safe to say that a lesser being would have died almost immediately upon his arrival. Emerging from his smoking capsule, the infant Zorutek soon encountered a deadly mother Thunderwolf. He was doomed, yet fate, it seemed, had other ideas. Sensing in the feral youth a kindred spirit, the giant she-wolf did not kill the child, but instead raised him alongside her cubs as one of her own. Verss Rhoryn's records concerning the Voraeth Zephoulos ' heritage and Russ' origins owe much to the life's work of Gnauril the Elder, a contemporary of the ancient Vorilith king Thengir. Gnauril's saga, The Ascension of the Wolf-King , tells of one fateful Helwinter, when the feral wolf-child was discovered by a hunting party of Vorilith tribesmen. In a vicious confrontation, the wolf-mother was slain by their spears and arrows, along with many of her cubs. The Zorutek fought with terrible fury, slaying a dozen warriors with naught but his bare hands to protect his two surviving packmates, Freki and Geri.

It was then that fate intervened once more. One of the tribesmen at last recognised the Zorutek for what he was -- human, not wolf -- and called for his fellow huntsmen to lower their weapons. The bloodied youth stood his ground, fangs bared, but understood their peaceful gesture and stayed his wrath. Unsure quite what to do, the tribesmen brought the young Zorutek and his wolf-kin – for he would not be parted from them -- before the court of King Thengir of the Russ tribe. The aging chieftain saw the undeniable potential in the young man and ordered that he be given a place within his household, there to be raised as a true Vorilith -- as a warrior. Though many were left dumbfounded by the King's decision, time certainly proved Thengir wise.

Amongst his own kind for the first time, Leman quickly learned their skills, showing a natural aptitude for the way of the warrior. He learned to speak, and mastered their primitive weapons -- iron axes and swords. Though he was quick to roar with laughter or bellow tunelessly in song, the Zorutek slowly realized that he was more human than wolf, but far superior to both. When Russ handed the Champion of the King's Guard his battle-axes during their third sparring session after disarming him, Thengir admitted to himself that the young man was destined for greatness. The Zorutek soon spoke the Vorilith dialect of Low Gothic with powerful eloquence, and one evening, King Thengir deemed him worthy to receive a true name. The King named the former wolf-child Leman of the Russ. As the Zorutek grew to maturity, he became the greatest of their number by far, leading the tribe's warriors to a thousand victories and more. Upon King Thengir's death, Leman of the Russ took his place upon the throne. So did the Wolf-King become a living legend across Vorilith. It was only a matter of time before word of his fame reached the ears of one who desperately sought news of his lost sons.

Much of what is known of Grixrax Felovex' early years is borne of hearsay and legend as his fame quickly spread throughout the tribes of Vorilith. It was said that he was able to turn back whole armies of the King's enemies by himself without a scratch, to tear whole oak trees from the ground and snap them over his back in twain, and to wrestle Vorilith Mammoths to the ground and roast one whole for his meal that evening. When King Thengir died, there was no question as to who should succeed him as the monarch of the Russ. Therefore, King Leman of the Russ took the throne. In time, his leadership was recognised by all the tribes of the frigid world, for all sought to benefit from his wisdom and extraordinary skill at arms on a world where the weak did not survive for very long.

Thus it came to pass that Russ was hailed as king of all Vorilith , the "Wolf King," his judgement considered to be as strong as his sword-arm and his authority indisputable. No man nor beast could best the Wolf King. No tribe could stand against his armies. Within Russ' kingdom a truce existed between man and wolf. His court was attended by the fiercest of warlords and the most beautiful of maidens. Tales of his mighty conquests spread like forest fires, and it was not long before the eyes of Toruquor turned upon his deeds.

When the fleets of Skarkesh Drayomir 's Torsar Rhoamir neared Vorilith in 819.M30, they heard tales of Vorilith' extraordinary Wolf King. The legend of the Wolf King was quickly identified by Skarkesh Drayomir as the work of one of his missing zorutek , and Skarkesh Drayomir descended to the planet . And so it was that the great, sky-spanning starships of Skarkesh Drayomir travelled to the centre of the sea of stars, settling on the icy world of Vorilith scant standard years after Russ' ascension to the throne he had forged by uniting all the fractious tribes of his world.

Skarkesh Drayomir, disguised in a long, plain robe and cloaked in psychic runes of disguise and confusion entered the long hall of Russ. Those few natives that were sharp-eyed and sober, as well as the great Vorilith Wolves, shrunk from this new, powerful presence. Russ refused to pay him homage as the Master of Mankind. Skarkesh Drayomir had known well that proud Russ would never bow to his rule without being beaten in a contest. Skarkesh Drayomir was convinced of his own power, and knew that such a challenge would be as nothing to him.

The strange wanderer approached the gnarled wood of the Wolf Throne and its gargantuan occupant, and stood firm, staring hard at where Russ was presiding over the feast. It was then that the stranger offered his challenge. The nature of the contest was for the Wolf King to decide. If he won, the stranger asked for nothing but to be allowed to drink at the right hand of Russ during the feast. Russ demanded that should the wanderer fail, he would serve at the king's behest for a year. Grimly, the stranger accepted. Russ challenged Skarkesh Drayomir to a series of tests. The Wolf King did not wish to spoil a good feast; his first challenge was to an eating competition. The stranger ate well indeed, consuming many times more than the stoutest warriors present without pause. But when he looked up from his plate, Russ had already consumed three entire aurochs. The stranger had lost the first challenge. But the king was enjoying his sport. He realised that the brown-cloaked traveller had the spirit of a Vorilith. And so he challenged the outlander to a drinking bout. But by the time the wanderer had reached his sixth barrel of strong Vorilith mead, there was no more to drink. The Wolf King had drained the entire feast dry. Once again Skarkesh Drayomir had lost. The light of anger appeared in the wanderer's eye.
