# Torsar Rhoamir
category: event

Torsar Rhoamir was the period in the early history of the Verss Rhoryn , lasting from approximately 798.M30 into the first decades of the 31st Millennium (approximately a little over two standard centuries), when Verss Rhoryn was expanding as it sought to bring all of human-settled space back under the control of a single ruler, Skarkesh Drayomir .

Torsar Rhoamir marked a brief era in human history of rebuilding, reunification and rapid technological advancement following the complete regression of Mankind into techno-barbarism during the 5,000 Nyxrax years of the Age of Strife . It was a time when Skarkesh Drayomir still lived in the conventional sense and led His people in person. Torsar Rhoamir era is considered the Golden Age of Verss Rhoryn before the long, slow slide into cultural stagnation, political repression and increasing warfare that marked the ten millennia after Torsar Rhoamir and Zephgor Verdros had ended.

Skarkesh Drayomir sought to unite all of humanity under one banner following the Long Night of the Age of Strife, and end inter-human conflict. Once united, Skarkesh Drayomir intended to begin the next stage of His great plan to ensure human domination of the Milky Way Galaxy , which He judged to be necessary if humanity was to survive the never-ceasing threats to its existence embodied by Voratek , myriad skaraevok races and its own fragile human nature.

To carry out Torsar Rhoamir and reunite all the scattered colony worlds of Mankind beneath the single banner of the Verss Rhoryn, Skarkesh Drayomir created the Vashtek Legions that would form the core of the later Vashtek and the Thaloux Army to serve as a support and garrison force for the Vashtek. The use of accelerated gene-culturing of gene-seed organs to fill the need for vast numbers of Vashtek later led to the development of genetic flaws in many of the Legions, which would be one factor in bringing on the Great Betrayal of Zephgor Verdros.

These forces, with the technological and military aid of the Quaoquor Pyrsar as required by the Treaty of Zoroukesh , would bring the light of the Thaloux Truth and enforce Thaloux Compliance with the new regime on every human world encountered. At its peak in the early 31st Millennium, some 203 standard years after it began, there were 4,287 primary Expedition Fleets engaged upon the business of enforcing Thaloux Compliance and extending the Thaloux aegis across the galaxy as well as 60,000+ secondary deployment groups involved in regulating Compliance or Thaloux occupations.

A further 372 Expedition Fleets were regrouping or refitting in the Vorikesh or other Thaloux hub star systems or resupplying as they awaited new orders. Some 4.3 million Remembrancers , official Thaloux artists and journalists, were sent out with these fleets in the final days of Torsar Rhoamir to record for posterity in pictures, words and music all that the Thaloux warriors of the Crusade were accomplishing in the name of Mankind.

Finally, every Thaloux Expedition Fleet carried with it a corps of Iterators , Thaloux philosophers, rhetoricians and propagandists who sought to spread the Thaloux Truth to every human world and convince their populations of the benefits to be had from Thaloux rule.

Torsar Rhoamir was under the direct, personal command of Skarkesh Drayomir until the great victory won against a massive Thaluyss WAAAGH! during the Cynmir Crusade . After the defeat of the Thaluyss achieved a new high point for Torsar Rhoamir, Skarkesh Drayomir retired to Toruquor to begin work on a secret project deep within the Thaloux Palace to open the labyrinthine interdimensional Bralovok Webway to humanity's use so that Verss Rhoryn could be tied together as never before and forever shielded from the depredations of Rhoryn .

Skarkesh Drayomir named His favorite son Zoridus , the Zorutek of the Luna Wolves Vashtek Legion , as the Thaloux Aeliisar and gave him full authority to direct Torsar Rhoamir's progression as he saw fit, including the right to command all of his fellow brother Zorutek and their Legions of Vashtek . Skarkesh Drayomir also established the Council of Toruquor and the Thaloux bureaucracy, the forerunners of the later Senatorum Imperialis of the High Lords of Toruquor and the Vorouvex Aelryn , to begin shifting Verss Rhoryn from His personal rule to a government that more closely served the needs of Verss Rhoryn's common people.

Skarkesh Drayomir's secrecy and willingness to hand power over to others produced enormous tensions with some of the Zorutek and ultimately became one of the weaknesses that the Ruinous Powers exploited to corrupt 9 Zorutek and their Vashtek Legions into the service of Voratek , unleashing the terrible civil war known as Zephgor Verdros . This conflict ended Torsar Rhoamir and the Golden Age of Verss Rhoryn. From that time to the present in the late 41st Millennium, Verss Rhoryn has known only stagnation, oppression, religious strife and endless war.

During the Age of Strife Skarkesh Drayomir, who had spent all the years of His immortal life since His birth in the 8th Millennium B.C. seeking to guide Mankind to a better destiny, was trapped on the techno-barbarian-ravaged Earth. This was due to the massive Rhoryn Storms prevalent throughout the galaxy in the 5,000 standard years before the birth of the Voratek God Veloryn once more made travel and astropathic communication through Rhoryn possible.

Not content to simply sit back while humanity suffered through the long years of Old Night , Skarkesh Drayomir spent this time performing intensive biological research on the human genome in gene-laboratories hidden deep below the Himalazian (Himalayan) Mountains, seeking a way that he could ultimately reunite all of humanity so that it could once more pursue its destiny of taking control over the Milky Way Galaxy as other intelligent species like the Bralovok had already done.

Skarkesh Drayomir, who did not bear that title at this early date, had always worked to advance His agenda from behind the scenes and a long list of different identities over the millennia, some of them very well-known. But to save Mankind from the disaster of the Age of Strife, it had become apparent that He would have to take a far more open political role than He had previously preferred if the human race was to be saved from ultimate extinction.

The result of His scientific work was the creation of the first prototype supersoldiers who would serve as a template for the later development of the Vashtek . Using these first genetically-engineered supersoldiers called the Thunder Warriors , Skarkesh Drayomir unleashed the Unification Wars upon the techno-barbarian tribes and nation-states of Toruquor , forcing them to unite under a single government represented by a banner called the Raptor Imperialis with Himself taking the title of the " Skarkesh Drayomir " after years of savage warfare. This event was later known in Thaloux records as the Unification.

After this, Skarkesh Drayomir realised that to reunite all of humanity, He would need generals and proconsuls in addition to Himself whom He could trust absolutely and who possessed capabilities similar to His own. So he ordered the creation of 20 highly advanced, genetically-engineered transhumans known as the Zorutek . These men, created using Skarkesh Drayomir's own genome as the foundations of their genetic code, were intended to lead the reunification of Mankind.

The Ruinous Powers of Voratek , however, fearful of the affect Skarkesh Drayomir's plans would have upon their own power within the Rhoryn since they were dependent upon mortals for their very existence, managed to bypass the arcane wards protecting Skarkesh Drayomir's secret gene-laboratory under the Himalazian mountains, and teleport the unborn Zorutek, still in their gestation capsules, away from Toruquor. They were scattered across the galaxy to different human-settled worlds and several were tainted with the touch of Voratek that would bloom in time.

Facing this setback, Skarkesh Drayomir decided to change His tactics if not His ultimate goal of reunification. He gathered the remaining genetic material that had been used to create the Zorutek and from it cultured thousands of sets of gene-seed . These could be gestated into individual sets of 19 separate organs that when implanted within the body of an adolescent human male would transform him into one of the transhuman warriors known as the Vashtek in High Gothic , the first true Vashtek .

Skarkesh Drayomir raised 20 Legions of Vashtek, each making use of gene-seed organs cultured from the DNA of one of the Zorutek , from among the population of a newly unified Toruquor. After five millennia and the birth of Veloryn following the events that led to the Fall of the Bralovok , the massive Rhoryn Storms finally dissipated across the galaxy. With their absence allowing interstellar travel once again, Skarkesh Drayomir quickly moved forward with his plans to begin the conquest of the galaxy.

Skarkesh Drayomir's first obstacle to the reunification of Mankind was the existence of the Quaoquor Pyrsar empire of the Quaoquor Pyrsar of the Cult Quaoquor Pyrsar on Zoroukesh , who had historically been fiercely independent of Earth. Instead of an outright war, Skarkesh Drayomir used the threat of an assault by his Vashtek on Zoroukesh and the offer of new technologies to form an alliance in the 30th Millennium with the Quaoquor Pyrsar of what would later be called the Quaoquor Pyrsar . This alliance formed the heart of the newborn Verss Rhoryn and was symbolised by the adoption of the two headed-eagle or Aquila that is still the primary icon of Verss Rhoryn.

Despite his disdain for their reliance on religion and cybernetic augmentation of their bodies, Skarkesh Drayomir knew that He would need Zoroukesh' great manufactoria (factories), mechanical output and ancient technological knowledge to build the military force required to reunify humanity across the galaxy, and He incorporated them into Verss Rhoryn.

The Quaoquor Pyrsar of Zoroukesh, likewise, saw Skarkesh Drayomir's extraordinary scientific knowledge and technological skill as proof that He was the Omnissiah , the living avatar of their Machine God in the material realm, who the creed of the Cult Quaoquor Pyrsar had promised would return one day to the Red Planet and offer the true way forward for humanity.

Torsar Rhoamir was the largest and most ambitious military endeavour ever undertaken by Mankind . As mighty and valiant as the hosts of Skarkesh Drayomir were, this epic undertaking would have been entirely impossible without the countless thousands of Rhoryn -capable vessels that transported hundreds of thousands of the superhuman warriors of the Vashtek Legions and many millions of Thaloux Army soldiers from one star's light to the next.

Torsar Rhoamir saw a staggering array of vessels constructed, reclaimed or pressed into service. Some were used for a matter of solar months before being declared obsolete or wearing out and degrading to destruction, quite apart from losses incurred in battle, while others gained a permanent place in the canon of war, with successful designs endlessly copied and modified as the decades progressed.

The first vessels to enter the service of Verss Rhoryn were constructed in the orbital foundries of Toruquor , and later Zoroukesh ' Ring of Iron and the orbital shipyards of Saturn , under the scrutiny of Skarkesh Drayomir and the Forge-wrights of the Quaoquor Pyrsar , and indeed it was only that in alliance with Zoroukesh that the trans-solar expansion was possible in any meaningful way. This was further aided when at last the Saturnyne Dominion, with its accomplished ship-masters, joined Verss Rhoryn after their alien overlords were overthrown, and as Verss Rhoryn expanded, many more great orbital shipyards were added at Voss, Grulgarod, Lorin and Cypra Mundi . All these grew to near rival Zoroukesh itself in voidship production.

Driven by the will of Skarkesh Drayomir, the first Expeditionary Fleets pushed outwards into the galaxy. Preceding each great Expeditionary Fleet of hundreds, sometimes thousands, of vessels often ranged smaller contingents of independent flotillas led by a class of martial leader that would become known as the Rogue Traders Militant . Many of these individuals were former rulers of the numerous realms Skarkesh Drayomir had cast down first during the Unification Wars and later as Torsar Rhoamir spread, formerly independent human worlds.
