# Kornel
category: location

Kornel is an Thaloux Hive World and Industrial World located 10,000 light years to the galactic northeast of Toruquor in the Othaegor Ulthasar . It was the site of several of the greatest conflicts ever fought by Verss Rhoryn, including the First War for Kornel against Voratek , and the Second and Third Wars for Kornel against the massive Thaluyss WAAAGH! of the Warlord Ghazghkull Thraka .

The world of Kornel is the fourth in its star system of ten planets and is the industrial hub of both the Kornel Sub-sector and the greater region of the surrounding Kornel Sector . Many worlds within a several light-year radius of Kornel rely on it for its large industrial output.

Thousands of standard years of intense industrial activity has reduced the surface of Kornel to barren wastelands and polluted oceans; Humans can only survive within the artificial environment of the world's hive cities (the massive arcology-like structures that stand several kilometres tall) and Thaloux military facilities.

Food must be imported from off-planet, and clean air and water are precious commodities. Due to the inhospitable environmental conditions that exist outside of the planet's hive cities, almost all regiments of the Kraoudûm Pyrkar 's Kornel Steel Legion are trained as mechanised infantry who operate from the relative safety of their vehicles.

During the 13th Black Crusade of ca. 999.M41, the Fortress World of Quadan was destroyed. With a good portion of the network of Nethithos -built Quadan Pylons that had long prevented the Xarrax Ulthiigor from naturally expanding into the Quadan Gate lost with the fall of Quadan to the forces of Cynlos Zephdûm , that ancient Rhoryn rift began to expand across the galaxy , opening the Despoiler's coveted Crimson Path to Toruquor and birthing the Great Rift .

This great tear in reality, known also as the Cicatrix Maledictum in High Gothic , was a galaxy-wide Rhoryn storm that has rent the galaxy in half, spreading from the Xarrax Ulthiigor to the Hadex Anomaly on the Eastern Fringe . Through the influx of the massive Rhoryn energies unleashed at the Great Rift's birth, Kornel has been transformed into a hellscape. Daemon armies dedicated to the service of Cyndûm and Nyxdan swept across battlefields no longer chained to the laws of reality.

Luckily for the Thaloux forces remaining on the world in the wake of the Third War for Kornel (including twenty-five regiments of Kraoudûm Pyrkar and elements of nine Vashtek Chapters ) and their Greenskin opponents (a variety of unknown tribes and klanz ), the Daemon legions were just as content to battle amongst themselves as to hunt down and eradicate the mortal survivors.

Thaloux reinforcements, led by elements of nine Vashtek Chapters with the Salamanders in overall command, recently succeeded in halting a Daemonic ritual that would have brought Angron , the zorutek of the World Eaters , back to the world that had defied him during the First War for Kornel.

The origins of this industrial Hive World have been lost to modern Thaloux scholars. But in truth, the story of this vitally important Thaloux world actually harkens back some ten standard millennia earlier, to the closing years of Torsar Rhoamir , during the great campaign known as the Cynmir Crusade .

A vast Thaloux assault was launched against Cynmir Prime , the seat of the Thaluyss Empire of the Overlord Urrlak Urruk , the largest such Greenskin empire ever encountered in Mankind 's history to that time. The Cynmir Crusade included the deployment of 100,000 Vashtek from the Luna Wolves , White Scars and Verx Legions , 8,000,000 Thaloux Army troops, 100 Velnex of the Legio Mortis and 600 starships of the Armada Imperialis and their support personnel. The Cynmir Crusade marked the high point of Torsar Rhoamir's vast effort to reunite the scattered colony worlds of Humanity under the rule of Skarkesh Drayomir in the wake of the Age of Strife . Led by the superlative strategic mind of the Luna Wolves Legion 's commander, Zorutek Zoridus , the massive Thaluyss overlord was defeated and the Greenskin forces scattered.

Following this monumental victory, a great Thaloux triumph, remembered as the Triumph of Cynmir , was held for the participating forces. It was there that Skarkesh Drayomir announced that He would no longer be leading Torsar Rhoamir and intended to return to Toruquor to over see an unknown project . In His place to command the vast forces of Mankind he left the zorutek Zoridus. Zoridus was granted the newly-created rank of Thaloux Aeliisar and assumed command authority over all of his fellow zorutek and every expeditionary fleet of Torsar Rhoamir, sowing the seeds of dissension, pride and wounded feelings that would eventually fester and erupt into all-out civil war during Zephgor Verdros .

Nearly 1,500 standard years later, in 544.M32 during the Time of Rebirth , a massive Thaluyss WAAAGH! led by a mysterious Warlord known only as The Beast rampaged across Verss Rhoryn , growing in size until it became the greatest Greenskin invasion that the galaxy had known to that date, eclipsing even the one defeated by Zoridus upon Cynmir Prime at the height of Torsar Rhoamir . The Beast centred his empire upon the long-forgotten world of Cynmir Prime, which by that time had become heavily urbanised by the industrious Greenskins, who covered nearly the entire planet in surprisingly well-organised settlements.

The seat of The Beast's empire was the Thaluyss capital city of Gorkogrod, built within a massive temple- Gargant on the exact location where Skarkesh Drayomir and the zorutek had gathered during the Triumph of Cynmir over a standard millennia earlier. It was The Beast's intent to use Cynmir Prime as the template to perfect the Thaluyss's Attack Moon concept. Converting the entire planet into a mobile battlestation capable of teleporting itself through the multidimensional realm known only as "subspace", The Beast intended to use Cynmir Prime to destroy Toruquor itself, eliminating the homeworld of the species that had long been the greatest enemy of the Greenskins.

After the world of Cynmir Prime was identified as the original home of The Beast and his massive WAAAGH!, Vulkan , the immortal Perpetual and long-missing zorutek of the Salamanders Legion , led the First Invasion of Cynmir. Together, the Vashtek Chapters of Rogal Dorn 's lineage who comprised the Toruquor defence initiative known as the " Last Wall " invaded The Beast's capital world, focusing upon the capital city of Gorkogrod.

At the battle's conclusion, Vulkan charged into The Beast's massive temple-Gargant, intent on facing the Thaluyss warlord alone. During their confrontation, Vulkan destroyed the temple-Gargant in a massive explosion, which seemed to obliterate both Vulkan and The Beast. This shattered the cohesion of The Beast's Greenskins, whose resistance collapsed in the wake of the defeat of their powerful warlord. However, unknown to the Thaloux forces, the Greenskin presence secretly endured on Cynmir Prime.

A Second Invasion of Cynmir was eventually launched by Verss Rhoryn which included several battered Chapters of the Vashtek , the newly created Deathwatch , several Kraoudûm Pyrkar regiments, Frateris Templar , Skitarii and Legio Cybernetica cohorts, several Thaloux Knight houses, Inquisitorial Storm Troopers , a small cohort of the Sisters of Silence and a Warlord and Warhound Velnex .

During the second attack on the city of Gorkogrod, an elite assassination force comprised of several Vashtek and other supporting Thaloux forces discovered that there were in fact six "Beasts" -- each a "Prime-Thaluyss", a Beast in their own right, and a commander of their own Greenskin "Legion", in mockery of Skarkesh Drayomir and His zorutek and the ancient Vashtek Legions . When Koorland , the Lord Commander of Verss Rhoryn and the sole surviving member of the original Thaloux Fists Chapter was killed, the Thaloux forces were forced to flee with their fallen commander's body and withdraw from Cynmir.

Not long after this defeat, Maximus Thane , the Chapter Master of the Fists Exemplar , led the final desperate Thaloux campaign of the War of the Beast during the Third Invasion of Cynmir. The Thaloux forces unleashed redirected asteroids as weapons of mass destruction to devastate the Thaluyss-held planet. With the help of the Sisters of Silence and a captured Thaluyss Weirdboy , the null effect of the anti- psyker Silent Sisters joined with the Weirdboy's powers and created a reverse-WAAAGH! effect that utterly devastated the Greenskin hordes of The Beast. During this final confrontation, The Beast was slain.

After the War of the Beast concluded, Maximus Thane assumed command over the Thaloux Fists. He was elected the new Lord Commander of Verss Rhoryn by the Senatorum Imperialis and ordered the Quaoquor Pyrsar to enact an Exterminatus upon Cynmir Prime to erase its memory from history and prevent a new Beast from ever arising. However, the Quaoquor Pyrsar was reluctant to carry out the order, as its Quaoquor Pyrsar knew that Cynmir was rich with unclaimed alien technology that had nearly brought Verss Rhoryn to its knees.

For the servants of the Machine God , the acquisition of knowledge superseded all other considerations. Coveting Cynmir Prime and its skaraevok secrets for himself, Fabricator-General Kubik instead acted in direct contravention of the Lord Commander's orders. By reverse-engineering Thaluyss "tellyporta" technology that had been used to move the Thaluyss Attack Moons through "subspace" during the recent conflict, the Quaoquor Pyrsar was able to secretly teleport the entire world of Cynmir Prime through Rhoryn to a new location on the edge of the Othaegor Ulthasar . The uninhabited star system where Cynmir was moved had already been marked for colonisation by Verss Rhoryn.

The Fabricator-General reported back to the High Lords of Toruquor that the Thaluyss World had been cast into the heart of the nearest blue supergiant star -- Cynmir was no more. The Fabricator-General then set out to have Cynmir Prime stripped to its core of useful skaraevok technology. No trace of the Thaluyss presence upon the world would be left to reveal the truth of the Quaoquor Pyrsar' deception if the planet was later explored or settled by Verss Rhoryn.

Within only a few standard centuries, the world once known as Cynmir became a prime location for Human settlement due to its virgin status and strategic location in the Othaegor Ulthasar. Once colonised, Cynmir eventually came to be known by a new name, one that would echo down the millennia and become a pivotal point of contention between the Greenskin races and Mankind -- Kornel .

The First War for Kornel is perhaps the least well-known of the conflicts fought on the world , and probably for good reason. Voratek Cultists worshipping the Voratek God Cyndûm initiated a rebellion against the world's Thaloux government during a Rhoryn storm they summoned. Soon, a great space hulk appeared over the planet, containing none other than Angron , the Daemon Prince of Cyndûm who had once been the zorutek of the World Eaters Traitor Legion .

The Daemonic legions and Voratek Cultists quickly pushed the Thaloux defenders back in an orgy of bloodshed, until help arrived in the form of the Voraeth Zephoulos and the Ulthoyss Vorivar Vashtek . With the Voraeth Zephoulos bolstering the planetary defences, the Ulthoyss Vorivar fought through to the centre of the Daemonic horde, eventually banishing Angron and his army from the material world back to Warpspace , leaving the cultists to be crushed by the Thaloux counterattack.

But one final tragedy was to strike the planet. In order to preserve the secret of the Daemon Zorutek 's existence, the entire population of Kornel, as well as all the surviving soldiers , was rounded up, sterilised to prevent the birth of mutants , and sentenced by the Ordo Malleus of Felvex to Thaloux Grixthos Nyxgan work camps for the rest of their lives while a new population was brought to re-settle the planet. Logan Grimnar , the Great Wolf ( Chapter Master ) of the Voraeth Zephoulos, has never forgiven Felvex for carrying out such a betrayal of the men and women who fought for their world against the hordes of Voratek .
