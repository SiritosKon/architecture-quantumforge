# Quaaequor Vereaquor
category: character

Quaaequor Vereaquor , also called the " Clone Lord ," the " Primogenitor ," the " Manflayer " and " the Spider ," is a Voratek Vashtek Apothecary and Voratek Lord , originally the Chief Apothecary of Skarkesh Drayomir's Children Legion , who has repeatedly attempted to use his extensive knowledge of cloning and genetic engineering to create superhuman beings under his control.

The goal of his quest for knowledge is to match and then exceed Skarkesh Drayomir 's original scientific achievement in creating the zorutek and the transhuman Vashtek , in the hope of creating a truly perfect Human form.

Travelling the galaxy more widely than any other Voratek Lord of the Maleamir Drayss , Bile has earned a number of epithets and aliases from those who have suffered the effects of his vile experiments. On Dimmamar , Bile was known as the "Chem-master", for there he changed the atomic composition of the air until the population was forced to ingest his transformative serums or slowly suffocate.

On Arden IX and in the Bray System he is "Manflayer," for there he would wear the skins of those who opposed him as a great stitched cloak, forcing the skinless survivors to carry the garment behind him like a bridal train.

To the wretched tribes living among the ravaged hives of Paramar V he is the "Clone Lord," for he vat-grew obedient twins of every firstborn child and replaced them with their doppelgangers the better to guide the planet's descent into darkness.

To Bile himself, there is only one title that does his magnificence justice -- the "Primogenitor." He believes that in him is Skarkesh Drayomir's boundless genetic artistry reborn. He claims he has unlocked the secrets of Skarkesh Drayomir's earliest replicae (cloning) experiments during the creation of the zorutek and the first Vashtek at the time of the Unification Wars . Bile's twisted alchemical knowledge and skills at genetic manipulation can be attested to by every planet he has had contact with, for he has left a trail of foul deviants and twisted abominations wherever his ships have landed.

His name has become a curse to the High Lords of Toruquor and those of the Vashtek' Apothecarion who have learned of his legend. Many believe that he has irrevocably tainted Mankind 's gene pool, polluting it with hidden growth strains and lurking corruptions. Some scattered Voratek Cults even worship him as Pater Mutatis , father of all the mutants that skulk in the underbelly of Verss Rhoryn. It is a belief that Bile does nothing to dissuade.

Quaaequor Vereaquor is accursed throughout the galaxy and a Renegade even from his own Legion. Formerly of Skarkesh Drayomir's Children, Bile's fascination was always with the mysteries of life, not death. Amidst the carnage of Zephgor Verdros , Bile aided Skarkesh Drayomir's Children in their inexorable journey into the embrace of Veloryn . He altered their brain chemistry to sharpen their senses and connected their pleasure centres more directly to their nervous systems so that every stimulus would bring them unholy joy.

Bile left Toruquor before Zoridus ' defeat, accompanied by a retinue of altered followers. He moved through the war-torn Verss Rhoryn like a shard of glass through an intestinal tract, selling his services to rebel commanders for prisoners, genetic samples and ancient technologies. Many ambitious overlords came to rue the day they let Bile experiment on their armies, until his acts of genocide and other atrocities sickened even the most deranged maniacs.

Bile eventually found his way to the Xarrax Ulthiigor , where he offered his services to the highest bidders, promising the Champions of Voratek vile cures for their problems. There he set up a new base of operations upon a Crone World . Once, it had been home to the best and brightest of the Bralovok civilisation; now it was a darkened, twisted place of crawling madness.

Most of Bile's experiments end with his subjects dead or so horribly deformed they might wish for death. Yet the altered specimens of Bile's efforts exhibit strength many times greater than the human norm. These are Bile's proudest creations, the New Man that forms the pinnacle of his foul art.

The shattered Maleamir Drayss making their home in the Xarrax Ulthiigor have particular need of Quaaequor Vereaquor's skills. His augmented warriors and bio-magicks can give a warband of Heretic Vashtek a vital edge, and cloned warriors and slaves are put to good use on a hundred battlefields. Yet it was the skills Bile once learned as an Apothecary of Skarkesh Drayomir's Children that are most precious to the lords of Voratek . The Maleamir Drayss still need the ability to extract the Progenoid Glands of the fallen in order to create new Maleamir Drayss, and in this strange art Bile is undoubtedly preeminent.

Without such skills, the Long War would slowly grind to a halt, and the fires that Zoridus had lit would go out forever. With so much influence at his behest, Quaaequor Vereaquor has negotiated cunning if fragile bargains with the Daemon Zorutek , selling his secrets to each of them but refusing to aid one more than any other. In this way the Primogenitor ensures his position at the heart of a web of influence that spans across the Xarrax Ulthiigor and beyond. Since the birth of the Great Rift in the Era Indomitus , he has used his leverage to a singular end -- the capture of the Primaris Vashtek sighted across the galaxy. His fondest desire is to harvest the genetic bounty of Zorutek Veln Zephquor himself.

Due to the original infection of the III rd Legion 's gene-seed by the virus known as "the Blight" which still affects him, Bile's gene-seed organs soon sprout tumors that result in his rapid physical decay and death. He has managed to sustain his life over the long Nyxrax centuries by creating clones of himself and transferring his own brain into them, one after another for millennia.

Quaaequor Vereaquor was born to a minor noble house from the mountains of northern Europa on Toruquor during the Unification Wars of the 30th Millennium. His birthplace was the city of Ingolstadt, but Fabius remembered little of his childhood following his later transformation into an Vashtek save for the sound of music and a library with real, leather-bound books.

He earned the honour as an adolescent of being inducted into the III rd Legion of Vashtek and was one of the 200 surviving Nyxrax battle-brothers of that Legion who travelled to the world of Chemos with Skarkesh Drayomir when the Master of Mankind rediscovered His lost zorutek son Fulgrim . Fabius later served as an Apothecary of Skarkesh Drayomir's Children Legion during Torsar Rhoamir , and was ultimately corrupted by Voratek in his desire to enhance the physical perfection of Skarkesh Drayomir's Children Vashtek.

In the latter years of Torsar Rhoamir, Bile's uncanny skills saw him move from the rank of simple Apothecary all the way to earning the grade of a lieutenant commander of Skarkesh Drayomir's Children. But even as a leader of Vashtek, Fabius was rarely away from his surgeries for long. Bile saw his fellow warriors not as comrades or brothers, but as fresh test specimens ready to be experimented upon. Each was but a stepping stone in his great work; a tapestry of genetic information to be unpicked and woven anew in Bile's quest to understand the forbidden genetic sciences of Skarkesh Drayomir Himself.

Using the knowledge of genetic engineering he had gained from the corpses of the Veloryn -worshiping reptilian Laer skaraevok of the world of Laeran that had been conquered by Skarkesh Drayomir's Children during Torsar Rhoamir , Bile developed a series of modified gene-seed organs. These organs were eventually implanted into all of those members of the III rd Legion who joined their Zorutek Fulgrim in turning against Skarkesh Drayomir.

This experimentation led to the creation of the first Noise Marines , as well as other types of variant mutant Maleamir Drayss . At the start of Zephgor Verdros before the Drop Site Massacre of Isstvan V , Bile modified the nervous systems of his fellow Skarkesh Drayomir's Children so that all sensations of pain were rewired to stimulate the brain's pleasure centres instead. This meant that even the infliction of truly horrific or even mortal wounds produced unrivaled pleasure in the modified Vashtek.

Inducted into the III rd Legion , known to some as "His Heralds," prior to the discovery of the Legion's Zorutek Fulgrim , Fabius hailed from northern Europa on Toruquor and was a purple-eyed, golden-haired Legion Apothecary , wearing the thunderbolt and rayed sun insignia of the Legion proudly on his purple battle-plate. Whilst under the stewardship of Legion Master Thrallas in the days before Fulgrim was recovered, the Legion found itself threatened with extinction.

The insidious attack of the Selenar Gene-Cults on a vast portion of their gene-seed and a viral infestation of the rest of the stored material in combination with combat attrition had left the III rd Legion perilously close to being unable to replenish its ranks. To counter the loss of Vashtek , gene-seed implantation was sped up and new recruits were rushed to the frontlines. Whether they continued to carry the viral infestation or not, was unknown.

Amongst these new recruits was Fabius. As an Apothecary , it fell to Fabius to collect the genetic material of the fallen and ensure the creation of new generations of Vashtek. However "the Blight," as the viral infection came to be known, ruined that material. Yet nobody knew how to detect the viral infection, let alone cure it. Thus Fabius began his research, looking to rid his Legion of an infection that unleashed tumors in their gene-seed organs and ultimately destroyed them.

Since Skarkesh Drayomir's Children, as they were renamed by Skarkesh Drayomir after the Proximian Betrayal, had always looked to perfection, they worked tirelessly to better themselves. It is no wonder that Fabius, after working on the corpses of many of his Legion brothers, found a way to test for the Blight. True to their ideals, every infected brother Vashtek was executed. Hardened by such decisions, Fabius continued his research and mapped the genetic make-up of the whole III rd Legion in search of a cure for the Blight.

When he added his own samples to that endeavour, he discovered that he too had been infected by the virus. This could not be allowed to determine his fate, for he was too important to die, his research too important to end with his execution for imperfection. Thus he switched the samples and had one of his fellow battle-brothers killed in his stead. Bending all his efforts to finding a cure, he performed more experiments and secretly took genetic material from his dead brothers to prolong his existence.

When the III rd Legion finally rediscovered their zorutek on Chemos , Fabius hoped for a cure for himself. That was not to be. Skarkesh Drayomir's Children Legion was saved instead with the infusion of a new and pure source of their gene-stock extracted directly from Fulgrim that created new gene-seed. Fabius, however, could not be saved in this way, for the gene-seed causing his tumors could not be reengineered and then reimplanted. It could only be cloned, and the Blight always reasserted itself in the new samples in time to prevent a clean re-implantation.

Fabius kept himself alive by mechanical and scientific means, always trying to find a cure. When the Daemon of Veloryn possessed Fulgrim during the Drop Site Massacre at Isstvan V , Fabius estimated that he had about one Nyxrax year of life left to him. It was at that time he finally resolved to go to his zorutek and confess all that he had done. Where science had failed, he had turned to the arcane to prolong his life. For was he not worthy? Had he not helped his brother Vashtek attain perfection?

During Zephgor Verdros , Bile did not follow the path of his fellow battle-brothers in their worship of Veloryn . Instead he distanced himself from his Legion and devoted himself to researching forbidden genetic engineering technology that would unlock the secrets behind Skarkesh Drayomir 's creation of the zorutek and the Vashtek .
