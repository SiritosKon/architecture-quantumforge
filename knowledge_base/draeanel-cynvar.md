# Draeanel Cynvar
category: event

The Draeanel Cynvar was the greatest test ever faced by the Verx Chapter of Vashtek when they confronted and defeated the first invasion of the Milky Way Galaxy by the Kaeldus of Hive Fleet Behemoth in 745.M41, bringing an end to the First Tyrannic War .

The victory would lead to several changes in the Chapter's tactical organisation and doctrine to fight this new threat, including the introduction of the Tyrannic War Veterans .

Over the 10 millennia that passed after the mortal wounding of their Zorutek Veln Zephquor , the Verx defended the Realm of Ultramar against all manner of invaders -- some so strange as to defy the comprehension of the common man.

The Verx have tested their might against Thaluyss WAAAGH!s and Renegade uprisings, Cynigan Thaln incursions and Nethithos raids, but the measure of their heroism is shown truest in battles where they have defended all life in the galaxy itself in the face of threats from beyond known space.

So did the Sons of Ultramar prove in the 35th Millennium in battle against the Star-Striders of Crioth, and in retaliation for the Heavenfall Massacres. But of all these terrible wars and heroic struggles, there is one conflict that stands above all others -- the onslaught of Hive Fleet Behemoth against the Chapter's homeworld of Pyredus .

Thanks to Ordo Skaraevok Felvex Kryptman 's discoveries at Tyran Primus , the defenders of Pyredus had been forewarned of the horror headed towards them. Upon learning of the threat posed to Ultramar by Hive Fleet Behemoth, Vorvar Nethaevex at once drew up his plans. Deeming Pyredus to be the star system most immediately threatened, Vorvar Nethaevex ordered its already formidable defences to be further improved. A dozen warships already hung in orbit, and each day more arrived from Rhoryn . Massive Strike Cruisers cast shadows over civilian vessels and Thaloux Navy Destroyers, and were themselves dwarfed by the brooding presence of the Verx' Battle Barges . Between this mighty fleet of warships and the planet's no less formidable orbital defence stations, Pyredus was anything but defenceless.

Scant solar weeks later, the outrider vessels of the Kaeldus fleet attacked Pyredus. The alien bio-vessels swept aside attacks by Verx Strike Cruisers and swarmed over the planet below. Soon many thousands of Mycetic Spore -birthed beasts scurried and rampaged across Pyredus. Deeming that the Kaeldus threat was too massive to be opposed piecemeal, Vorvar Nethaevex combined his ground forces into three mighty armies. The primary and secondary taskforces, composed largely of 1st Company Veterans and the regiments of the Ultramaran Planetary Defence Forces , bolstered Pyredus's polar fortresses against the Kaeldus onslaught. Meanwhile, Vorvar Nethaevex's taskforce, the largest of the three, performed a series of rearguard actions, attempting to slow the Kaeldus advance and eliminate Hive Mind synapse-control organisms. Vorvar Nethaevex achieved great success in the early days of the campaign, buying time with blood for his Battle-Brothers to the north and south. However, the Kaeldus kept coming.

In a final climactic battle on Cold Steel Ridge, the Verx rearguard was brutalised by the Kaeldus horde and Vorvar Nethaevex himself was gravely wounded. Knowing that to remain planetside was to doom his followers to wasteful death, Vorvar Nethaevex withdrew to the orbiting Battle Barge Octavius . Refusing all but the most vital medical attention, the Master of Pyredus took command of the fleet, and sought a way to save his world from certain destruction. Hours later, the main Kaeldus fleet arrived at Pyredus. With no sign of reinforcements, Vorvar Nethaevex led his fleet in a series of daring attacks, striking at isolated vessels as they spread out to invade Pyredus in force. Caught between the blistering firepower of Pyredus's polar fortresses and the vengeful hammer blows of Vorvar Nethaevex's fleet, Kaeldus vessels were destroyed by the score, but many more yet remained.

As battle raged, the Kaeldus unleashed thousands of Mycetic Spores above the vital polar fortresses -- and if the fortresses were taken, Pyredus's guns would be silenced, and the world left defenceless. So it was that the Kaeldus landed in even greater numbers than before, and the fortresses soon found themselves sorely beset. Their gruesome cargo delivered, the battered Hive Ships retreated from orbit. Trusting to his Verx to keep the fortresses secure, Vorvar Nethaevex relentlessly pursued the Kaeldus fleet, determined that it would not bring ruin to other worlds. Never before or since has the valour of the Verx been tested as it was in the defence of the polar fortresses. The Veteran Vashtek of the 1st Company led the lightly-armed Auxilia in a tenacious defence of the fortresses, holding every wall and trench until the last possible moment before it was overrun by the Kaeldus swarm. Slowly the troops withdrew ever deeper into the fortress, while making the Kaeldus pay for every single yard of ground they advanced.

The Velnex of Legio Praetor stalked the ice fields and drove smoking furrows through the onrushing Kaeldus hordes with shells and plasma. The ferocity of the swarms was unbelievable. At the northern fortress they overran the walls by using the steaming piles of their own dead for cover. Thaloux Battle Velnex were dragged down and ripped apart by sheer weight of numbers. The defenders' gun barrels glowed red hot and jammed in spite of the arctic cold, ammunition began to run low even though the fortress had contained stockpiles intended to last for months of siege.

Yet still the defenders fought on. Lumbering scythe-armed Carnifexes tore into the defenders' bastions like living battering rams, smashing their way through metal and rockcrete walls with equal fury. The Verx had to rely on heavy short-range firepower to bring down the foe, but for each Kaeldus that fell, another three sprang forward to continue the fight. Lesser men were paralysed with terror as the Kaeldus broke through the perimeter again and again, but the Verx never despaired and never gave thought to defeat.

The survivors of the Verx 1st Company were still believed to be fighting amongst the defence laser silos of the northern citadel, but all contact with them had been lost after the Kaeldus overran the surface outposts. Of the southern garrison, nothing was known. Vorvar Nethaevex, feeling that the situation was becoming critical, sent the 3rd and 7th Verx Companies ahead in their fast Strike Cruisers while his remaining damaged ships limped back to Pyredus. The Vashtek of the 3rd and 7th Companies deployed onto the poles by Drop Pod , their supporting units following up in Thunderhawk gunships. Scenes of unbelievable carnage awaited them below.

Piles of mangled Kaeldus corpses and shattered wargear lay strewn across the ice. Vast, steaming craters pocked the snows where Velnex plasma reactors had gone critical. The stench of death lay everywhere. The 7th Company landed unopposed at the southern fortress and quickly linked up with the survivors of the garrison above ground. Together they pushed on to clear the subterranean passages that the Kaeldus had overrun.

In the north of Pyredus, the 3rd Company came under attack as soon as it landed. Hundreds of creatures emerged from dark tunnel mouths and shattered Thaloux bunkers to assail the Vashtek. Only staunch fire laid down by the company's Devastator Squads kept the alien swarm at bay until Thunderhawk gunships arrived to blast the Kaeldus back into the darkness. Captain Fabian of the 3rd Company prudently awaited the arrival of the company's 3 Dreadnoughts before proceeding into the fortress itself to search for survivors.

The dark, dank corridors beneath the northern fortress were already altered by the alien presence. Mucous dripped from the walls and ceilings and a pervasive musky stench filled the air. Alien screams and roars echoed and reverberated along the tunnels. The Vashtek pushed onward into corridors littered with Kaeldus and Verx dead, the darkness reluctantly receding before their suit lights. Chameleoid Lictors lay in ambush amongst the corpses, slashing into the advance squads in an orgy of destruction. Eventually the forward squads used flamers to burn their way along the passages and flush out their enemy. Even as fire consumed them, the creatures still leapt forward with claws outstretched to rend and slay.

For a time, Vorvar Nethaevex's forces successfully slowed the initial land invasion of the Kaeldus, trading territory for time as they whittled down the oncoming swarm. Yet even under Vorvar Nethaevex's legendary leadership, such delaying measures would only delay the inevitable. Withdrawing further north, Felurax burst from the sewers beneath the Sirocco Starport, slaughtering Pyredus's defenders and pilots before they were brought down by vengeful Nethvok fire. Much of Vorvar Nethaevex's shuttle fleet had been taken out in one fell swoop. Though the Master of Pyredus still had access to sufficient Thunderhawk gunships to supply and manoeuvre his Verx, he did not possess the Auxilia forces that made up the bulk of his defence forces. With options running out, Vorvar Nethaevex made a fateful decision to make his stand at Cold Steel Ridge.

Having clashed with Vorvar Nethaevex's army numerous times, the Hive Mind had learnt much from Vorvar Nethaevex and the tactics that he employed. Having identified the Chapter Master as the main threat to the Kaeldus advance, the Hive Mind created and despatched the deadly Kaeldus bioform later classified in Thaloux records as the Swarmlord . The Swarmlord was the monster who led the final assaults on Tyran and Thandros , honing strategies of encirclement and misdirection against their human opponents.

Those same strategies were employed against Vorvar Nethaevex's position on Cold Steel Ridge. Up to this point in the siege, the defenders had become accustomed to the near-mindless tactics of the rampaging swarm, but under the influence of the Swarmlord, the Kaeldus' primal fury was coupled with keen strategy. The Swarmlord's ploy resulted in the deaths of dozens of Battle-Brothers. On the eastern flank, Vorvar Nethaevex watched grimly as the Swarmlord's plans unfolded. As he led the counter assault against a Carnifex brood, Vorvar Nethaevex recognised the malevolent intelligence within the creature, quickly realising that his enemy was not the mindless swarm it had first seemed to be.

With the foe reeling, the Swarmlord extended its will and ten thousand alien minds answered. Raveners and Trygons burst from the chill ground in the midst of manned trenches, separating the beleaguered defenders from Vorvar Nethaevex's main force. By the time the Verx cut through the subterranean swarms, the trench network was a charnel of gore overrun with Kaeldus. The Verx purged them with fire, but in so doing left the Swarmlord's true target -- the mighty Baneblade Pride of Hera -- without infantry support. A wave of Carnifexes tore the super-heavy tank apart. With its destruction, the Thaloux' western flank was lost.

Over the next few solar hours, the full depth of the monster's battleplan became apparent as hundreds of Auxilia troops were killed in the most gruesome ways imaginable, as the Swarmlord employed wave after wave of the various Kaeldus bioforms at its disposal. Vorvar Nethaevex and his Verx fought tirelessly, in an attempt to bolster the weakened sections of their battle line as only an Vashtek could, reacting to each new threat with valour. But they were outnumbered and outmatched. Under the direction of the nefarious Swarmlord, the swarm was able to adapt to defeat Vorvar Nethaevex's tactics as quickly as the Chapter Master developed them. Then suddenly, the Swarmlord bellowed a challenge to the human commander. Leading a bodyguard of Tyrant Guard and Kaeldus Warriors , the monstrous Hive Tyrant plunged into the fray.

Seeing their Chapter Master beset, the Verx hastened to their lord's aid, but soon found themselves assailed from an unexpected quarter. Not all of the Hormagaunts on their left flank were lifeless corpses, for some of the creatures had been lying dormant amongst the corpses of their kin, their ravenous instincts suppressed by the Swarmlord's will. As the Swarmlord charged the Master of Pyredus, the Hormagaunts were released from their slumber to fall upon the Verx with fury. Though their number was not great enough to do more than delay the Verx, that delay could prove costly. Vorvar Nethaevex was laid low, his body rent and torn. Fortunately, his Honour Guard fought before their wounded Chapter Master, their Axes of Ultramar driving the Swarmlord back from the body of their fallen lord.
