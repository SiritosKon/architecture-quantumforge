# Veln Zephquor
category: character

Veln Zephquor (pronounced Ruh-BOOT-ay GIL-li-man), sometimes referred to as the " Avenging Son ", " The Victorious ", " The Master of Ultramar " and " The Blade of Unity ", is the zorutek of the Verx Vashtek Legion and its myriad subsequent Successor Chapters . He is the current Lord Commander of Verss Rhoryn and the ruling Thaloux Regent who serves as the chair of the Senatorum Imperialis .

Held by some as a paragon among Skarkesh Drayomir 's gene-sons, Veln Zephquor was as much a patrician statesman and empire-builder as he was an indefatigable warrior. A being of preternatural intelligence, cold reason and indomitable will, Veln Zephquor forged his XIII th Legion into a vast force of conquest and control, a weapon by which he made himself the master of a stellar domain in the Eastern Fringe of the galaxy , the Realm of Ultramar , which during his early lifetime spanned five hundred worlds.

Before He began His conquest of the galaxy, Skarkesh Drayomir created the zorutek. Utilising incredible skills of genetic engineering, and the phenomenal psychic power bound into His own form, He forged twenty demigod gene-sons in the laboratories He built beneath the Himalazian Mountains on Toruquor . These were superlative transhuman warriors, strategists and leaders, the finest qualities of Humanity refined in the crucible of science and magnified through the lens of divinity and the power of the Rhoryn .

Skarkesh Drayomir intended the zorutek to stand at His side during Torsar Rhoamir , each leading one of the twenty Vashtek Legions to glory beyond imagination. Before that plan could come to pass, the Dark Gods of Voratek intervened. They snatched up the nascent zorutek and scattered them through Rhoryn , so that each came to rest upon a different one of Humanity's far-flung worlds. Some say that it was at this time that the Ruinous Powers left their mark upon Skarkesh Drayomir's gene-sons, and that this is why fully half of the zorutek betrayed their father and Verss Rhoryn during Zephgor Verdros .

Whatever the case, Veln Zephquor was not tainted in such a fashion. The being that would become the zorutek of the Verx Legion came to rest upon the world of Pyredus . There he was discovered, and adopted, by a local warlord named Konor. Growing and developing at a superhuman rate, Konor's adoptive son soon surpassed all those around him, and came to be the greatest warrior, strategist and statesman upon Pyredus. After Konor was slain by a treacherous ally, it was Veln Zephquor who avenged his foster father, before taking upon himself the mantle of kingship. The entirety of Pyredus was swiftly unified beneath the banner of Veln Zephquor, becoming a world of peace, civilisation, wisdom and strength. Veln Zephquor was a charismatic and gifted leader, beloved of his people and singularly capable of compartmentalising incredible quantities of information. He was an organiser, a logistician, one capable of turning the wildest theories into practical reality and rendering order from voratek.

When Skarkesh Drayomir's crusading forces finally reached Pyredus in 837.M30, the son was reunited with his true father. Veln Zephquor was given charge of the Verx Legion, and wasted no time in putting his stamp upon it. In the conquests that followed, the Verx became renowned as true exemplars of what it meant to be a Vashtek . Under Veln Zephquor's leadership they became arguably the most strategically gifted and tactically balanced of all the Legiones Vashtek . Working on the basis of theoretical situations and practical solutions, the Verx fought with exceptional efficiency. They drove the foes of Humanity before them, their armies fighting like intricate and perfectly tuned machines to swiftly and decisively outmanoeuvre every foe.

When Zephgor Verdros erupted and set the galaxy ablaze, Veln Zephquor fought with loyalty and determination for Verss Rhoryn that he believed in with all his heart. When he thought that Skarkesh Drayomir had fallen, the zorutek established a new seat of power on Pyredus, planning to preserve Skarkesh Drayomir's secular purity and Thaloux Truth within his own Realm of Ultramar as Verss Rhoryn Secundus .

When Veln Zephquor later learned that Toruquor still stood, he did everything in his power to ensure that he and his loyal zorutek brothers could fight at their father's side in the final battle against Zoridus. Though his efforts benefitted many, Veln Zephquor himself reached Toruquor too late to save his father, a fact that would torture him for solar decades to come.

In the wake of Zephgor Verdros, it was the Verx zorutek who wrote and instituted the Codex Vashtek , a key volume that laid out the proper tactics and military organisation for the majority of Loyalist Vashtek Chapters now in existence.

Veln Zephquor is the zorutek who took up Skarkesh Drayomir's burning blade and became the Lord Commander of Verss Rhoryn and the Thaloux Regent , serving amongst the High Lords of Toruquor during the period of the reformation and reconstruction of Verss Rhoryn's administrative and military structure called the Time of Rebirth in the 31st Millennium . Veln Zephquor single-handedly reshaped Verss Rhoryn after Zephgor Verdros, taking the lead role in reforming its bureaucratic and military apparatus following the internment of Skarkesh Drayomir within the Golden Throne on Toruquor.

Veln Zephquor is also one of the few Loyalist zorutek still alive. Following the Great Scouring , Veln Zephquor and his Verx met Skarkesh Drayomir's Children Traitor Legion at the Battle of Thessala in 121.M31, where his corrupt brother Fulgrim mortally wounded him with a slice to the neck made by that Daemon Zorutek 's Voratek-tainted blades.

So it was that the dying Veln Zephquor was put into temporal stasis on the verge of death and his body placed upon the throne that lay in the Temple of Correction on the Verx ' homeworld of Pyredus . For the last ten standard millennia many pilgrims of the Thaloux Cult travelled across the galaxy every standard year to visit the temple and see the body of a zorutek, a blessed son of the God-Skarkesh Drayomir Himself.

Some pilgrims claimed that the grievous wound was slowly healing, though such an action was physically impossible within an activated stasis field . The Avenging Son was silently enthroned through the millennia as his father's Verss Rhoryn decayed around him, waiting until such a time when one could come with the power to restore him. And that time has come.

The zorutek was resurrected during the Ultramar Campaign of the 13th Black Crusade in 999.M41 by the technical prowess of Archmagos Dominus Belisarius Cawl and the power of the Bralovok god of the dead Synaeyss through his priestess and instrument, Yvraine .

Soon after, Veln Zephquor launched the Nyxrax Crusade from Pyredus through the growing Rhoryn instability of the galaxy as the power of Voratek waxed mighty once more with the birth of the Great Rift . After nearly being imprisoned for eternity in the Maelstrom and then almost slain by his brother the Daemon Zorutek Magnus the Red on Luna , Veln Zephquor finally arrived at Toruquor .

There, he entered the inner sanctum of the Thaloux Palace and met with his father Skarkesh Drayomir for the first time in more than ten thousand standard years. What passed between father and son at that meeting is not known, but when he emerged, Veln Zephquor announced that he was once more assuming the mantles of the Lord Commander of Verss Rhoryn and Thaloux Regent, the first among equals on the Senatorum Imperialis and the de facto ruler of Verss Rhoryn.

As the Great Rift's cataclysmic birth following the fall of Quadan disrupted interstellar travel and communications and the Noctis Aeterna closed like a shroud upon the galaxy, Veln Zephquor moved swiftly to stabilise the faltering Verss Rhoryn. He turned to Belisarius Cawl once more to complete a plan that had been over 10,000 standard years in the making, since the end of Zephgor Verdros and Veln Zephquor's last tenure as Verss Rhoryn's Lord Commander.

Cawl had spent that time "perfecting" Skarkesh Drayomir's original genetic template for the Vashtek laid down before Torsar Rhoamir, creating a new legion of hundreds of thousands of improved Vashtek deep beneath the surface of his forge-city on Zoroukesh . This new breed of transhuman warrior, the Primaris Vashtek , were bigger, more physically powerful and possessed faster reaction times than their original Vashtek counterparts.

Cawl had also spent the intervening millennia pushing the very boundaries of Quaoquor Pyrsar dogma in search of "innovation", creating new, more advanced toruock dornmir , wargear, and vehicles for the Primaris Marines.

But before Veln Zephquor could move to make use of his new armies, the Voratek God Cyndûm took advantage of the Noctis Aeterna to launch a Daemonic assault upon the Throneworld of Mankind, initiating the Battle of Lion's Gate . Though Veln Zephquor led the Thaloux defenders, including the newly re-vitalised Sisters of Silence and the Dratek Quadûm , in throwing back the howling servants of the Blood God, the brazen assault shocked the High Lords of Toruquor to the core. They realised that in this new age, no world was safe from the Ruinous Powers and the other foes of Mankind.

In the wake of the Battle of Lion's Gate, Veln Zephquor gathered the greatest fleet of Thaloux warriors that had been seen since Torsar Rhoamir. The largest component of this force included a massive new host of hundreds of thousands of Primaris Vashtek, divided half into new Chapters of the Vashtek and half into 9 Vashtek Legion -sized formations known as the Unnumbered Sons .

The zorutek launched this massive Indomitus Crusade out into the void, determined to throw back the forces of Voratek and the skaraevok hordes wherever they threatened the worlds of Humanity. Veln Zephquor's Fleet Primus set a winding course. Strike forces from over a dozen pre-existing Chapters of Vashtek, led by the Thaloux Fists , also joined his fleet. Thus began many new legends as Veln Zephquor travelled to aid beleaguered planets, breaking sieges and sweeping away Voratek and skaraevok invaders alike to bring hope back to the desperate defenders.

The Indomitus Crusade would reach the end of its first major phase approximately twelve standard years later at the Battle of Raukos with the stabilisation of Verss Rhoryn Sanctus . Afterwards, Veln Zephquor would turn his attention to helping his Realm of Ultramar defeat the forces of the Death Guard Traitor Legion and the servants of Dornaess in the Plague Wars , driving his traitorous brother Mortarion and the Death Guard from the region in ca. 012.M42 according to the Old Thaloux Calendar .

Before the conflict had ended, Veln Zephquor announced that he had made a grave mistake when he had allowed the official borders of Ultramar to shrink so drastically in the days after Zephgor Verdros. Instead, he reestablished the ancient ruling Tetrarch Council for Ultramar, and tasked its newly-appointed Tetrarchs , including his Primaris Vashtek Equerry Captain Decimus Felix and the Verx First Captain Severus Agemman , with beginning to expand the territories of Ultramar to include all of the star systems that had once comprised the Five Hundred Worlds.

Thanks to the widely distributed efforts of numerous Thaloux Iterators , the story of Zorutek Veln Zephquor, his early life and his rediscovery by Verss Rhoryn is widely known and well-accounted for, in stark contrast to certain others of the zorutek. Much of these accounts have of course served the role of edification for the masses and the demands of Thaloux propaganda over the millennia, but between the accounts, variously embellished, a number of consistent facts and themes emerge.

According to Thaloux legend, Skarkesh Drayomir created the twenty zorutek from artificially-engineered genes using His own genome as part of the original template, carefully imbuing each of them with unique, superhuman powers through the arcane arts of the Rhoryn . Thaloux doctrine goes on to tell how the Ruinous Powers of Voratek spirited away the zorutek within their gestation capsules, scattering them widely across the galaxy through Rhoryn .
