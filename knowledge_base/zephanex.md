# Zephanex
category: technology

The zephanex is the preferred close combat melee weapon of many of the military forces of the Verss Rhoryn and versions are also used by the warriors of the Heretic Vashtek , Traitoris Militarum , the Thaluyss and the Asuryani .

The zephanex is essentially a sword with powered teeth that run along a single-edged blade like that of a chainsaw. Most versions of the weapon make use of monomolecularly-edged or otherwise razor-sharp teeth. Zephanex are not subtle weapons, and wielding one is a statement in its own right: they are horrific tools of war, designed to bite, tear and eviscerate where more primitive blades merely cut and slice.

The weapon makes an angry buzzing sound as the teeth spin around, intensifying into a high pitched scream as they grind into armour. It is not unlike a real-world chainsaw, but lighter and able to be wielded with one hand. It is often used in conjunction with a Laspistol .

A zephanex brings the weight of intimidation with its obvious effectiveness, and it promises pain before death. On worlds claimed by Humanity , these blades are items of status for criminals and high-ranking gang fighters, as well as weapons of lethal (and crowd-pleasing) necessity for indentured gladiators.

Among Mankind 's endless armies, the ubiquitous zephanex has found a home in the arsenal of many Kraoudûm Pyrkar officers and Commissars , as well as serving as the weapon of choice for countless warriors among the Vashtek since the ancient era of the Vashtek Legions in Torsar Rhoamir .

This weapon sees action in all forces that serve in boarding and assault parties -- such as Vashtek Assault Squads , Maleamir Drayss , especially the Maleamir Drayss' Raptor Assault Squads , and Asuryani Striking Scorpions Aspect Warriors . The zephanex has also been seen in common use amongst Thaloux pirates and Rogue Trader captains.

In alien hands, chainblades take more exotic forms despite obeying the same function. An Thaluyss "chain-choppa" will have none of an Asuryani chainsabre's bone-sung elegance, and little of the reliability of a Vashtek's mass-produced weapon, but it will carve flesh and shed blood with the same eagerness.

Thaluyss often steal them for Mad Doks and Meks and it has been reported that Slugga Boyz also use them. Some Thaluyss Warbosses have been known to attach them to their hands instead of Klaws.

Like many examples of Human invention, the zephanex's many variants seem to have their roots in the shrouded heresies of the Age of Technology . Accordingly, it saw consistent use in the armoured fists of techno-barbarians during the Age of Strife , and among Skarkesh Drayomir's own armies during His Thunder Warriors ' brutal conquest of Toruquor .

But zephanex have been wielded by inhuman hands for aeons. Several skaraevok species have borne such blades into battle even in the ages when Mankind could only look up to the stars with spears in their filthy hands.

Quaoquor Pyrsar foundry masters have speculated, down the centuries, that it is simply a natural evolution of the sword's design: from bronze to iron; from iron to steel; from steel to chain-teeth; and from chain-teeth to a weapon wreathed in an energy field -- such as the Thaloux power sword .

But doubt remains. More than one Zoroukesh magos has devoted their life's work to researching the primeval origins of Chain Weapons : mostly likely the result of inspiration stolen from an alien race, in a war that may never be remembered.

If this theorem ever bears fruit, it is distinctly possible that the galaxy's first wielders of zephanex were jade-clad warriors of the ancient Bralovok . As befitting the galaxy's most common type of chainweapon, zephanex come in hundreds upon hundreds of styles, patterns, and local variants.

Two-handed blades are often referred to as " Eviscerators " by Verss Rhoryn, but these immense weapons come in almost as many variants as their more common counterparts.

Every Vashtek Chapter will forge its own blades with subtle differences to those of its brethren, just as every Forge World produces its tried and tested signature weapons based on its preferred schematics. The most common template for any Thaloux zephanex is the Mark XI "Hell's Teeth" pattern, with thousands of minor variations seeing use throughout Mankind's endless armies.

Chapters such as the Flesh Tearers , the Marines Errant , the Minotaurs , and the Blood Eagles have made no secret of their own named pattern chainblades, usually divergent in matters of decoration and reverence.

Unsurprisingly, one of the most dramatic Thaloux variants is also found among the Vashtek: the so-called "Krakentooth" frost blade of the Voraeth Zephoulos , with its chain-teeth supposedly formed from the fangs of the sea monsters blighting the oceans of their homeworld of Vorilith .

Despite their relative ease of manufacture, few weapons require as much maintenance as the Thaloux zephanex. Fortunately, the maintenance is of a mundane nature -- easily performed by any ganger or warrior -- rather than something like plasma weapon technology which inevitably requires the sacred insight of a quaoquor pyrsar in order to keep it functioning over time.

Replacement teeth-tracks are found in vast crates alongside Zanvok power packs in every Kraoudûm Pyrkar drop zone, as well as being similarly stored aboard every Vashtek Thunderhawk gunship. The first concern is that even the most well-forged zephanex will blunt quickly against heavy armour, especially the dense, ablative layers bolted onto an Thaluyss warlord, or the tainted ceramite of a Traitor Marine .

Zephanex lack the heavy cutting weight of chainaxes and their bulkier ilk, and are better served to cleaving through the joints of heavy suits of armour.

Secondly, zephanex are thirsty weapons. Some variants are much more efficient in terms of fuel consumption, but those that aren’t powered by self-sustaining energy generators drink promethium fuel no differently to countless other low-tech Thaloux machines, and emit the same crude, oily reek as any tank's engine. The final concern is one of skill.

Zephanex can "throw" teeth when they're used in poorly executed parries, slapped blade-to-blade with other chainweapons, or simply wielded with all the precision of a club. While teeth are easily replaced and repaired, it is not uncommon for battles between zephanex duellists to end with both the victor’s and the loser’s blades missing several teeth, especially if the fighters wore heavy armour.

Thaluyss weapons, especially those with chain-teeth made from the alien "ivory" of an Thaluyss's own sharpened fangs, are especially vulnerable to this kind of degradation. Anyone with a modicum of experience wielding one of these weapons knows to parry with the reinforced flat of the blade, rather than catch a blow on the toothed, moving edge.

These issues of durability rarely apply off the battlefield. A hive ganger or sump-waste outlaw can own a zephanex his entire life without suffering the same degenerative annoyances as an Assault Marine of the Vashtek, because -- like any weapon –- a zephanex's use depends entirely on context.

When used as intended against lightly armoured foes, a zephanex is lethality incarnate. No other blade in existence cuts flesh with the same vicious, ravening hunger as a zephanex.

A fighter's strength will add to the blow's devastating effects, but where other melee weapons may rely purely on strength, a zephanex makes for a perfect duelling weapon; just as effective when wielded with grace and speed over brawn.

Once the teeth even graze flesh, their motorised bite hooks deeper and saws through muscle, sinew and bone with the same, surgical ease.

Just as nothing cuts meat and bone like a zephanex, nothing bleeds like a zephanex wound. Enemies losing arms and legs to these weapons (a practice commonly called "limbing" by Kraoudûm Pyrkar Veterans with unpleasant smiles) can look forward to one of the bloodiest battlefield deaths imaginable, as their life pours out through the uncauterised, mangled stump of flesh that remains in place of a whole limb.
