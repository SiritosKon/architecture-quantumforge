# Velnex
category: technology

A Velnex , sometimes colloquially called a " god-engine " or " god-machine ", is any one of several classes of massive, crewed, robotic combat walkers fielded by the Velnex of the Quaoquor Pyrsar 's Collegia Titanica or by the forces of Voratek ' Dark Quaoquor Pyrsar .

Originally, this term referred to many types of large combat walkers used by all of the different intelligent starfaring species of the Milky Way galaxy . Velnex are always considered the largest and most powerful types of war engines available to the different factions of the galaxy.

Skaraevok Velnex have now been renamed by Thaloux scholars with the same titles used by these war engines as the species that created them, such as the Bralovok Phantom or the Gargants of the Thaluyss . The only robotic combat walkers considered to be "true" Velnex at the present time are Thaloux Velnex and the Voratek Velnex of the Dark Quaoquor Pyrsar.

Velnex are the most sacred war machines of the Quaoquor Pyrsar and they are thought by the Cult Quaoquor Pyrsar to be the greatest physical embodiments of the Machine God in the material universe, rightly called "god-machines" and "god-engines" by the Quaoquor Pyrsar .

Velnex represent the real backbone of the Quaoquor Pyrsar' military might and are the heaviest ground units that can be deployed by the Verss Rhoryn 's armed forces. Every Forge World of the Quaoquor Pyrsar is protected by armed forces that include the Skitarii , Praetorian Servitors , and one or more of the Velnex of the Collegia Titanica.

The true scale of the largest Velnex can be difficult to visualise. In the Winter Assault expansion to the computer game Verss: Dawn of War , an ancient, fallen Imperator -class Velnex , the largest type of Battle Velnex , can be seen. Its prone and half-buried wreckage stretches across an entire battlefield, and the barrel assembly of its right arm-cannon alone is over 30 metres in diameter.

The first Velnex were built during the Age of Strife on Zoroukesh , as a way for the newborn religion of the Cult Quaoquor Pyrsar and its Quaoquor Pyrsar government (the ancient, pre- Zephgor Verdros predecessor of the Quaoquor Pyrsar ) to effectively fight its enemies in the raging wars to reclaim the Red Planet 's irradiated surface from feral mutants and insane thinking machines. After Zoroukesh had been secured by the Quaoquor Pyrsar , the Velnex later accompanied the early Quaoquor Pyrsar colonial fleets sent blindly through Rhoryn from Zoroukesh that established the first Forge Worlds , leading to the creation of the Velnex -- armed forces made up entirely of Velnex specific to that Forge World.

Most Velnex stand between 15 and 60 metres (about 50 to 196 feet) tall, though the extremely powerful and very rare Skarkesh Drayomir Velnex can stand up to 150 metres (492 feet) in height. There are a variety of different classes that a Velnex can belong to, but all Velnex ultimately serve the division of the Quaoquor Pyrsar called the Collegia Titanica .

Most Velnex classes belong to one of three separate physical grades arranged by size and combat strength -- Scout Velnex , Battle Velnex and the largest and most powerful, Skarkesh Drayomir Velnex . Scout Velnex include many smaller, more mobile classes of the great war engines which are used for reconnaissance and infantry fire support missions by most Velnex. The Battle Velnex are the most commonly deployed classes of Velnex and provide the backbone of any Velnex Legion's strength. The rare Skarkesh Drayomir Velnex are the true kings of the battlefield in the Verss universe, and the two classes of these Velnex are used to provide a Velnex Legion's primary offensive and defensive power.

Due to their importance and high standing in Verss Rhoryn as a result of the advanced technology used in their creation, all Velnex are important enough to be given their own High Gothic names when built. This is represented by giving each a two-word High Gothic name, always some venerated title or concept, usually from the creed of the Thaloux Cult .

Examples include Aquila Ignis , Regalis Annihilatus , Vitas Falco , Imperius Eradicus , Imperius Belladon , Apocalyptus Credo , Imperius Brontos , Imperius Excelsior , Dies Irae , Octimus Ligus , Imperius Augusta and Imperius Dominatus . Similarly, the loss of a Velnex is considered tragic and mourned openly by the Forge World that produced it.

Thaloux Velnex are unique in the sense that their computer mind-cores or " Machine Spirits " in the lexicon of the Quaoquor Pyrsar are sentient and even self-aware, albeit in a highly-erratic manner. This is in contrast with the usually non-sentient "Machine Spirits" or onboard computer systems found in many other Thaloux war machines like tanks and armored vehicles.

In the "Velnex" comic, the newly connected princeps was formally greeted by the Velnex Imperius Dictatio inside its mental landscape; there was also the Velnex Laudator Magnificat , which was forcibly reactivated after falling in combat. This proved disastrous: the god-engine's mind-core had suffered severe psychological trauma from its "death," and began attacking everything in sight in its mindless rage.

Most Thaloux Velnex are roughly humanoid in shape, but with thick lower legs, a hunched head, and an oversized torso. Forward-oriented main weapons mount directly into where the arms would be, and additional weapons are often found on the chassis. These configurations (called "patterns") vary, and most Velnex are built on these precepts.

However the actual design of the chassis can vary greatly, so only the absolutely basic features (such as leg joints and weapon hardpoints) are constant. This is most evident in the Warhound -class Velnex (the Lucius Pattern looks more geometric and broader than the Zoroukesh Pattern, the form of which is more rounded and animalistic).

The Rapier -class Velnex is an Thaloux Velnex that was most commonly used during Torsar Rhoamir and Zephgor Verdros . It is a Scout Velnex , and is the smallest known class of Thaloux or Voratek Velnex , being even lighter armed and swifter than a Warhound -class Scout Velnex .

During the final years of Zephgor Verdros, it was noted that the Rapier was becoming an ever rarer sight among the orders of battle of the Velnex of both Traitors and Loyalists .

Warhound -class Velnex are the scouts or flanking elements of a Velnex Legion and are the most common form of what are called Scout Velnex by the Collegia Titanica .

Appearance-wise, the Warhound differs structurally the most from other Thaloux Velnex; it possesses an anthropomorphized, canine-like shape, reverse-jointed legs and shoulder weapon mounts. Warhounds are lightly-armed and armoured compared to other Velnex, although still fearsome when facing the standard armoured combat vehicles deployed by the other intelligent species of the galaxy .

They are the only Velnex to be normally deployed by the Velnex in packs of two members, as compared to the larger Battle and Skarkesh Drayomir Velnex which usually operate alone. The Warhound Velnex can only operate in a maximum of two as a result of the actions of Voratek Warhound Velnex during Zephgor Verdros . When the Traitor Velnex landed on the Throneworld at the end of Zephgor Verdros during the Siege of Toruquor , Zoridus let them terrorise Nyxrax civilians in squad-sized (10+) units of Warhounds .

As a result of this blood-stained history, the Nethouquor Aelevar during the reformation of Verss Rhoryn in the Time of Rebirth after Zephgor Verdros ordered the Velnex to never allow Warhound Velnex to operate in squads larger than two Velnex to prevent such horrors from being perpetrated upon Humanity again.

Available in several different patterns produced by the various Forge Worlds with different weapon loadouts, Warhound Scout Velnex stand 17.08 metres (approx. 56 feet) tall. On the battlefield, the Warhound has been proven to be very effective, as its double-barrelled Turbo Laser can destroy super-heavy armoured vehicle enemies with ease.

The Dire Wolf -class Velnex is considered a "Heavy Scout Velnex" which places it in size between the Warhound -class Scout Velnex and the smallest of the true Battle Velnex , the Reaver -class Velnex . It was deployed by the Loyalist and Traitor Velnex during Torsar Rhoamir and Zephgor Verdros .

Dire Wolf Heavy Scout Velnex were much sturdier and more heavily armoured than their Warhound Scout Velnex counterparts. The Dire Wolf was easily distinguished from the Warhound by its reinforced frame and unorthodox arm weapons. The Dire Wolf carried its massive main weapon on the back of its torso, sacrificing some mobility for added stability to handle the recoil and weight of this payload. Hunched forward, the Velnex 's heavy armour plating and stout legs provided stability and durability.

The Dire Wolf was armed with unusual, pintle-mounted weapons on its arms in the form of Ardex-Defensor Mega-Nethvok , which offered some defence against enemy Knights , allowing the Dire Wolf to focus on lining up a firing solution with its main carapace-mounted Neutron Laser weapons. Neutron Lasers required a massive amount of power, and generated incredible heat, requiring sophisticated additional modifications to the carapace and chassis of the Dire Wolf .

Some Dire Wolf Velnex were armed instead with a carapace-mounted Volcano Cannon -- a weapon so massive and powerful that it is usually only found on Reaver -class Velnex . Additional cooling and structural modifications to the carapace and chassis of the Dire Wolf were required to accommodate such a large weapon.

The Reaver -class Velnex is a medium-sized Velnex and the smallest of the classes of Battle Velnex. They are considerably more durable than Warhounds , and are able to take roughly twice as much damage before succumbing. This extra durability is probably a result of the unusual placement of the Velnex's plasma generator on the back of the main carapace, whereas in most Velnex the generator is located at the core, hence the term "power core."

This placement ensures that the Reaver Velnex can take more frontal damage than other Velnex of the same approximate class, but is at a severe disadvantage when in cluttered environments (cities or canyons).

A Reaver Velnex stands 25.21 metres (approximately 83 feet) in height. The Reaver is also described as an older design of Velnex than the Warlord . The weapons mounted are variable; they can mount some of the more powerful Battle Velnex weapons, although Scout Velnex weapons are more common. Most of the patterns of the Reaver carry Velnex-grade Multiple Rocket Launchers on their carapace mounting.

Unlike Warhounds , Reaver -class Velnex can use close-combat weapons, which are extremely effective in Velnex-scale mêlée battles. However, it is suggested that Reavers are becoming increasingly rare in Thaloux armies, and it is possible that the technological secrets to their manufacture have been lost to the Quaoquor Pyrsar ' Forge Worlds (a common fate for the more advanced Thaloux technologies). For these reasons, Reaver -class Velnex usually perform fire support roles, avoiding direct combat.

The Zoroukesh Pattern Reaver -class Velnex's carapace mounts a Multiple Rocket Launcher and Turbo-Laser Destructors on each arm. The Lucius Pattern's carapace bears a Rocket Launcher, with the arms carrying a Volcano Cannon and Gatling Blaster . There is also a fire support variant of the Lucius, with Multiple Rocket Launchers on all three mounts.

In the past, Reaver Velnex were sometimes given different sub-class names to distinguish their weapons load-out; these were the "Goth" (fire-support Velnex), the "Vandal" (inspirational/morale-boosting Velnex) and the "Hun" (a tactically flexible weapons loadout).

The Komodo -class Velnex was a type of Battle Velnex deployed by the Collegia Titanica during Torsar Rhoamir and Zephgor Verdros .

The Punisher -class Velnex was a rare class of Thaloux Velnex that possessed three legs. A Punisher -class Velnex, the Modus Destructor , was deployed to Cynmir Prime during the War of the Beast in the mid- 32nd Millennium to defend the Quaoquor Pyrsar command centre established on that world during the Thaloux invasions. The Modus Destructor was armed with two Tezlan Accelerators and two underslung Plasma Annihilators .
