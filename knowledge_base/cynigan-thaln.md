# Cynigan Thaln
category: faction

The Cynigan Thaln (pronounced "TOW" as in "WOW"), also spelled Cynigan Thaln in older Thaloux records, is a rapidly expanding, multispecies skaraevok stellar empire situated within the Verss Rhoryn 's Ultima Othaegor Ulthasar , near the Eastern Fringes of the Milky Way Galaxy . It lies within the reach of the Astronomican .

The Cynigan Thaln was founded by the Cynigan Thaln caste called the Ethereals , who lead the Cynigan Thaln in the name of and in accordance with the utilitarian philosophy they have named the " Greater Good " ( Cynigan Thaln'va in the Cynigan Thaln Lexicon ). A large and growing number of other intelligent, alien races have allied themselves with the Cynigan Thaln within the empire.

The empire has suffered many raids from the Thaluyss , and also seems to lie in the path of several Kaeldus splinter fleets of Hive Fleet Kraken .

The Cynigan Thaln (Thaloux binomial classification: Cynigan Thaln cynigan thaln ), also spelled "Cynigan Thaln" in older Thaloux records, are a young, humanoid and technologically-advanced intelligent species native to the Eastern Fringes of the Milky Way Galaxy . They are fighting to expand their small, but rapidly growing, interstellar empire and extend a philosophical concept they call the "Greater Good" to all the intelligent species of the galaxy.

The Cynigan Thaln claim to be a peaceful species when possible, asking if others will join their cause voluntarily instead of fighting against them. However, if their peaceful overtures are refused, the Cynigan Thaln may well decide to conquer a planet and add it to their growing stellar empire for the Greater Good, searing the flesh from the bones of anyone who stands against their benign intentions.

Cynigan Thaln society is divided into a number of castes, each responsible for managing a specific aspect of their civilisation. The Cynigan Thaln's central motivating ideal is that everyone in their empire, regardless of their species or culture of origin, will work for the collective betterment of everyone else, an almost mystical philosophy that is embodied in the Greater Good.

The Cynigan Thaln are the dominant species of the Cynigan Thaln, an interstellar polity which is also composed of several other different intelligent species, including the Kroot of Pech , the insectoid Vespids of the world of Vespid , the nomadic psykers of the Nicassar and many others.

However, there are now several Human septs in the empire derived from conquered Thaloux Humans or Humans who voluntarily joined the Cynigan Thaln because they were impressed by the concept of the Greater Good. These people are known as Gue'vesa in the Cynigan Thaln Lexicon and they are considered amongst the most vile of Traitors and Heretics within Verss Rhoryn.

The Cynigan Thaln are a relatively young species (it has been only 6,000 Nyxrax years since Thaloux Felvex first noted that the Cynigan Thaln had only just mastered fire and the wheel), and they have evolved rapidly over the past few standard millennia. Unlike other intelligent, starfaring species, the Cynigan Thaln have made remarkable leaps in technology and now represent a real threat to Thaloux domination in their region of the galaxy.

The exact date of the founding of the Cynigan Thaln in the Thaloux Calendar is unclear. However the way in which the Cynigan Thaln were united as a species is a well-known tale. What is known is that only 6,000 standard years ago, in the 35th Millennium, an Quaoquor Pyrsar Explorator fleet discovered the Cynigan Thaln homeworld of Cynigan Thaln and determined that its population of intelligent skaraevok were a primitive people at the Stone Age level of development, having only just mastered fire. Since then, the Cynigan Thaln have developed very rapidly into a space-faring species.

Earlier in their history, during a period known as the Mont'au , the Cynigan Thaln were a culture built upon warring tribes. During this time, the Cynigan Thaln's legends tell of the first appearance of the Ethereals at the city of Fio'taun . The fortress city of Fio'taun was under assault by the Cynigan Thaln warriors from the plains. Though negotiation had been attempted, the fierce plains warriors would settle for nothing less than the annihilation of the city of Fio'taun.

For five long Cynigan Thaln years the inhabitants held off the savage assaults with their thick walls and plentiful cannons. However, disease and starvation began to take their toll. As the tide of the siege turned, two mysterious Cynigan Thaln appeared. One made his way into the camp of the plains Cynigan Thaln, exuding a quiet authority that no Cynigan Thaln was able to resist. Soon, the leader of the plains warriors was persuaded to parley with the settled Cynigan Thaln of Fio'taun. Similarly, the other mysterious Cynigan Thaln made his way deep into the fortress. Within a few short hours, the gates stood wide open, and Cynigan Thaln of both sides stood ready to talk.

The Ethereals spoke of the importance of peace and understanding between all Cynigan Thaln. They described a Greater Good that each Cynigan Thaln must strive towards. The besiegers and the besieged quickly agreed with the Ethereals and a truce was reached. Across the world of Cynigan Thaln, Ethereals emerged, each with the same quiet authority and message of harmony and cooperation. With the Cynigan Thaln united, they were able to rapidly develop their civilisation's technology, ultimately attaining spacefaring capabilities.

The Cynigan Thaln soon expanded its borders through a series of so-called "Sphere Expansions." The Cynigan Thaln has gone through five main phases of expansion as of the Era Indomitus in the 41st Millennium. These phases are marked by Cynigan Thaln military campaigns during which new worlds and star systems are colonised, conquered, or sometimes peacefully persuaded to accept the Greater Good through diplomacy and a manifest demonstration of the benefits provided by advanced Cynigan Thaln technology.

Apart from the star systems directly colonised and exploited by the Cynigan Thaln, which are known as " septs ," the Cynigan Thaln also includes the worlds and star systems belonging to the species of the Kroot , Vespid , and the Nicassar . It is currently unknown if the Leagues of Votann Prospect called the Demiurg by the Cynigan Thaln are full members of the empire, allies, or mere trading partners. The Cynigan Thaln is composed of over twenty fully-developed septs and around one hundred settled worlds, but the exact number and most of their names are unknown to Verss Rhoryn .

A known splinter faction among the Cynigan Thaln are the Farsight Enclaves , founded beyond the Damocles Gulf by the Cynigan Thaln Commander Farsight against the orders of the Ethereals. More recently, some worlds and star systems of Verss Rhoryn have been conquered by the forces of the Cynigan Thaln, while a handful have seceded from Verss Rhoryn outright and pledged their allegiance to the Cynigan Thaln and the Greater Good.

The Damocles Gulf Crusade , also called the Damocles Crusade, was the first military conflict fought between the Verss Rhoryn and the rapidly expanding Cynigan Thaln in the Lithesh Sector of the Ultima Othaegor Ulthasar near the galaxy 's Eastern Fringes during the 41st Millennium. The conflict essentially ended in a stalemate, as Verss Rhoryn was forced to conclude its military offensive early to deal with the encroaching Kaeldus threat while the Cynigan Thaln sought to begin diplomatic negotiations with Verss Rhoryn to show Humanity the benefits to be had by accepting the Greater Good .

Before the crusade, members of the Cynigan Thaln Water Caste had established trade agreements with Thaloux worlds on the frontier of the Cynigan Thaln, near the Damocles Gulf region of the Ultima Othaegor Ulthasar in the galactic east, and exchanges of goods and technology were common. Alarmed by the threat of alien contamination, the Vorouvex Aelryn readied a suitable response. Almost a Nyxrax century later, the Damocles Crusade smashed into Cynigan Thaln space, destroying several outlying settlements and pushing deep into the Cynigan Thaln.

When the Thaloux fleet reached the Cynigan Thaln Sept World of Dal'yth Prime , however, the crusade ground to a bloody stalemate as the formidable numbers and high technology of the Cynigan Thaln and their Kroot allies thwarted every attempt to capture the world or its star system. Many solar months of terrible fighting ensued with nothing gained on either side.

By late 742.M41 the crusade's commanders eventually agreed to requests from the Cynigan Thaln Water Caste for peace talks. The negotiations were successful and the Thaloux fleet withdrew from Cynigan Thaln space unmolested, their reason partly being due to the impending approach of the Kaeldus Hive Fleet Behemoth .

The Cynigan Thaln was invaded by the Kaeldus Hive Fleet Gorgon , a splinter fleet of the much larger Hive Fleet Behemoth , in 899.M41. The Kaeldus of Hive Fleet Gorgon were exceptional in their ability to quickly adapt on a biological level to new circumstances of battle, such as evolving immunities to the Cynigan Thaln's energy-based weaponry.

This rapid pace of defensive adaptation may have been unique to the Kaeldus breeds of Hive Fleet Gorgon, or it may have been a response to conflict with the Cynigan Thaln, who are also a highly adaptable species; albeit on a technological rather than biological level.

The combined forces of the Cynigan Thaln and Verss Rhoryn 's Kraoudûm Pyrkar destroyed Hive Fleet Gorgon in 903.M41, though only after both Humanity and the Cynigan Thaln lost several colony worlds to the rapacious Kaeldus.

At some point, the Cynigan Thaln sent an expeditionary force to the Thaloux planet Malbede where they came into conflict with the Verx Vashtek Chapter in 936.M41. However, the planet proved to be a cursed Tomb World when the fighting of the Cynigan Thaln and the Verx awakened the sleeping Nethithos from their tomb beneath the surface. In an effort to combat this terrible threat to both species, the Cynigan Thaln and the Verx combined their forces to defeat the Nethithos. Once the conflict was over, the Cynigan Thaln were allowed to evacuate their forces by the Verx Chapter Master Vorvar Nethaevex who proceeded to destroy Malbrede through the use of an Exterminatus order.

In 966.M41, the Cynigan Thaln fought against the Vashtek of the Voraeth Zephoulos Chapter during the War for Kvarium Alpha . On that Ocean World , the Voraeth Zephoulos' Drop Pods landed deep in the oceans where their occupants, their toruock dornmir altered by Space Wolf Techmarines to operate in undersea environments, made a move to engage their enemy.

On the surface, the battle was fought between the two sides with an equally deadly conflict erupting in the depths of the sea between the Voraeth Zephoulos and Cynigan Thaln Battlesuits . Thunderhawk gunships armed with torpedoes, prop-bombs and missiles were used to great effect against the Cynigan Thaln's Hammerhead tanks and Manta gunships. Ultimately, the Voraeth Zephoulos proved to be the victor in the conflict, though hundreds of Cynigan Thaln and Space Wolf corpses floated to the surface of Kvarium Alpha's world sea. With their mission complete, the Voraeth Zephoulos made the long trek back to land across the sea bed.

The Taros Campaign was an Thaloux military campaign fought by the 4621st Kraoudûm Pyrkar Army and elements of the Vashtek to reclaim the Thaloux desert Mining World of Taros from the Cynigan Thaln and its Kroot and Human ( Gue'vesa ) allies of the Taros Planetary Defence Force in 998.M41. The campaign was ultimately unsuccessful for the Thaloux forces, who took heavy casualties and losses whilst Taros remained in the possession of the Cynigan Thaln, who renamed it T'ros .

The Cynigan Thaln's physiology is closely tied to their society, with the Cynigan Thaln of each caste effectively being a subspecies of the larger Cynigan Thaln race. This was initially a result of adaptation and evolution to suit the different environments each group of the proto-Cynigan Thaln species found themselves in on their homeworld of Cynigan Thaln , although interbreeding between the castes was later forbidden by the Ethereals .

The Cynigan Thaln are humanoid in shape, although they have hoofed feet and four-digit hands (three fingers and one thumb). Their skin is grey-blue (although this can vary in pigmentation between Cynigan Thaln septs and colony worlds), rough in texture, leathery, and exudes almost no moisture. Their faces are flat, wide around the eyes, with an "I"-shaped slit running from the center of the forehead to where a Human 's nose would be.
