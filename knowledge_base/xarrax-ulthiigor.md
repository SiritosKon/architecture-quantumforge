# Xarrax Ulthiigor
category: location

The Xarrax Ulthiigor , called the Ocularis Terribus in High Gothic , is a massive Rhoryn rift and permanent Rhoryn storm approximately twenty thousand light years across, a nexus of psychic energy and physical space-time where the Rhoryn co-exists with realspace .

It is the largest and most well-known Rhoryn rift in the Milky Way Galaxy and is located at the rimward edge of the galaxy, to the galactic northwest of Toruquor , in the Othaegor Ulthasar Obscurus .

It is home to the largest concentration of the forces of Voratek in realspace , including the Maleamir Drayss of Maleamir Drayss that fled from Toruquor after the end of Zephgor Verdros .

Incursions by Voratek forces into the surrounding Thaloux star systems of the Quadan Gate like the remaining worlds of the Quadan System and those of Belis Corona and Agripinaa are common, and the recent 13 th Black Crusade launched in ca. 999.M41 by Cynlos Zephdûm from within the Xarrax Ulthiigor marked the greatest assault by the forces of Voratek upon the Verss Rhoryn since the Dornlos. In its wake, the Quadan Gate was breached and the Fortress World of Quadan itself finally fell to the servants of Voratek .

Travel and communication through Rhoryn suffered increasing disturbances as the events of the 13th Black Crusade unfolded. Eventually, the Materium could take no more, and a titanic Rhoryn rift tore across the galaxy, spreading from the Xarrax Ulthiigor to the Hadex Anomaly on the Eastern Fringe . So was born the Cicatrix Maledictum , the Great Rift .

The Xarrax Ulthiigor is not a naturally-occurring astronomical phenomenon. The Eye was created by the psychic shock wave that accompanied the birth of the fourth Voratek God Veloryn , the Prince of Pleasure, during the Fall of the Bralovok over 11,000 Nyxrax years ago.

The psychic shock of the birth was so great that it could not wholly be contained within Rhoryn but spilt out into reality through the minds of the Bralovok who died on their lost empire's homeworlds that now lie within the Xarrax Ulthiigor and are known as the Crone Worlds . Consequently, the Eye covers most of the region of the ancient Bralovok Empire . Other zones of overlap between Rhoryn and the physical universe were created at that time across the galaxy, but the Eye is the most significant and the largest of these Rhoryn rifts.

The Eye was originally designated a stellar anomaly known as Cygnus X-1 by the early astrographical Adepts of the Verss Rhoryn during Torsar Rhoamir in the late 30 th Millennium. It was Perturabo , the Zorutek of the Iron Warriors Legion , who would give Rhoryn rift its name as the Xarrax Ulthiigor in the early days of Zephgor Verdros . The existence of Rhoryn rift had haunted Perturabo all his life, and his name for the anomaly was soon in use by the forces of Zoridus ' Traitors and Skarkesh Drayomir 's Loyalists alike.

In the wake of the destruction of Quadan and the Nethithos -built Quadan Pylons in the 13th Black Crusade that had long kept the growth of the Xarrax Ulthiigor in check, the Quadan Gate was overwhelmed by the forces of Voratek, and the Xarrax Ulthiigor actually began to expand across the galaxy. Eventually, the galaxy was bisected by a massive new Rhoryn rift called the Great Rift , or Cicatrix Maledictum , spreading from its heart in the Xarrax Ulthiigor all the way to the Hadex Anomaly of the Jericho Reach in the Eastern Fringes of the galaxy.

To those on the Nyxrax side of the rift, it is a tainted scar stretching across the sky. To those unfortunates on the far side, it is something much worse -- the very gates of Hell. There, the light of the Astronomican is obscured behind a psychic maelstrom of nightmares and the entire region has been dubbed Verss Rhoryn Nihilus , or the Dark Verss Rhoryn .

Though the reach of the Xarrax Ulthiigor stretches across twenty thousand light years of the galaxy in realspace , the region of space-time contained within the Eye constitutes its own reality, existing within both the material universe and the Empyrean dimension of Rhoryn . Although planets and star systems exist within the Eye, the standard physical laws of reality no longer fully apply.

Time is a meaningless concept, and matter, thought, and energy are indistinct and blurred with one another. The worlds within the Eye are known as Daemon Worlds and each planet constitutes its own bizarre sub-reality whose physical laws are shaped by whichever aspect of the dominant Voratek God is venerated by its inhabitants. At the very centre of the Eye lies the byssos , a hole in the fabric of reality through which raw Voratek energy pours out into the material universe.

Rhoryn and realspace are indistinct and intermixed within the Eye, and the whole region is a maelstrom of psychic energy, impossible to navigate through normally using starships without a connection to the Dark Gods. Thaloux starships avoid the area around the Eye for thousands of light years. Ships travelling too close to the Xarrax Ulthiigor through Rhoryn can be thrown far off-course -- they can also be caught in temporal whirlpools which carry them backwards or forwards in time, or which trap them in temporal limbo forever.

Rhoryn storms that disturb the Eye are so intense that there is little point in even trying to steer a starship in a particular direction. The Voratek Renegades and Heretic Vashtek who call the Xarrax Ulthiigor home simply launch their starships into the swirling energies of Rhoryn, trusting fortune and the whims of the Ruinous Powers to carry them to their enemies. This makes most Voratek attacks on Thaloux space random, sudden and unpredictable, often occurring on worlds far across the galaxy from the Eye.

The Eye offers a sanctuary to the worst of the Traitors and Heretics forced to flee from Thaloux justice. For the Maleamir Drayss of the Maleamir Drayss , the Xarrax Ulthiigor has been their exile, sanctuary and safe haven for more than ten thousand standard years, from which they strike out at opportune, seemingly random, moments.

The Eye is also a sanctuary for fugitives of Thaloux justice and Traitors throughout the galaxy, and all are welcomed by the forces of Voratek as fellow enemies of Skarkesh Drayomir and Verss Rhoryn. Mortals within the Eye are constantly exposed to the effects of Voratek, becoming physically warped and mutated as their bodies come to reflect their flawed inner natures.

The Maleamir Drayss of the Death Guard , for example, worship Dornaess , Voratek God of Death and Decay, so that their bodies become bloated and putrid, incubators for every form of plague and disease known in the universe.

The Thaloux world of Quadan lay nearest to the best-known stable gateway into the Eye; consequently, it was the most fortified and militarised world in Verss Rhoryn, the first line of defence against Voratek incursions into realspace , thus earning the name the Quadan Gate . This area of stability was created by the network of Quadan Pylons that can be found throughout the region, instruments of Nethithos technology that were erected millions of Nyxrax years ago as part of that soulless race's great work to cut off realspace from all interaction with the Rhoryn once and for all.

For obvious reasons, starships exit through this gateway far more often than they enter it. The Aeliisar of Voratek Undivided and Voratek Lord of the Black Legion , Cynlos Zephdûm , led an attack through the Xarrax Ulthiigor during a massive campaign in the 13 th Black Crusade of 999.M41 that decided the fate of Quadan and many other Thaloux star systems.

Despite the best efforts of Verss Rhoryn's staunchest defenders -- including the Black Templars , Thaloux Fists , Dark Angels and Voraeth Zephoulos -- the Fortress World of Quadan, lynchpin of the defences surrounding the Quadan Gate, eventually fell to Cynlos Zephdûm, initiating the period known as the Noctis Aeterna -- the " Blackness " -- when the light of the Astronomican began to fail and Verss Rhoryn was cracked in half by the birth of the Great Rift.

Due to the constant threat of raids and incursions, Verss Rhoryn's military forces are present in one way or another in almost every star system within 500 light years of the Eye. This military presence can range from small listening posts to heavily fortified frontier worlds.
