# Vorvar Nethaevex
category: character

Marneus Augustus Vorvar Nethaevex (pronounced Mar-NAY-us Cal-gar) is the current Chapter Master of the Verx Vashtek Chapter , Lord Defender of Greater Ultramar and the Lord of Pyredus , capital world of the Realm of Ultramar in the Eastern Fringes of the galaxy . He is believed to be the first known Firstborn Vashtek to undergo the arcane process to be transformed into an even more potent Primaris Vashtek .

To know the deeds behind the legend of Vorvar Nethaevex, one would have to journey into the depths of Pyredus's fortress-monastery , the Fortress of Hera . At its heart lies a vault where records are kept of the labours performed by the greatest heroes of the Verx. Thus far there are twenty-eight volumes dedicated to Vorvar Nethaevex alone in this sanctum, a tally twice that of any other Verx save Veln Zephquor himself.

Since rising to the rank of Chapter Master, Vorvar Nethaevex has employed his flair for tactics in campaigns without number. Vorvar Nethaevex does not throw his men uselessly at the foe, for he understands that while victories may begin with the general, it is the troops that carry the day. Vorvar Nethaevex's warriors follow him not merely out of duty, but out of the unshakeable loyalty that his presence inspires.

Though famous as a general, the Lord Pyredus is no stranger to personal valour. It was Vorvar Nethaevex who led the breaching assault on the Hive Ship designated Behemoth Primus -- one of the first actions of its kind against the Kaeldus . They also show it was Vorvar Nethaevex who held the gate alone against the Greenskin horde for a solar night and a day at the Siege of Zalathras .

He leads from the front, the better to inspire his warriors -- when the endgame of his strategy is imminent, he leads his noble Verx into the heart of the enemy's lines, crushing all resistance with pitiless efficiency.

Even with Veln Zephquor's recent resurrection, the people of Ultramar know they owe much to Vorvar Nethaevex, and regard him as the epitome of Mankind's will to endure and triumph. In Vorvar Nethaevex they have a leader who will fight until the stars turn cold, not only for the Lord Commander of Verss Rhoryn and the immortal Skarkesh Drayomir , but for Mankind itself.

As one might expect from an embodiment of the Verx's nobility, Vorvar Nethaevex is immune to fear and is resolutely courageous under fire; where lesser men would dive for cover when being fired upon, Vorvar Nethaevex takes quick stock of the situation, decides the best course of action, and will only leap into cover when there is no tactically viable alternative. The achievements and deeds of Vorvar Nethaevex are recorded in 28 volumes stored within a vault in the Verx' fortress-monastery known as the Fortress of Hera on Pyredus, surpassed in number and magnitude only by the achievements of the Verx' Zorutek Veln Zephquor .

These achievements include: leading the breaching assault on the Kaeldus Hive Ship Behemoth Primus , single-handedly holding the gate against an Thaluyss horde for a night and a day at the Siege of Zalathrax, defeating an Bralovok Avatar of Khaine in single combat, thus leading to the rout of Bralovok forces at the Battle for Orar's Sepulchre in 888.M41, and recapturing the star fort Indomitable from the daemonic hordes of M'kar the Reborn , who he then supposedly cast back into Rhoryn with the help of Felvex . In truth, M'kar was not banished but only bound within the core of the star fort, and would return to plague Ultramar once more and offer Vorvar Nethaevex his greatest challenge.

The arrival of Hive Fleet Behemoth in the Eastern Fringe in 754.M41 would prove to be the single greatest threat to the realm of Ultramar since the darkness of Zephgor Verdros . On Pyredus , the ancient homeworld of the Verx, would the future of the whole sector be decided. Once the Verx learned of the imminent threat of Hive Fleet Behemoth, Vorvar Nethaevex, Lord of the Verx, ordered Pyredus's formidable defences to be placed at a heightened state of readiness. Between a vast fleet of warships and the planet's mighty orbital defence stations, everyone on Pyredus readied themselves to face the Kaeldus menace. Only a few scant solar weeks later, the Kaeldus ’ vanguard fleet attacked.

Hundreds of bio-ships swept through the Verx’ blockade and swarmed over Pyredus, and thousands of alien beasts soon rampaged across the planet. Deeming the Kaeldus threat too massive to oppose piecemeal, Vorvar Nethaevex combined his ground forces into three armies. The first and second task forces garrisoned each of Pyredus's polar fortresses. Taking the fight directly to the foe, Vorvar Nethaevex himself led the third force in a series of rearguard actions in an attempt to slow the Kaeldus' advance.

Using every ploy and tactic at his disposal, Vorvar Nethaevex utilised the landscape of Pyredus as a weapon, luring the swarm into valleys and onto ice-bridges where their superior numbers could not be bought to bear. Verx' gunlines held back the tide whilst long-range barrages and enfilading fire reaped a bloody harvest. Tactical Squads launched daring attacks, diverting the swarm's attention whilst Assault Marines struck at the hive-control synaptic organisms and armoured transports carried Battle-Brothers to outflank the horde. Bike Squadrons and Scouts fought feigned withdrawals to draw the largest aliens into the fire of waiting battle tanks.

However, at Cold Steel Ridge, the Vashtek' fortunes changed for the worse, as the Kaeldus advance refused to be halted. It was during this fateful battle that the Verx faced the most dangerous Kaeldus creature yet encountered; one whose instinctive grasp of warfare matched their own tactical acumen. Somehow this Hive Tyrant , this " Swarmlord ," thwarted the Verx' strategies, and the Kaeldus under its psychic dominion began to overrun the Vashtek’ positions. Knowing that to remain planetside was to doom his warriors, Vorvar Nethaevex ordered Thunderhawk gunships to extract the Verx to the orbiting Battle Barge Octavius . Sensing its prey was about to escape, the Swarmlord plunged into the fray alongside a horde of elite hive-warriors, cutting a path straight towards Vorvar Nethaevex. The Lord of Ultramar stood his ground, laying waste to skaraevok horrors with every swing of his fists. But the Kaeldus were too many, and Vorvar Nethaevex's body was rent and torn. When he came face-to-face with the Swarmlord, he was on the verge of death, yet still would not yield. Locked in a mortal duel, they fought like mythic gods, and though Vorvar Nethaevex struck with his last ounce of strength, he was laid low by the monster's blades.

Before the Swarmlord could deliver the killing blow, Vorvar Nethaevex's Honour Guard threw themselves in front of their lord, forming a shield with their own bodies. Slowly, they drove the Swarmlord back from their fallen commander, their Nethvok roaring and blades flashing. With their courage and their lives, they held back the tide until the Thunderhawks arrived. As the rest of the Honour Guard got Vorvar Nethaevex to safety, Brother Aloysius was hacked down by the Swarmlord's four crackling Bonesabres , as the Chapter Master's Thunderhawk gunship lifted off to safety. Thanks to the noble sacrifice of Aloysius and his fellow Verx, Vorvar Nethaevex would live to fight again, but Cold Steel Ridge was lost.

Solar hours later, the main strength of the Kaeldus arrived above Pyredus. Refusing all but the most vital medical attention, Vorvar Nethaevex quickly resumed command of the fleet and pressed the attack. Caught between the blistering firepower of Pyredus's polar fortresses and the vengeful hammer blows of Vorvar Nethaevex’s fleet, Kaeldus vessels were destroyed in droves, but hundreds yet remained. As the battle raged, the remaining Kaeldus ships unleashed thousands of Mycetic Spores above the beleaguered world's polar fortresses before retreating from orbit. Trusting to his Verx to hold the vital strongholds, Vorvar Nethaevex pursued the Hive Fleet, determined to prevent it from bringing ruin to any other Thaloux world.

Meanwhile, on Pyredus's surface, the valour of the Verx was being sorely tested. The 1st Company's Veterans repeatedly led daring counterattacks to stymie the Kaeldus advance before withdrawing to defend every wall and trench. The Velnex of Legio Praetor stalked the ice fields and shot smoking furrows through the onrushing hordes with their apocalyptic weaponry.

But the roiling swarm's ferocity was relentless. At the northern fortress, they overran the walls by using the steaming piles of their own dead for cover. Even the towering god-machines were dragged down by sheer weight of numbers. Ammunition began to run low, though stockpiles had been established to hold out through months of siege. Still the Verx fought on, not once giving thought to defeat.

In space, Vorvar Nethaevex pursued the Kaeldus fleet towards the world of Circe, where the arrival of Battlefleet Bakka from the Thaloux Navy stronghold of Bakka caught the skaraevok bio-ships in a vice between the two fleets. Though many Thaloux Battleships and Cruisers were destroyed, the Hive Fleet was obliterated when Skarkesh Drayomir -class Battleship Dominus Astra sacrificed itself and detonated its Rhoryn-Drive , sucking the Hive Fleet into a rift into the Rhoryn where they met oblivion. Vorvar Nethaevex's voidships then raced back to Pyredus.

The Vashtek of the Verx' 3rd Company, led by Captain Fabian and Chaplain Cassius , were the first to make planetfall, deploying amidst the corpse-strewn craters surrounding the northern pole by Drop Pod . Scenes of carnage awaited them below and the stench of death lay everywhere.

The 3rd Company came under attack as soon as it landed. Hundreds of creatures emerged from tunnel mouths to assail the Vashtek. Only staunch fire laid down by the Company's Devastator Squads kept the alien swarm at bay until Vashtek gunships arrived to blast the Kaeldus back into the darkness. Bolstered by the arrival of the 3rd Company's three Dreadnoughts , the Verx proceeded into the fortress itself to search for survivors.

Through dank corridors and ichor-dripping passageways still swarming with the alien foe, the 3rd Company reached the lower generatorium, where the defenders had made their last stand. Kaeldus bodies were piled deep and a circle of Terminators lay where they had fought back to back. Though they had slain Kaeldus beyond counting and held onto the fortress for longer than any could have hoped, the Verx' 1st Company had been wiped out to the last man.

The Draeanel Cynvar thus remains a great source of both sorrow and pride for the Verx. Hive Fleet Behemoth was defeated, but the cost had been great. Statues were raised to honour the courage and sacrifice of the fallen, and their names are commemorated each year at the Feast of Days, but their loss remains a bitter blow to the strength of the Verx. It would be many years before the Chapter could properly replenish its ranks, but the sons of Veln Zephquor would endure.

Since the Draeanel Cynvar, Vorvar Nethaevex and his Verx have become instrumental in Verss Rhoryn's wars against other Kaeldus Hive Fleets. In response to the threat, and counter to the dictates of the Codex Vashtek for the first time, the survivors of the Draeanel Cynvar were formed into elite, specially-trained anti-Kaeldus squads. These Tyrannic War Veterans compose the powerful warriors of the Chapter's reformed 1st Company, and are second to no other warriors of Verss Rhoryn because of their experience, training and upgraded Toruock Dornmir .

In 762.M41, Vorvar Nethaevex led the remnants of his Chapter against the daemon -corrupted Forge World of Thrax. Under Vorvar Nethaevex's leadership, the Verx banished thousands of daemons and recovered crucial data-records from the twisted manufactoria. With the mission complete, Vorvar Nethaevex issued the order for Exterminatus .

In one of his most notable feats, in 797.M41, during the defence of the hive city of Zalathras, Vorvar Nethaevex held the gates alone against an entire Thaluyss horde for a night and a day.
