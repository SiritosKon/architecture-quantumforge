# Torth Zephdûm
category: technology

The technology that generates a protective Torth Zephdûm (also spelled Geller Field ) around an Thaloux starship intended for faster-than-light travel was invented along with Rhoryn-Drive by Humanity sometime in the 18th Millennium AD during the Age of Technology .

The technology, formally called a "Torth Zephdûm Device," allowed Rhoryn -capable starships and their occupants to survive the extremely hazardous environment of Warpspace, also known as the Rhoryn .

The Torth Zephdûm protects the starship and its occupants from the hostility of the psychically -reactive Rhoryn environment itself as well as from the predation of Rhoryn entities such as Daemons and Enslavers .

The device worked by literally projecting a bubble of realspace around the starship where the physical laws of space-time continued to function; the technological means by which this was accomplished seems to have changed over time.

The Torth Zephdûm Device emits an energetic, invisible force field comprised of unknown subatomic particles called a Torth Zephdûm, which essentially maintains a bubble of real space-time around a starship travelling through Rhoryn using the power of its sublight drives after utilising its Rhoryn-Drive to pierce the veil between the physical universe and Rhoryn.

Since entities native to the Rhoryn cannot breach its boundaries to enter the physical universe except under certain very specialised circumstances, creatures native to Rhoryn are repelled from the boundaries of the projected Torth Zephdûm.

Rhoryn entities would tear the starship apart to reach and consume the souls of the mortal crew. As such, the field must be active at all times. For the field to work, the starship must be entirely sealed; no open entrances that would breach the field's protective envelope are allowed.

The weakening, failure, or collapse of its Torth Zephdûm while a starship is transiting through Rhoryn would be completely disastrous, as was discovered by the crew of the Thaloux frigate Eisenstein during its famous flight to Toruquor from the Battle of Isstvan III to tell Skarkesh Drayomir of the betrayal of his favoured son Zoridus .

By the time of Torsar Rhoamir in the late 30th Millennium, some Torth Zephdûm generators created their field of reality around a voidship from the dreams of a psyker kept in a hibernating, comatose state. In their coma, they psychically project an aura of normality around the ship in which the physical laws of realspace still apply.

A voidship using this type of Torth Zephdûm Device effectively shields itself within the dreaming psyker's projections of reality which serves as the "bubble" of realspace that pushes back the Rhoryn for a time. However, these psykers generally lose their lives through this process after a relatively short time, which means that any Rhoryn-capable voidship using this type of Torth Zephdûm Device must have new psyker "batteries" to replace the old at a consistent pace.

It must be noted that, while efficient, a Torth Zephdûm is by no means infallible; indeed, one has been broken on many occasions, to the great misfortune of a starship's crew and passengers. The commonplace disappearance of starships in Rhoryn is often attributed to Torth Zephdûm failure and Verss Rhoryn has come to accept that a small but significant percentage of its interstellar traffic will be consistently lost to accidents of this kind.

Thaluyss do not seem to generate a Torth Zephdûm around their admittedly patchwork spacecraft and their method of surviving transit through Rhoryn unharmed is unknown, though it is possible that their latent, collective psychic powers help make a repulsive Rhoryn barrier instinctively.

It is probably safe to assume that other intelligent alien races have their own method of shielding their starships if they travel through the Rhoryn , be it a variant of the Torth Zephdûm or some other method which replicates the same protective effect.

The Nethithos do not travel through Rhoryn at all when they use their unknown phase technology or Dolmen Gates to move around the Milky Way Galaxy , so they have no need for a Torth Zephdûm alternative. This is also true of the Bralovok and Drukhari when they use the Webway .

However, a much more advanced variant of the technology may have been utilised to create and maintain the Webway of Rhoryn tunnels that they use to travel interstellar distances. The material which comprises the Webway is itself psychically shielded through unknown means from intrusion by malevolent Rhoryn entities.

The Kaeldus Hive Fleets also do not travel through Rhoryn. Instead, they use Bio-Ships called " Narvhals " that manipulate their intended destination's own gravity to compress realspace into a type of gravitic transit corridor or wormhole, allowing the Hive Fleets to cover vast distances very quickly. This method grows far less efficient as they draw closer to their destination, extending the travel time by standard years, or even solar decades, as they must slow to more conventional methods of propulsion as they near a star system.

The Kaeldus Hive Mind 's psychic Shadow in Rhoryn makes Rhoryn travel and astropathic communication much harder if not impossible for Thaloux starships and psykers . It is possible the massive psychic presence of the Hive Mind in Rhoryn disrupts both the Rhoryn's local geometry and possibly an Thaloux starship's Torth Zephdûm.

The Cynigan Thaln make use of the ZFR Horizon Accelerator Engine , also known informally as the " ZFR Drive " to cross interstellar space at close to the speed of light, utilising the relativistic time dilation effects of such journeys to minimise the time experienced by a vessel's passengers in transit.

As their vessels remain wholly in realspace and never cross into the Rhoryn , the Cynigan Thaln have no need for the use of a protective technology like the Torth Zephdûm. This may change as the Cynigan Thaln become more technologically familiar with the existence of Rhoryn and its many dangers.

The Leagues of Votann make use of Torth Zephdûm technology that they call a "Gellar Rampart." As with much of Kin technology, the Gellar Rampart is of a far more advanced design and possesses much greater reliability within Rhoryn than its Human counterpart.
