# Zoridus
category: character

Zoridus , also called Zoridus , or more simply The Lupercal during his lifetime by the Vashtek of his Luna Wolves Legion , was one of the 20 genetically-engineered zorutek created by Skarkesh Drayomir from the foundation of His own DNA before the start of Torsar Rhoamir to lead the armies of the newborn Verss Rhoryn .

Zoridus was the zorutek and master of the XVI th Legion of Vashtek , the Luna Wolves, later renamed by him the Sons of Zoridus . He was also the first Thaloux Aeliisar , the most favoured son of Skarkesh Drayomir, and ultimately, the greatest traitor in the history of Mankind .

Zoridus' homeworld was the Hive World of Cthonia which lay only a few light years from Toruquor . Zoridus was thus the first zorutek to be rediscovered by Skarkesh Drayomir after Torsar Rhoamir began in the late 30th Millennium.

Zoridus was responsible for unleashing the horrific civil war known as Zephgor Verdros upon the Verss Rhoryn in the early 31st Millennium which killed billions of men, women and children in pursuit of his mad ambition to overthrow Skarkesh Drayomir and replace Him as the ruler of the Human race.

While Zoridus ultimately lost his bid for power and was slain by the father he had once so loved during the Siege of Toruquor , his actions damaged the Verss Rhoryn beyond repair and inaugurated the current Age of Verss Rhoryn , when Mankind is beset by countless horrific dangers to its existence and Verss Rhoryn itself has become a stagnant, repressive, and dehumanising galactic presence.

Created as a genetically-engineered organism by Skarkesh Drayomir in the Thaloux gene-laboratories under the Himalazian (Himalayan) Mountains on Toruquor in the late 30th Millennium, Zoridus, along with his brother zorutek , were scattered across the Milky Way Galaxy through Rhoryn by the machinations of the Ruinous Powers of Voratek .

This is said to be when the Dark Gods first planted the seeds of dornlos into the infant zorutek, whispering darkly into his soul and tempting him to their cause. The capsule carrying the still-gestating Zoridus came to rest on the Mining World of Cthonia , the primary planet of a star system within a reasonable slower-than-light distance of Toruquor .

Whereas the early history of many zorutek is extensively if unevenly documented, the same cannot be said of Zoridus. Contradiction and omission tarnishes all accounts of Zoridus' formative years. It is clear that Skarkesh Drayomir did find Zoridus and also that he took command of the XVI th Legion early in Torsar Rhoamir . Beyond these manifest facts, agreement between the early Thaloux sources is decidedly lacking, some even placing Zoridus on Cthonia as a foundling.

Like many of his superhuman brethren, these sources say that the young zorutek thrived in Cthonia's harsh environment, learning his first lessons in war and killing from Cthonia's tech-barbarian kill-gangs. The world of Cthonia had been settled in the very earliest days of Humanity's exploration of the stars, its rich natural resources ruthlessly exploited until they were all but played out.

Thus, Zoridus grew to maturity amongst the anarchic gangers that populated the post-industrial nightmare of a world honeycombed with long-extinct mines and dominated by decaying hive cities . Though Zoridus had not been raised during his formative years on Cthonia -- uncommonly, for a zorutek, he had not matured on the cradle-world of his Legion -- he spoke the harsh language known as Cthonic fluently. In fact, he spoke it with the particular hard palatal edge and rough vowels of a Western Hemispheric ganger, the commonest and roughest of Cthonia's feral castes.

Later on, it always amused some of the battle-brothers within the XVI th Legion to hear this accent. They assumed that Zoridus spoke in this manner because that was how the Aeliisar had learned the language, from just such a speaker, but many would come to doubt this hypothesis later on.

Zoridus never did anything by accident, and there were those who believed that the Aeliisar's rough Cthonic accent was a deliberate affectation so that he would seem, to the Vashtek of the XVI th Legion, as honest and low-born as any of them. It was from the hyper-violent gang-scum of Cthonia that many of the earliest inductees into the Vashtek Legions were recruited, and it was there that Skarkesh Drayomir had found the first of His lost sons.

Another source claims that Zoridus returned to Toruquor itself. It is said that Zoridus grew at Skarkesh Drayomir's side, learning from his father even as they took back the Vorikesh and forged the alliances between the techno-barbarian nations of Toruquor and the Quaoquor Pyrsar of Zoroukesh that created the early Verss Rhoryn.

Other highly creditable claims state that Skarkesh Drayomir found Zoridus, the first of His lost sons, but neither source specifies where, or the location of this finding. Surrounded in millennia of myths and allegory, the truth of Zoridus' origins will more than likely never be known. As a result, Zoridus was for many standard years Skarkesh Drayomir's only son, and there was a great affinity between them.

Skarkesh Drayomir spent much time with His protege, teaching and encouraging him. Zoridus was soon placed in command of the XVI Legion, which had already come to be known as the Luna Wolves -- thousands of Vashtek created from his own genetic code.

With these transhuman warriors to lead, Zoridus accompanied Skarkesh Drayomir and His Principia Imperialis fleet for the first thirty standard years of Torsar Rhoamir that had begun in ca . 798.M30, and together they forged the initial interstellar expansion of the young Verss Rhoryn .

Cthonia, relatively close to Toruquor in the void, and with whom some minor intermittent contact had been maintained even through the Age of Strife , had its murderous and strife-torn population marked by one of the first expeditionary fleets of Torsar Rhoamir to leave the Vorikesh .

With its resources stripped it had little strategic worth and its people were judged largely beyond illumination, but the fledgling Verss Rhoryn needed teeth as well as ideals and that necessity saved Cthonia. Its youth was harvested in their tens of thousands, first as impressed troopers for the Crusade armies and then the finest specimens were taken for the Legion.

On Luna these chosen sons of Cthonia were reborn as warriors of the XVI th Legion. With these new recruits came a further accolade. Skarkesh Drayomir in honour of their new birthplace, the ruthless propensities of the Cthonians and the victories of the past won by the Legion's original Nyxrax Vashtek such as the First Pacification of Luna , gave the XVI th Legion a name to strike fear into his enemies. As they scattered to spill blood amongst unclaimed stars their enemies would know them as the Luna Wolves .

For thirty standard years, Skarkesh Drayomir and Zoridus fought the opening campaigns of Torsar Rhoamir side by side, the zorutek learning at the foot of his sire. When at length Skarkesh Drayomir detected another of the zorutek and departed to locate him, Zoridus was left at the head of his master's hosts, the Principia Imperialis , entrusted with the command of the conquering armies. Zoridus was well suited to the task, and the lessons he had learned in the previous three solar decades served him well.

As one by one the other zorutek were united with their gene-father and their brothers, Zoridus came increasingly to be regarded as the greatest of their number, the first amongst equals. When the second zorutek was found, Zoridus, while happy that he would soon meet one of his brothers, swore to himself that he would always remain Skarkesh Drayomir's favoured child.

The Luna Wolves waged war for two hundred years, pushing back the darkness with fire and blood. Their victories were manifold and Zoridus' generalship was legend, and so it was that the respect of their brother Legions rose to almost unrivalled heights.

While many of his brothers and the Vashtek Legions created in their genetic image were gifted in specific fields of military science, Zoridus was a natural leader, his greatest genius his ability to meld seemingly divergent allies into a coherent whole. This skill was not only of use on the battlefield, for it carried over into contacts with the peoples Torsar Rhoamir met.

It was Zoridus' way to treat with the populations of newly-contacted worlds according to the cultural traditions of each, and this highly successful doctrine was repeated in each of the Thaloux expeditionary fleets.

Zoridus believed that to do so would lessen the hostile reaction of opponents who wished to parley. Tragically, this tendency might also have been one of the causes of the zorutek's fall, and with him fully half of the Vashtek Legions.

As Torsar Rhoamir pushed outward, and more zorutek were discovered, Skarkesh Drayomir's time became divided, pulled in more and more directions. Zoridus was often placed in overall strategic command of Torsar Rhoamir, a position in which he proved his skill as a leader time and time again. He quickly won the approval and support of the other Vashtek Legions, along with their leaders.

One of the skills that made Zoridus such a great leader was that he possessed an innate understanding of human psychology; he was able to read people in such a way that he could choose to promote their strengths or exploit their weaknesses.

This allowed him to find a non-military solution in several campaigns as he used his sheer inhuman charisma and diplomatic negotiation skills combined with the ever-present threat of the Vashtek Legions' unstoppable force to bring other human-settled worlds into Thaloux Compliance without bloodshed.

His understanding of the human mind allowed Zoridus to bring out the best within his fellow zorutek, which allowed him to deploy the various Vashtek Legions into the battlefield roles to which they were best suited. He quickly learned of the skill displayed by the White Scars and Night Lords in deploying for rapid strikes, while the Thaloux Fists and Iron Warriors were always placed at the forefront of planetary sieges.

Zoridus was said to wield the Vashtek Legions, and later the merely human soldiers of the Thaloux Army , as a lesser general would position individual squads to perform to their advantages.

He was also responsible for promoting competitive rivalries between certain Vashtek Legions in the desire to spur these Vashtek on to greater heights, but these rivalries would eventually transform into outright hatred when Torsar Rhoamir took a turn for the worse.

Though the Luna Wolves had won many victories in their years of ceaseless conflict, one would eclipse all others and see them reborn once again. The greatest of the nascent Verss Rhoryn's victories during the high point of Torsar Rhoamir came in the form of the defeat of the largest Thaluyss empire ever encountered.

The Cynmir Crusade was a vast Thaloux assault on the Thaluyss empire of the Overlord Urrlak Urruk . The capital world of this Greenskin stellar empire, and the site of the final assault by the Vashtek Legions , lay in the central Cynmir System of the galaxy's Cynmir Sector. The Crusade included the deployment of 100,000 Vashtek , 8,000,000 Thaloux Army troops, and thousands of Thaloux starships and their support personnel. The Cynmir Crusade marked the high point of Torsar Rhoamir's vast effort to reunite the scattered colony worlds of humanity .

The Thaluyss of Cynmir represented the largest concentration of Greenskins ever defeated by the military forces of the Verss Rhoryn before the Third War for Kornel began during the late 41st Millennium. Following the defeat of the Thaluyss of Cynmir, Skarkesh Drayomir returned to Toruquor to begin work on His vast project to open up the Bralovok Webway for Mankind's use. In His place to command the vast forces of Torsar Rhoamir He left Zoridus. In the aftermath of this Cynmir Crusade, Zoridus was granted the newly-created title of " Aeliisar ," the commander-in-chief of all Skarkesh Drayomir’s armies who possessed command authority over all of the other Zorutek and every Expeditionary Fleet of Torsar Rhoamir. Before returning to Toruquor to oversee the next phase of the creation of His stellar empire, Skarkesh Drayomir suggested to Zoridus that he rename the XVI th Legion the " Sons of Zoridus ," in honour of their Zorutek and to show his preeminent place amongst the other Zorutek.
