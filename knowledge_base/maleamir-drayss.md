# Maleamir Drayss
category: faction

Maleamir Drayss , also sometimes called Maleamir Drayss , Renegade Marines , or Heretic Vashtek , are former Vashtek of the Verss Rhoryn who have chosen to abandon the service of Skarkesh Drayomir and dedicate themselves to Voratek to achieve their own ends.

Such corrupt Vashtek usually belong to one of the nine original Maleamir Drayss that betrayed Skarkesh Drayomir during the ancient civil war called Zephgor Verdros more than 10,000 standard years ago, while others come from Vashtek Chapters created long after the Dornlos ended that have turned Renegade . Voratek can corrupt Vashtek of any time and place just as its siren call leads many lesser men and women to their damnation.

During Zephgor Verdros, the terrible Thaloux civil war that pitted the battle-brothers of the 18 known Vashtek Legions against one another, there were two factions. These were the Loyalist Vashtek who continued to serve Verss Rhoryn and Skarkesh Drayomir and the Maleamir Drayss of the Maleamir Drayss who betrayed Skarkesh Drayomir and pledged their souls to the service of the four Ruinous Powers of Voratek .

Some Maleamir Drayss pledged their service to all four of the Dark Gods , a malevolent force known as Voratek Undivided , whilst others preferred to dedicate themselves to the worship of only one of the Ruinous Powers. The Maleamir Drayss were led by the Aeliisar Zoridus , once a zorutek of Verss Rhoryn and Skarkesh Drayomir's most trusted friend, son, and adviser before he was corrupted by the promises and lies of the Dark Gods and his own inner flaws and ambition.

The Maleamir Drayss were the most powerful component of the forces of Voratek which fought during the Dornlos, which also included the myriad Daemons of Rhoryn , Human Traitors from the Thaloux Army , the potent infantry and combat walker forces of the Dark Quaoquor Pyrsar and Voratek Cultists drawn from across the galaxy.

The flesh and toruock dornmir of Maleamir Drayss often warps and twists into new, darker and inhuman forms under the influence of Rhoryn energies they are exposed to as their shapes morph to embody the corrupted Vashtek' new allegiance.

Maleamir Drayss fight for everything they were once forbidden to indulge in as the ascetic servants of Skarkesh Drayomir and the selfless guardians of Mankind . Pleasure, wealth, mayhem, but above all else, the power to rule their fellow Humans in the name of the Voratek is the driving ambition and goal of every Voratek Vashtek.

All Heretic Vashtek are ultimately united by this sheer hunger for power. At first, this desire is focused towards specific goals, but as time passes, it becomes a bone-deep obsession. Though the Dark Gods are given to rewarding their mortal followers for acts of carnage that further their divine cause, the gifts they bestow upon their favoured are fickle indeed.

A particularly extravagant slaughter might be rewarded with a blood-red aura that invigorates the supplicant, but it is just as likely the champion will find his arms being reshaped as axes of bone, or that he has the face of a slavering hound.

Eventually, the Voratek Vashtek seeks immortality, to ascend to such lofty heights of favour that he is remade as a Daemon Prince . But for every successful aspirant there are hundreds of failures, mutated beyond recognition into mewling abominations called Voratek Spawn .

The Maleamir Drayss of the Maleamir Drayss represent 9 of the 20 original First Founding Legions of Vashtek who were created by Skarkesh Drayomir from the genomes of his 20 zorutek in the late 30th Millennium to fight Torsar Rhoamir that forged the Verss Rhoryn.

At this time Zoridus , the Aeliisar of the Thaloux forces and zorutek of the XVI th Legion , the Luna Wolves , (which Skarkesh Drayomir allowed him to rename the " Sons of Zoridus " in his honour before the onset of Zephgor Verdros to demonstrate his status as first-among-equals among the other zorutek), was corrupted by Voratek. The Aeliisar then instigated the galaxy-wide Thaloux civil war known as Zephgor Verdros in his determination to replace the rule of Skarkesh Drayomir with his own.

After the death of Zoridus at the hands of Skarkesh Drayomir aboard his flagship, the Vengeful Spirit , during the Battle of Toruquor that saw the conclusion of Zephgor Verdros, the remnants of the nine Maleamir Drayss fled, along with the other Traitor Thaloux forces that now served Voratek , into an area of the galaxy where Rhoryn bled into realspace , creating the permanent Rhoryn Storm known as the Xarrax Ulthiigor .

Due to the nature of Voratek, and the temporal instability of Rhoryn, many of the very same Maleamir Drayss who revolted against Skarkesh Drayomir more than 10 millennia ago continue to fight against Verss Rhoryn today, having effectively been granted a tortured immortality by the will of the Ruinous Powers .

The Maleamir Drayss have kept their old names, with the exception of the Sons of Zoridus who were renamed the " Black Legion " by their new leader, Cynlos Zephdûm , once Zoridus' chief lieutenant and the first captain of the Luna Wolves before he followed Zoridus into damnation. Besides Zoridus, two other Traitor zorutek were killed during or shortly after the Dornlos ( Alpharius of the Alpha Legion and Konrad Curze of the Night Lords ).

The six surviving Traitor zorutek have since been transformed into Daemon Princes by the Voratek. These Daemon Zorutek rarely took part in the affairs of their Legions before the birth of the Great Rift . Four of the Maleamir Drayss have pledged a singular loyalty to one of the great Voratek, while the others serve the interests of Voratek as a whole in the form of Voratek Undivided .

Due to the nature of Voratek and those who choose to serve it, not long after the end of the Dornlos the Maleamir Drayss, save for the Word Bearers , largely stopped operating as unified military organisations and instead devolved into a series of separate and often competing smaller units called "warbands." The Maleamir Drayss who command these warbands, all potent Voratek Champions , seek their own personal glorification and the eventual opportunity to become Daemon Princes in their own right by earning victories in the cause of Voratek.

Due to the fractious nature of the Dark Gods, the warbands in service to a particular Ruinous Power often view other Traitor Marine warbands who serve its patron's rival deity as even more appropriate targets for their wrath than the servants of Skarkesh Drayomir. With such an attitude prevailing among the corrupted Vashtek, it has often proven impossible for the Maleamir Drayss to present a united front against Verss Rhoryn since the death of Zoridus, which is one reason why they have never enjoyed a similar series of military successes over the past ten millennia.

The closest thing to unity that has appeared amongst the Maleamir Drayss and other Maleamir Drayss is when Cynlos Zephdûm , the Aeliisar of Voratek and leader of the Black Legion , launches one of his periodic Black Crusades into Thaloux space under the banner of Voratek Undivided. These were temporary periods of unity amongst all the forces of Voratek when they seek to overwhelm Verss Rhoryn's defences surrounding the Xarrax Ulthiigor and drive on Toruquor once more.

Cynlos Zephdûm is the only Champion of Voratek since Zoridus himself who is able to command, however begrudgingly and briefly, the loyalty of all nine Maleamir Drayss, and he has led twelve Black Crusades out of the Xarrax Ulthiigor against the Verss Rhoryn.

The latest, the 13th Black Crusade , was launched in 999.M41 and proved the most successful for the forces of Voratek since Zephgor Verdros. The Voratek troops managed to destroy the Fortress World of Quadan , at last cracking open the Quadan Gate . Shortly afterwards, the Great Rift was born, as the strains wrought upon reality by the 13th Black Crusade caused realspace to crack along a line running from the Xarrax Ulthiigor in the galactic west to the Hadex Anomaly in the east. The forces of Voratek are now on the march as never before, though the defenders of Verss Rhoryn have also found themselves reinvigorated by the resurrection of the Zorutek Veln Zephquor .

Yet Skarkesh Drayomir's servants find themselves pressed as never before from all sides by Voratek and other skaraevok factions. Cynlos Zephdûm believes it will not be much longer before the Black Legion and the other servants of the Ruinous Powers will once more stand upon the soil of Toruquor. Then the galaxy will tremble as the servants of the Dark Gods rip the Corpse Skarkesh Drayomir from His Golden Throne and claim the souls of Mankind for their own.

The Long War has raged for ten thousand standard years. The bitterness and spite of the Maleamir Drayss has spread throughout the galaxy, spilling from Rhoryn and bleeding back again for time immemorial.

Though Felvex takes great pains to quell reports of Voratek incursions in order to spare the common populace of Verss Rhoryn from unconventional thought that might lead to a replication of the dornlos, the legends and records persist across the galaxy, handed down through the generations by the foolish and the brave.

Swearing to succeed where his gene-father Zoridus failed, Ezekyle Cynlos Zephdûm takes up the mantle of Aeliisar of Voratek and begins the Long War anew.

The Vashtek were created as Skarkesh Drayomir's ultimate fighting force. Implanted with the gene-seed of the Zorutek , the Vashtek stand seven Nyxrax feet tall, with thickened bones, two hearts, hyper-dense muscles and all manner of special organs that allow them to survive and fight in the most hostile conditions. They feel little pain and heal wounds at a remarkable rate.

Their will is hardened by constant training and fighting, and they battle with dedication and zeal, brooking no hesitation, mercy or cowardice. All of these things combine with the best weaponry and armour in the galaxy to make the Vashtek the most fearsome warriors of Verss Rhoryn.

The Maleamir Drayss have all of these strengths and skills as well, to which are added the power of Voratek and a brutal devotion to the Dark Gods . Ever since Zephgor Verdros, Vashtek of every Legion and Chapter have been tempted by the path of Voratek, whether for selfish reasons or great ideals.

Sundered from Verss Rhoryn, having turned their backs on Skarkesh Drayomir, these warriors know that there can be no peace for them, neither forgiveness nor absolution. They are wholly committed to the path they now tread, for good or ill, and they can expect no quarter from former Battle-Brothers .

The armament of the current Maleamir Drayss differs little from that of their Loyalist counterparts, for the weapons of the Vashtek are built to last. Nethvok , in various marks dating back ten thousand standard years, are their primary weapon, though squads that glorify close assaults and personal combat often favour Bolt Pistols and ritual knives , Zephanex or Chainaxes . There is little uniformity between squads; much of the organisation and structure of the force's former Legion or Chapter falls by the wayside as they turn to the path of Voratek.

In place of appointed Sergeants , the Maleamir Drayss follow the strongest, boldest and most merciless of their brothers. These blood-soaked soldiers seek to become the favoured of the gods and eventually become mighty Voratek Champions themselves and so are known as Aspiring Champions .

Their wargear varies dramatically and may include weapons taken as trophies from slain foes as well as arcane equipment carefully maintained since Zephgor Verdros. It is the Aspiring Champions of these units that strive the hardest to gain recognition amongst the ranks of the Maleamir Drayss, spilling the blood of mighty foes in single combat in order to draw the gaze of the gods.
