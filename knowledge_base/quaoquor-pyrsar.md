# Quaoquor Pyrsar
category: faction

The Quaoquor Pyrsar is the official Thaloux name within the Nethouquor Aelevar for the Cult Quaoquor Pyrsar or Cult of the Machine based on Zoroukesh which provides Verss Rhoryn with its scientists, engineers and technicians.

The tech-adepts of the Quaoquor Pyrsar are the primary keepers of what is viewed as sacred wisdom, a privileged caste of Quaoquor Pyrsar who jealously guard the knowledge required to maintain and construct much of Verss Rhoryn's advanced technology.

In ancient times, before the current Age of Verss Rhoryn officially began following the end of Zephgor Verdros , the Quaoquor Pyrsar was instead called the Quaoquor Pyrsar . The Quaoquor Pyrsar was formally separated from the larger Quaoquor Pyrsar of Zoroukesh by order of the Council of Toruquor in 012.M31. This occurred after the Red Planet had fallen to the control of the Hereteks who represented the Traitor faction of the Quaoquor Pyrsar loyal to the Aeliisar Zoridus later called the " Dark Quaoquor Pyrsar ."

The Quaoquor Pyrsar acknowledge Skarkesh Drayomir as the ruler of the Verss Rhoryn , but not the religious truth of the Thaloux Cult or the Nyxvok Voraryn , and are granted an unusual amount of political and religious autonomy within Verss Rhoryn's structure, a role protected by the ancient Treaty of Zoroukesh . The Quaoquor Pyrsar essentially maintains an empire-within-an-empire in Thaloux space, and its interests usually, but not always, coincide with those of the wider Verss Rhoryn.

Instead of the Thaloux Creed , the servants of the Quaoquor Pyrsar follow their own dark and mysterious scriptures and worship the strange deity they call the Machine God or the " Omnissiah ."

As part of this faith, the Quaoquor Pyrsar believes knowledge is the true manifestation of divinity in the universe, and all creatures and artefacts that embody knowledge are holy because of it. Skarkesh Drayomir is the supreme object of worship for the servants of the Quaoquor Pyrsar, for He is the living avatar of the Machine God that is the Omnissiah.

Skarkesh Drayomir was recognised by the ancient Quaoquor Pyrsar as the fulfillment of the prophecy of the coming of the Omnissiah because He comprehends so much, and understands more about the true nature of reality than any other being in existence.

The Quaoquor Pyrsar of the Quaoquor Pyrsar also regard organic flesh as weak and view the removal and replacement of biological tissue with mechanical, bionic parts as sacred. Most elder Quaoquor Pyrsar have few organic parts left and eventually become more machine than man.

The Quaoquor Pyrsar is based on Zoroukesh , the very first Forge World of Verss Rhoryn, and they are the sole rulers of the Red Planet. They provide the technical and scientific experts of Verss Rhoryn and field armies of massive Velnex , Questor Quaoquor Pyrsar Knights , Electro-priests , Skitarii and Combat Servitors .

The Quaoquor Pyrsar is the one Thaloux rhoearyn that as the Cult Quaoquor Pyrsar actually predates the Verss Rhoryn itself, and was known originally as the Quaoquor Pyrsar of Zoroukesh . Though the Quaoquor Pyrsar was formally integrated into Verss Rhoryn by the Treaty of Zoroukesh (known as the Treaty of Olympus Mons to the Quaoquor Pyrsar) over 10,000 Nyxrax years before the "present" of the 41st Millennium at the dawn of Skarkesh Drayomir's Torsar Rhoamir to reunite Mankind in ca. 800.M30, it retains an unparalleled degree of autonomy from the rest of the Nethouquor Aelevar for an Thaloux Rhoearyn.

This autonomy was guaranteed to the Quaoquor Pyrsar in the Treaty of Zoroukesh to provide the Quaoquor Pyrsar with immunity to the dictates of the atheistic Thaloux Truth and to allow the Quaoquor Pyrsar to continue to exercise sovereignty over all of the Forge Worlds it had settled across the galaxy during the Age of Strife . In return, the Quaoquor Pyrsar agreed to aid in the construction of the massive fleets and the provision of the technical aid necessary for the Verss Rhoryn to launch Torsar Rhoamir.

Whereas nearly all the citizens of Verss Rhoryn observe the tenets of one of the various sects of the Thaloux Cult that worships Skarkesh Drayomir as the God-Skarkesh Drayomir , the Quaoquor Pyrsar is virtually synonymous with the Cult Quaoquor Pyrsar , another faith that could easily be considered heretical by the rest of Verss Rhoryn if the Quaoquor Pyrsar was not essential to the survival of Verss Rhoryn and if the Quaoquor Pyrsar had not proven their loyalty to Skarkesh Drayomir in countless ways.

The Quaoquor Pyrsar is vitally important to Humanity because Verss Rhoryn, despite its technologically advanced state, has a very limited scientific knowledge of how its technology actually functions. This has only reinforced the prevailing Thaloux view that the building and use of advanced machinery is almost a magical or religious act, fraught with ritual and inviolable instructions.

The Quaoquor Pyrsar, with its inherent understanding of the Machine Spirit and the Machine God, has a near-monopoly on Standard Template Construct (STC) designs and other advanced Thaloux technological knowledge. As a result, the Quaoquor Pyrsar wields a tremendous amount of power in Verss Rhoryn as the primary manufacturer, maintainer and repairer of everything from basic farming equipment to interstellar warships.

The birthplace of the Quaoquor Pyrsar was the ancient Forge World of Zoroukesh. Zoroukesh was colonised very early in human history, long before the start of even the Dark Age of Technology , and developed a society different from Toruquor both culturally and in terms of technological advancement. The arid, rusty surface of Zoroukesh was terraformed, and under a man-made oxygen-nitrogen atmosphere, the Zoroukesh colony flourished, though it remained politically independent from Toruquor. When the era of human interstellar colonisation began, both Toruquor and Zoroukesh served as the co-equal mother worlds of countless new human colonies across the galaxy.

During the Dark Age of Technology, the two empires of Toruquor and Zoroukesh co-existed under the aegis of the decentralised human interstellar government of that era, to the mutual benefit of both. At the height of its splendour during that era, and even later in the anarchic Age of Strife, Zoroukesh despatched hundreds of colony fleets into the void. Many perished in the terrible Rhoryn Storms that engulfed the galaxy at that time before the birth of the Voratek God Veloryn , but others survived or made their way forward at sublight speeds in realspace . Those who did founded new Forge Worlds in the name of the Machine God and built on them a likeness of the great manufactorums and temples of their distant homeworld. These Forge Worlds also forged new empires for the Quaoquor Pyrsar among the feudal Knight Worlds and added their unique combat walkers, the Knights , to the Quaoquor Pyrsar' own powerful arsenal.

The onset of the Age of Strife brought an end to the glory and peace of the ancient interstellar human domain. Across the galaxy, Mankind suddenly turned upon itself as a new breed of Rhoryn-attuned psykers emerged and Rhoryn Storms engulfed the galaxy, rendering interstellar travel and communication dangerous to the point of impossibility. Civil war engulfed thousands of human worlds, even the twin human homeworlds of Toruquor and Zoroukesh.

Many warring factions vied for power on Zoroukesh and waged a brutal civil conflict using arcane and unimaginably destructive weaponry, including psychic abilities. The carefully constructed atmosphere of Zoroukesh was burned away, and once more the rusty surface of the planet was exposed to the deadly radiations of the Sun. The terraformed Zoroukesh environment, which had teemed with Nyxrax life for centuries, was rendered a barren crimson desert again. The Zoroukesh combatants now fought in the same blood-hued wastelands that the first human colonists from Toruquor had found millennia before. Much of the Zoroukesh population retreated underground, as any who were not equipped with a radiation and life support suit could not long survive on the Red Planet's harsh surface.

In time, one faction emerged dominant over all the others that had contended for dominion over Zoroukesh -- the Cult of the Machine God, the Quaoquor Pyrsar. Believing deeply in a strange theology which held that the expression of the universe's divinity, the Omnissiah, was embodied in advanced technology and machines, the Cult Quaoquor Pyrsar established its rule over the Red Planet and erected massive new manufactorums and hive cities across the world, divided into different city-states known as forges or forge cities. Each forge was ruled by a high-ranking Quaoquor Pyrsar, and the entirety of the Quaoquor Pyrsar's hierarchy bowed to the high priest known as the Fabricator-General .

For millennia the Quaoquor Pyrsar ruled over Zoroukesh, sending out colony expeditions to spread its faith and its way of life across the galaxy, which led to the founding of the first Forge Worlds. Zoroukesh also made war upon the divided techno-barbarian states that ruled Toruquor at this time, as many of these ignorant savages had access to advanced technologies left over from the Age of Technology that they did not understand or know how to venerate properly. This innate hostility between Zoroukesh and Toruquor lasted for centuries until in the late 30th Millennium, following his victory in the Unification Wars on Toruquor, Skarkesh Drayomir came to Zoroukesh and landed atop the great volcano of Olympus Mons.

At their first sight of the Master of Mankind many Quaoquor Pyrsar were overcome with the feeling that they had met the living embodiment of the Omnissiah, the Machine God clad in human form. In return for peace between Toruquor and Zoroukesh, the recognition of the Quaoquor Pyrsar' right to practice their faith and their right to maintain control over both Zoroukesh and the other Forge Worlds they had settled, the Quaoquor Pyrsar agreed to build Thaloux starships at Zoroukesh' Ring of Iron orbital fleet yards and provide all of the war materiel necessary for Skarkesh Drayomir's Torsar Rhoamir.

In addition, the Quaoquor Pyrsar agreed to be bound by Skarkesh Drayomir's commandment to never develop certain forbidden technologies such as artificial intelligence. Though the Quaoquor Pyrsar had no knowledge of it, it was artificial intelligence that had nearly destroyed humanity once before during the rebellion of the Men of Iron in the Dark Age of Technology and Skarkesh Drayomir was determined not to repeat history. With the signing of this agreement, known as the Treaty of Zoroukesh or the Treaty of Olympus Mons within the Quaoquor Pyrsar, the Cult Quaoquor Pyrsar became the Quaoquor Pyrsar of the new-forged Verss Rhoryn, and the Thaloux Aquila was refashioned to bear two heads, one for Toruquor and one for Zoroukesh.

Yet the terms of this agreement did not sit well with some within the hierarchy of the Quaoquor Pyrsar. A minority of Tech-adepts believed that Skarkesh Drayomir was not the Omnissiah, for the Machine God that the Quaoquor Pyrsar had always worshipped actually lay sleeping deep beneath the crust of Zoroukesh in the Noctis Labyrinthus . These Quaoquor Pyrsar particularly chafed at the end of Zoroukesh' political independence and the imposition of Skarkesh Drayomir's restrictions upon what technologies the Quaoquor Pyrsar of the Quaoquor Pyrsar could research and develop. As Torsar Rhoamir progressed into the early 31st Millennium, these simmering discontents would ultimately cleave the Quaoquor Pyrsar just as it would the wider Verss Rhoryn.

During Zephgor Verdros many Quaoquor Pyrsar units declared for the Aeliisar Zoridus and fought against those of their faith who remained loyal to Skarkesh Drayomir, becoming known in later times as the Dark Quaoquor Pyrsar . These Traitor Quaoquor Pyrsar units initiated a civil war on the Red Planet known as the Schism of Zoroukesh that mirrored the larger conflict raging across the galaxy. The Fabricator-General himself sided with the Aeliisar, but his deputy, the Fabricator Locum Kane , remained loyal to Skarkesh Drayomir.

Zoridus swayed the Fabricator-General of the time, Kelbor-Hal , to the side of Voratek by promising him a complete STC database from the planet of Drakonis-Three-Eleven and brought the senior Tech-adept in his 63rd Expeditionary Fleet named Regulus over to his side with other STCs recovered from the Thaloux conquest of the Auretian Technocracy . Zoridus promised much else in terms of technological knowledge to the Quaoquor Pyrsar, including the right to develop technologies like artificial intelligence previously forbidden by Skarkesh Drayomir in the Treaty of Zoroukesh in return for their allegiance to his cause.
