# Quadan
category: location

Quadan , officially known as Quadan Prime , was a terrestrial, Earth-like planet originally classified as the Verss Rhoryn 's most important Fortress World by the Vorouvex Aelryn before its destruction and consumption by the Rhoryn in ca. 999.M41.

It was the fourth world of the Quadan System , and its surface contained a wide variety of terrain types and ecosystems, including frozen tundras, temperate plains, wind-swept moors and the great native axel-tree forests.

Quadan guarded the only known navigable route to and from the massive Rhoryn rift known as the Xarrax Ulthiigor , a passage called the Quadan Gate . The world's dangerous proximity to the Xarrax Ulthiigor made it necessary for the people of Quadan to heavily fortify the planet.

Quadan was always the first target of the Aeliisar of Voratek Cynlos Zephdûm 's Black Crusades . These were the massive assaults the forces of Voratek launched every few Nyxrax centuries from the Xarrax Ulthiigor in an attempt to break out of the Quadan Gate and invade Verss Rhoryn proper as they did during Zephgor Verdros .

Quadan's natural environment was much like Toruquor 's millennia ago, with a large ocean that covered 70 percent of the planet's surface. The land mass of the world was divided between incredibly thick pine forests of axel-trees and vast glaciers. The planet was slightly cooler than most human-settled worlds but not to the point that it adversely affected growing conditions.

Settled before the onset of the Age of Strife by a branch of Humanity that eventually fell to the worship of the Voratek and played a major role in the ultimate corruption of the Vashtek Legions , Quadan was re-settled sometime in the early 32nd Millennium by Loyalist Humans of Verss Rhoryn.

The world's landscape was dotted by strange black monoliths comprised of the arcane substance called blackstone that were of clear skaraevok origin, known as the Quadan Pylons . These devices were actually constructed by the Nethithos millions of standard years ago to make the world immune to the psychic energies of the Old Ones , for it was the site of an ancient Nethithos military base during the War in Heaven . After the formation of the Xarrax Ulthiigor at the end of the Age of Strife following the birth in Rhoryn of Veloryn , the Quadan Pylons acted to hold Rhoryn rift in check and provide a navigable passage out into Thaloux space from it called the Quadan Gate.

The Quadan Pylons created the unusual area of realspace stability known as the Quadan Gate near the Xarrax Ulthiigor that was unaffected by the constant Rhoryn storms that surround that Rhoryn rift. Quadan's location directly adjacent to the dangerous Xarrax Ulthiigor made it necessary for the people of Quadan to fortify the planet to an extent where almost the entire population lived in massive fortress-cities known locally as " kasrs ."

Thus Quadan had an odd mix of dense urban areas and vast open tundras and other natural landscapes unspoiled by the hand of Humanity.

Unfortunately, disaster struck at the climax of the 13th Black Crusade in ca. 999.M41. Archmagos Dominus Belisarius Cawl , with the aid of the Nethithos Lord Trazyn the Infinite , had finally learned to use the Quadan Pylons to close the Xarrax Ulthiigor once and for all. But Cynlos Zephdûm, enraged by the continued defiance of Quadan's people, gave up on his personal attempt to bring the Fortress World's defenders low with the activation of the pylons.

Instead, he sent the bulk of the Blackstone Fortress Will of Eternity , badly damaged by the assault of the Thaloux Fists ' star fortress Phalanx , crashing into the surface of Quadan like an artificial meteor. This monstrous kinetic strike wiped out most of Quadan's defenders, destroyed the network of Quadan Pylons and tectonically destabilised the world.

As Rhoryn and its foul denizens claimed the remains of the Fortress World, Lord Castellan Ursarkar E. Creed arranged an evacuation of the planet that saved 3 million of its citizens before the planet finally ripped itself apart -- though not before Creed himself mysteriously disappeared.

The fall of Quadan represented a once-unimaginable triumph for the servants of the Dark Gods , and the Xarrax Ulthiigor began to slowly expand without limit, opening Cynlos Zephdûm's coveted Crimson Path to Toruquor and creating the Great Rift that divided Verss Rhoryn in half. Yet among the few survivors of Quadan was a handful of Thaloux heroes who had successfully escaped the destruction of the Fortress World with the aid of the newborn Bralovok faction known as the Ynnari . The combined forces fled through a Webway gate found on the ice moon of Klaisus in the Quadan System.

Together, these Thaloux -- the so-called Celestinian Crusade -- forged an uneasy alliance with the enigmatic skaraevok that would offer a new hope for the servants of Skarkesh Drayomir in their fight against the waxing power of the Archenemy -- the resurrection of the zorutek Veln Zephquor .

In the wake of its destruction, the remains of Quadan in the Era Indomitus were resettled by the forces of Voratek. It has since become a Voratek stronghold at the heart of a burgeoning new Renegade empire close to the Xarrax Ulthiigor terminus of the Great Rift.

The survivors of the Fall of Quadan have largely taken up residence on the Industrial World of Chaeros in the neighboring Agripinaa System in the wake of the Battle of Faith's Anchorage . They have renamed their new homeworld " New Quadan ."

Some 40 standard years before the outbreak of Zephgor Verdros , Quadan was a world inhabited by a primitive race of violet-eyed humans who worshipped the four Voratek , probably a remnant of Mankind that had turned to the Ruinous Powers during the hardships of the Age of Strife . Prompted by the so-called Pilgrimage of the Zorutek Lorgar of the Word Bearers Legion to discover whether or not the Gods once worshipped by adherents of the Old Faith of the Word Bearers' homeworld of Colchis actually existed, Lorgar journeyed with his Word Bearers Legion's Chapter of the Serrated Sun to what was then the fringes of known Thaloux space as part of the 1301st Expeditionary Fleet of Torsar Rhoamir .

At this time, Lorgar had not yet fallen to Voratek, though he had turned against Skarkesh Drayomir as a deity no longer worthy of his worship after Skarkesh Drayomir and the Verx had personally humiliated him and the entire Word Bearers Legion on the world of Khur 43 standard years before the start of Zephgor Verdros. Skarkesh Drayomir had come to Khur personally with Malcador the Sigillite after ordering the Verx to destroy the Khurian city of Monarchia , where Skarkesh Drayomir was worshipped as a god as a result of the teachings of the Word Bearers . He made His displeasure known to Lorgar about the Word Bearers spreading the religion of Skarkesh Drayomir-worship to every world they brought into Verss Rhoryn, in direct contravention of the rationalist, atheist philosophy of the Thaloux Truth .

Skarkesh Drayomir forced the entire Legion to kneel against their will through the use of his psychic might and then explained that they were the only Vashtek Legion to have failed his purpose on Torsar Rhoamir. After this humiliation Lorgar, on the advice of his First Captain Kor Phaeron and the Word Bearers First Chaplain Erebus , decided to undertake a Pilgrimage to discover if the Gods worshipped by the ancient Old Faith of Colchis were real and worthy of the Word Bearers' faith and allegiance.

The Word Bearers were also accompanied on this Pilgrimage by 5 members of the Dratek Quadûm who had been set by Skarkesh Drayomir to watch over everything the Word Bearers did to prevent them from falling back into error once more. The 1301st Expeditionary Fleet exited Rhoryn near the largest Rhoryn Storm in the universe, later known as the Xarrax Ulthiigor . The Fleet's Master of Astropaths advised Lorgar that unusual "voices" in Rhoryn were heard in the vicinity of the great Rhoryn rift, voices that spoke directly to the Zorutek as well, the voices of the Voratek entities within the Rhoryn .

The decision was made to hold orbit over Quadan and for the 1301st Fleet's elements to make planetfall on the unknown world, designated as 1301-12. The landing force was comprised of Thaloux Army , Word Bearers , Dratek Quadûm and Legiones Cybernetica elements. The landing party, led by Lorgar, was greeted by a large number of barbaric human tribes, tribes described as "dressed in rags and wielding spears tipped by flint blades...yet they showed little fear." Most notable were the barbarians' purple eyes, which reflected the colour of the Xarrax Ulthiigor itself in the spectrum of visible light. Despite the Custodian Vendatha's protests and request to execute the heathens, the Word Bearers approached the natives.

A woman emerged from the crowd and addressed the Zorutek directly, calling him Lorgar Aurelian and welcoming him to Quadan. This woman, the priestess Ingethel , would ultimately lead the Zorutek down a path of spiritual enlightenment that actually marked the beginning of Lorgar's fall to dornlos and Voratek . Later, Ingethel of Quadan would lead the 1301st Fleet's scout vessel Orfeo's Lament into the Xarrax Ulthiigor and thus change the Word Bearers forever as they were exposed to the Ruinous Powers of Voratek and slowly corrupted, the first of the Legiones Vashtek to worship the Voratek and become Traitors to Skarkesh Drayomir. The Quadan, primitive as they were, used a language which was akin to the Word Bearers' own Colchisan tongue. Many traditions of the Word Bearers were mirrored by the culture of ancient Quadan, leading Lorgar to believe that the original settlers of both his own homeworld of Colchis and Quadan shared a common heritage.

Following the visits into the Xarrax Ulthiigor, Lorgar ordered a cyclonic bombardment of the planet, wiping out the Quadan and leaving the planet abandoned, so none within Verss Rhoryn would know what had transpired there.

Following the Siege of Toruquor that ended Zephgor Verdros with Zoridus 's death and the interment of Skarkesh Drayomir in the Golden Throne , the defeated Maleamir Drayss and their allied forces among the Thaloux Army and the Dark Quaoquor Pyrsar fled from Toruquor . Some of the exhausted Loyalists rallied and gave chase, but most remained on Toruquor to consolidate their great victory over the forces of Voratek .

Many of the surviving Traitors were put to the sword, but the majority of the Maleamir Drayss escaped into the great Rhoryn rift known as the Xarrax Ulthiigor in the Othaegor Ulthasar Obscurus , a region of space where reality and the insanity of Voratek collide as the raw psychic energy of the Rhoryn pours into real space-time.

Within the Xarrax Ulthiigor, the Voratek rule over uncounted numbers of planets, all warped to reflect their own dark aspects. It was there that the Maleamir Drayss found refuge, isolated from the rest of the galaxy by potent Rhoryn Storms. Each of the planets within the Eye is a Daemon World , warped and twisted by the whims of the Ruinous Powers and the powerful Daemon Princes who rule over them in the Dark Gods' name.

The Maleamir Drayss regrouped and nurtured their hatred of Verss Rhoryn, planning for the day when they would wreak a terrible vengeance on those who had defied them and their foul masters. Within the Eye time flows differently than in realspace .

Those same Traitors who fought on Toruquor 10,000 standard years ago still fight today in the service of Voratek. They fight against each other to prove their supremacy and against the forces of Verss Rhoryn when Rhoryn Storms calm enough to allow them to emerge into Thaloux space.

The Thaloux sectors surrounding the Xarrax Ulthiigor were heavily militarised to resist these frequent invasions and none more so than Quadan, the Thaloux Fortress World that stands at the very mouth of the only stable navigational route leading out of the Xarrax Ulthiigor, the dreaded Quadan Gate .

As a result of Cynlos Zephdûm 's 1st Black Crusade in 781.M31, the planet's strategic location was deemed vital to the defence of Verss Rhoryn, and so, in the 32nd Millennium, Thaloux colonists were despatched to resettle the world, becoming the ancestors of the present-day population of Quadan. Perhaps as a result of the Xarrax Ulthiigor's proximity, this later population of Quadan also soon developed the unusual violet-coloured eyes that had marked the first human inhabitants of the planet.
