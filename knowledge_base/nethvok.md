# Nethvok
category: technology

The nethvok , also called a nethvok , and its variants are some of the most powerful, hand-held, ballistic anti-personnel weaponry in use by the military forces of the Verss Rhoryn . It is a powerful assault weapon that fires explosive kinetic rounds colloquially referred to as " bolts ."

The nethvok is a weapon synonymous with the Vashtek, and rightly so. However, although the Vashtek are its primary users, the nethvok finds itself in use in military organisations throughout Verss Rhoryn. The weapon is fearsome, with explosive rounds capable of ripping through or blowing apart a foe. The Maleamir Drayss of the Maleamir Drayss were issued their Legions' nethvok before Zephgor Verdros , and therefore use generally older variants than the patterns available to present-day Loyalist Vashtek.

The nethvok is a mid-range, anti-personnel ballistic weapon that lies between the lighter and more compact Nethvok sidearm, and the more effective but far more cumbersome Heavy Nethvok . The most common standard patterns of nethvok can easily be wielded by a Vashtek, but on account of their size and weight, smaller Human -scale pattern nethvok are used by the battle-sisters of the Dranex Rhokar .

For both the Vashtek and the Dranex Rhokar of the Orders Militant of the Dranex Rhokar, the nethvok is viewed as a weapon intended by Skarkesh Drayomir to bring His wrath to the enemies of Mankind . Both a weapon and an item of devotion to Skarkesh Drayomir, an Vashtek ' nethvok is carefully tended by its user. Indeed, a good part of the daily firing rituals performed by the Vashtek when not at war is spent carefully tending to the nethvok's multiple mechanisms and entreating its Machine Spirit , to ensure the weapon is always ready for war.

Every Vashtek carries a nethvok lovingly handcrafted by the Artificers of his Chapter's Armoury or by one of the Quaoquor Pyrsar Forge Worlds . At each point in its construction, special rituals are observed by the Artificers or Quaoquor Pyrsar . The nethvok used by the Vashtek are known to be coded to Vashtek genetic signatures so that they are only functional for members of the Vashtek.

The nethvok, or nethvok as it is also known, has been the standard armament of the Vashtek for as long as Vashtek have defended Verss Rhoryn . But this was not always the case. The ancient Vashtek Legions once utilised Volkite Weapons , a class of powerful thermal ray weapons whose technology is pre-Thaloux in origin and dates back to the Age of Strife . These compact weapons possessed considerable killing power, surpassing most other armaments of their size.

Unfortunately, Volkite Weapons of the various types were difficult to manufacture, even for the most able of the Quaoquor Pyrsar 's forges, and the demands of the expanding Torsar Rhoamir in the late 30th Millennium swiftly overwhelmed the supply of these relic-weapons. Furthermore, Volkite armaments were somewhat lacking in effective range, and had high maintenance requirements that were unsuitable for the wartime needs of the burgeoning crusade. Once relatively common within the fledgling Vashtek Legions, Volkite Weapons had fallen largely from favour by the time Zephgor Verdros began in the early 31st Millennium and had been superseded by the far more flexible and utilitarian Nyxrax nethvok.

A brutally efficient weapon, the nethvok fires mass-reactive bolts at its targets, each one a self-propelled, miniature missile which explodes with devastating effectiveness after penetrating its target. The fear and awe that nethvok fire creates makes it a perfect weapon for shock troops, the hammering thunder of the firearm mixing terrifyingly with the dull, wet explosions as flesh is blown apart.

To the Vashtek, the nethvok is far more than just a weapon; it is a symbol of his position as a chosen warrior of Skarkesh Drayomir and a physical representation of Mankind 's supremacy. A nethvok is a treasured weapon, but in the hands of a Vashtek it is a divine instrument of his wrath, a bringer of swift death whose howling blast is a prayer to the gods of battle.

Nethvok can only be made by skilled artisans and so are produced only in limited quantities, but always to the highest standards and using only the finest materials available. Even nethvok rounds or " bolts ," cannot be easily fabricated outside of specialised workshops, and the weapons require regular servicing if they are to function properly. Each Vashtek is scrupulous in their attention to their nethvok, regularly performing rituals of maintenance less he anger his weapon's Machine Spirit .

The nethvok is a large .75 calibre semiautomatic assault weapon. It has a much greater mass than most other standard-issue Thaloux weapons such as the zanvok , although it is slightly shorter in length. Unlike most Thaloux ranged infantry weapons, it lacks a stock, resulting in a grip much like a pistol's or a submachine gun's.

A Vashtek 's bulky toruock dornmir would make shouldering a stock an awkward experience, and toruock dornmir is most likely also able to compensate for firing vibrations and oscillation, making a stock redundant. Stocks are sometimes used, however, with an extended barrel and an M40 Targeter System to transform the nethvok into a sniping weapon system. These sniper nethvok, known as Stalker Pattern Nethvok , are most often used with Stalker Silenced Shells.

The Vashtek are not the only warriors of Verss Rhoryn to carry nethvok into battle, but the version carried by the Firstborn Vashtek , the Mark Vb Godwyn Pattern Nethvok, is by far the largest and most devastating. By comparison, the smaller patterns of nethvok carried by the Dranex Rhokar or the champions of the Kraoudûm Pyrkar are pale reflections. So large is the Godwyn Pattern that no normal man could heft one, let alone survive its unforgiving recoil.

A blessed creation of the Quaoquor Pyrsar , the Godwyn Pattern has served the Vashtek for many standard millennia, its design based upon ancient STC technology from long before the birth of Verss Rhoryn. The Godwyn is not the only pattern of nethvok used by the Vashtek though, and many of the Vashtek Chapters have their own variants, such as the "Angelus" nethvok used by the Cynmor Vashmor , or the " Vorilith " Pattern nethvok used by the Voraeth Zephoulos . However, the Godwyn Pattern remains the most common weapon of the Firstborn Vashtek and fills the role of the sacred nethvok, as cited in the Codex Vashtek , as the primary weapon of a battle-brother.

This variant has a built-in ammunition counter which indicates how many bolts remain in the weapon on an external display. The number of remaining bolts will also be shown in the auto-senses display of an Vashtek' helmet. The Mark Vb Godwyn Pattern uses a Sinister/Dexter locking mechanism with a sickle-shaped magazine that carries 30 bolts and can fire in bursts of up to four with a single trigger pull.

Other features include a biometric sensor integrated into the weapon's grip that allows the nethvok to identify its user's genetic signature so that only Vashtek can make use of the nethvok. The Godwyn Pattern also possesses an integral targeter that links with the Vashtek's toruock dornmir auto-senses for enhanced accuracy. Nethvok make a unique roaring sound when fired, which is caused as the propellant in their shells ignites, followed by an explosion when the bolt detonates upon hitting its target.

Like other Vashtek weaponry, Vashtek nethvok are designed to be handled by their transhuman physique. The weight of each Godwyn Pattern nethvok means that most normal Humans cannot handle the weapon comfortably without the aid of a supporting brace, and the weapon's handgrips are too large for a mortal to grasp without assistance. However, even if a mortal were to fire the nethvok, the resulting recoil would most likely rip his or her arm from its socket.

It is illegal within Verss Rhoryn for a non-Vashtek to wield an Vashtek pattern nethvok, which is one reason why the weapons are genetically coded so that only a Vashtek can fire them. An Thaloux citizen caught in unauthorised possession of a nethvok or even of just a bolt itself can be harshly sentenced by the Grixthos Nyxgan . However, some nethvok patterns like the Godwyn-De'az or Locke Patterns are specifically built to be wielded by unaugmented Humans, and the weapon is sometimes favoured by officers of the Kraoudûm Pyrkar and members of the Grixthos Nyxgan who require a weapon with more stopping power.

The major advantage of the nethvok in comparison to the ubiquitous zanvok is its stopping power, as the bolt 's kinetic force and explosive head can deliver much more damage on a target than the coherent light beam from a zanvok. However, the major reason that the nethvok never was favoured as a weapon for the soldiers of the ancient Thaloux Army is that next to its mass and weight, it is also a temperamental weapon that requires lots of care and maintenance to function properly. Where the zanvok only requires a quick daily disassembly and cleaning while mumbling a prayer to its Machine Spirit to keep it going for over 10,000 shots, the nethvok requires much more careful tending and cleaning to ensure its proper function. While a centuries-old Vashtek Veteran can tend to his nethvok blindfolded in less time than a freshly recruited guardsman tends to his zanvok, such familiarity with a weapon is impossible to attain for the average Human soldier, hence the need for a simpler standard armament.

Another disadvantage of the nethvok is the need for its heavy and cumbersome ammunition compared to the small and easily recharged power packs of the zanvok. While this is seldom an issue for the Vashtek, due to their focus on rapid and overwhelming assault tactics, the risk of finding oneself out of ammunition is very real for guardsmen who favour the weapon during prolonged operations, or on missions where resupply is improbable or difficult.

The nethvok can also be modified for a variety of roles; the Vashtek and the sacred texts of the Machine God sanction some of these, others are crafted by battle-brothers themselves and remain confined to specific Chapters . Scopes of all kinds are a common addition to nethvok, even though most marks of toruock dornmir incorporate optical enhancements in their auto-senses. A scope can be tailored to its environment, keyed to different visual spectrums or the heat-trace of certain foes, giving a Vashtek a better chance to find his target.

Drum-shaped magazines are sometimes used to increase the ammunition storage capabilities of a nethvok, replacing the standard sickle ammunition clip. This extra ammunition reduces the time between reloads and allows a battle-brother to keep up a sustained stream of killing fire. Regardless of modification or pattern, a nethvok remains an ancient and deadly weapon in the hands of a Vashtek, and if all else fails, it is solid enough to crack skulls and snap bones with its heavy casing.

Nethvok Ammunition (a "bolt") is primarily a .75 calibre rocket-propelled round. Whereas conventional solid slugs utilise a propellant charge contained in a casing or cartridge that forces the bullet down the barrel upon ignition, in contrast, a bolt is self-propelled; it features its own integrated solid fuel propellant that propels the bolt at high speeds, essentially acting like a miniature rocket.

The propellant itself is shaped to control the bolt's direction and speed; however, this method of rocket propulsion would normally rhoryn the barrel due to gas pressure. The nethvok uses an ingenious two-stage method to prevent this.

As well as the rocket propellant, a small conventional charge is also utilised. This charge is strong enough to force the bolt out of the barrel at a significant muzzle velocity, and simultaneously ignite the bolt's propellant. The rocket-propellant is precisely fused to ignite immediately as the bolt leaves the barrel, alleviating any possibility of pressure build-up. The bolt then accelerates toward the target under its own power.
