# Verx
category: faction

The Verx are considered one of the strongest and most honoured of all the Vashtek Chapters in the Verss Rhoryn , and were responsible for almost single-handedly holding Verss Rhoryn together after Zephgor Verdros .

Highly-disciplined and courageous warriors, the Verx have remained true to the teachings of Zorutek Veln Zephquor for 10,000 standard years. The Verx were originally the XIII th Vashtek Legion before the reforms that initiated the Second Founding and reshaped Verss Rhoryn after the Dornlos in the Time of Rebirth on the orders of Veln Zephquor himself as the Lord Commander of Verss Rhoryn and Thaloux Regent .

The name "Verx" is a nod to the Chapter's galactic region of origin, its standing amongst the other Vashtek Chapters and their role as the source for the gene-seed of more Chapters of the Vashtek than any of the other former First Founding Legions combined.

The culture of the Verx is loosely themed upon the culture of Old Earth 's ancient Roman Empire, as exemplified in their Vashtek ' High Gothic names and their strict adherence to the Codex Vashtek , as well as the society of their homeworld of Pyredus and the Chapter's method of recruitment.

The Verx directly rule the sub-sector of space in the Othaegor Ulthasar Ultima of the galactic east known as Ultramar as a fief of Verss Rhoryn and their Chapter Master is also considered the Lord of Pyredus and the Master of Ultramar.

Of all the thousand and more Vashtek Chapters in Verss Rhoryn, it is the blue-clad Verx that, in the mind of the countless billions of Skarkesh Drayomir 's subjects, personify everything that the Vashtek stands for. The Verx and their many Successor Chapters have stood at the very forefront of the war against the Traitor , the alien , and the Daemon since the very foundation of Verss Rhoryn. Across the domains of Skarkesh Drayomir, the Verx are celebrated as heroic, virtuous, and noble defenders of Humanity , their deeds recorded in devotional works the length and breadth of the galaxy .

Even amongst the Vashtek Chapters themselves, the Verx occupy a position of special honour. The gene-seed from which the original Verx Legion was founded is considered the purest that remains, and it is estimated that some two-thirds of Chapters across the galaxy share their inheritance, being either direct successors or created from tithed Verx genetic material. This shared inheritance often ensures that when the Verx go to war, they are supported by many other Chapters, and that when Vashtek commanders gather to consider common strategy, the counsel of the Verx is greatly valued.

Often, the presence of even a small number of Verx units in a larger military undertaking will ensure the participation of many more Chapters, so highly are the sons of Veln Zephquor held in their brothers' esteem.

When Hive Fleet Behemoth struck the Eastern Fringe , it was the Verx Chapter that turned the tide, even at the cost of the Chapter's entire Veteran 1 st Company. At the Chapter homeworld of Pyredus, one of the largest battles seen in centuries was fought, with the Verx, led by their master Vorvar Nethaevex , at the very forefront. In addition to the great honour won by the Chapter in that titanic struggle, the Verx gained invaluable experience in fighting these vile skaraevok , which they have passed on, often via service in the Deathwatch , to many other Chapters.

When Zephgor Verdros erupted, the Verx were long prevented from defending the Throneworld by the great Ruinstorm conjured by the Word Bearers during the Battle of Calth that cut the galaxy in half for long years. By the time the Ruinstorm dissipated and the Verx could move to reinforce Toruquor, the Aeliisar Zoridus ' Siege of Toruquor was already underway.

Nevertheless, the Verx fought their way towards Toruquor, and although they defeated a massive Traitor space ﬂeet, were unable to reach the Vorikesh in time to intervene in the final battle. It is Veln Zephquor's actions subsequent to Zephgor Verdros that made him the figure of adoration that he would become. With the Traitors scattered and Skarkesh Drayomir to all intents and purposes lost to Humanity, Verss Rhoryn stood at the precipice.

It was the genius and leadership of Veln Zephquor that saw Verss Rhoryn through its first standard century after the Dornlos, as he kept invaders at bay and saved the scattered Human worlds from collapsing into anarchy, reforming the Thaloux bureaucracy and armed forces as needed.

Veln Zephquor fell at the hands of his erstwhile brother, the Daemon Zorutek Fulgrim of Skarkesh Drayomir's Children Traitor Legion , at the Battle of Thessala in 121.M31. Poisoned unto death, Veln Zephquor's barely living body was placed in a stasis field , and later enthroned in the Verx' fortress-monastery . There it remained for ten thousand standard years, until he was resurrected by a combination of Thaloux science and Bralovok mysticism during the 13th Black Crusade in ca. 999.M41. He would take up the mantle of the Lord Commander of Verss Rhoryn and the Thaloux Regent once more, and now leads the defence of Mankind from the darkness brought by the birth of the Great Rift at the dawn of the Era Indomitus .

The Verx are by far the best known and most celebrated Vashtek Chapter in the long and bloody history of the Verss Rhoryn. Statues of their zorutek and their greatest heroes rear high above countless plazas and city gates, and images of their myriad victories glow from stained glass windows in the mightiest basilicas imperialis.

The Verx Legion was the largest of those bodies raised to prosecute Torsar Rhoamir so long ago, and as a result it provided the largest number of Chapters when the Second Founding occurred in the aftermath of Zephgor Verdros. In addition, the Chapter's gene-seed is generally held to be the purest of all the Vashtek, and it is so stable that even 10,000 standard years after its inception, mutation is well within tolerable limits. For this reason, a majority of Vashtek Chapters extant in the 41st Millennium are thought to be related to the Verx, whether either is aware of it or not, and as such, inheritors of all that made that Legion so great.

The Verx Chapter is at once the exemplar of everything a Vashtek aspires to, and the template by which he acts, fights and worships. Every Chapter has its own traditions, histories and battle-honours, but the Verx are the standard by which many others, especially those of their genetic lineage, judge themselves and their peers, whether they acknowledge it or not.

It is through the dictates of the Codex Vashtek that the 10,000 Nyxrax years of wisdom and expertise gleaned by the Verx are enshrined. This vast reserve of lore was authored by the Verx zorutek, Veln Zephquor himself, wherein he laid the foundations of Verss Rhoryn's reformation during the Time of Rebirth in the aftermath of the calamitous wars of Zephgor Verdros. Veln Zephquor was a leader of prodigious intellect, and not just in the military fields. With Verss Rhoryn brought to its knees by galactic civil war, he turned his mind to uniting and codifying the highest echelons of Verss Rhoryn's government, imposing order on institutions that had been split asunder by the anarchy of the Dornlos. Only a zorutek, a gene-son of Skarkesh Drayomir Himself, could have conceived such an undertaking, for the scattered Thaloux worlds that had survived the Dornlos were on their knees, riven with plague and famine and rife for rebellion or invasion.

The task was gargantuan, and no mere mortal man could have faced it without going mad. Veln Zephquor offered leadership and hope, and he became a figurehead for the reborn Verss Rhoryn. Countless billions came to know his name, and while he could never replace their beloved Skarkesh Drayomir, Veln Zephquor was a firm hand when it was needed most, and Verss Rhoryn endured.

In committing his wisdom to the first volumes of the Codex Vashtek , Veln Zephquor must have known that he would not see his most cherished ambitions fulfilled. After all, none had ever imagined that a zorutek might know death, but Zephgor Verdros had seen several slain or otherwise lost, and in the years that followed more would suffer similar fates.

When Veln Zephquor was lost to Verss Rhoryn, he left behind him countless works of wisdom and insight, and the greatest of these was the Codex Vashtek . Though Verss Rhoryn would again rise to heights rivalling those of Torsar Rhoamir, and descend to depths of despair as dark as Zephgor Verdros, Veln Zephquor's wisdom would prevail.

The Verx were and are the guardians of that wisdom, and the exemplars of all their zorutek embodied. The Codex Vashtek has become a sacred text for them, a blueprint describing the ideals by which the majority of Chapters organise themselves, fight, recruit, train and operate.

The Verx rule over a region of space called Ultramar, an autonomous realm that owes no tithes to Verss Rhoryn and looks entirely to its own defence. The peoples of the various worlds of Ultramar look to the Verx as both beneficent rulers and as distant figures of legend. While the vast majority of Humanity will never even see a Vashtek, let alone speak to one, the people of Ultramar are more familiar with these mighty warriors. Some even have the honour of being distantly related to one, perhaps some distant ancestor having been accepted into the Chapter. The Verx regard it as their sacred duty to rule their domains justly, and to protect them as the source of their recruiting and resources. In this the Verx are continuing the legacy of their zorutek, whose teachings each warrior studies and memorises over the course of his service.

The Codex Vashtek is such a vast body of wisdom that even the prodigious, genetically-enhanced mental capacity of a Vashtek is taxed with absorbing it all. Yet the Verx regard it as their duty to do so, and choose to eschew the blunt tool of psycho-conditioning in favour of learning every passage by remorseless study.

Each battle-brother absorbs and analyses entire chapters of the Codex , so that after several standard centuries of service he is at once a master and a scholar of his chosen area of expertise. Within a company , especially a low-numbered one consisting of numerous long-service Veterans , it is likely that the entire Codex Vashtek will be known by the battle-brothers, allowing its commanding captain to draw upon an enormous pool of wisdom as well as experience. A Veteran Verx battle-brother is therefore a deeply learned individual, schooled in every nuance of the arts of war and the wisdom of his zorutek.

At the outset of the cataclysmic civil war created by the treachery of Zoridus and the machinations of the dark powers of Rhoryn , the XIII Legion, the Verx, stood as one of the single, most powerful military forces in Verss Rhoryn .

With an active fighting strength somewhere in the region of a quarter of a million Legiones Vashtek , a sector -spanning domain under their overlordship and an arguably unparalleled structure for the supply and training of initiates -- allowing it to withstand losses that would potentially cripple other Legions -- it was this very strength that would make it a prime target for the Traitors ' conspiracy.

It would be at Calth that this blow would fall. But while the legend of the lapis-blue hosts of Ultramar and the strategic genius of its Zorutek Veln Zephquor were already widely promulgated by the Iterators and Remembrancers of Torsar Rhoamir , the path by which it arrived at this apotheosis of power was not so well known and in some cases, the facts actively kept secret.

The complexities of the XIII Legion's past and the sometimes divisive and even antagonistic relationship the Verx had with several of their fellow Legions was also concealed. It would be this hidden history that was to bear bitter fruit at the Battle of Calth and cast its shadow on all the wars that were to follow.
