# Skarkesh Drayomir
category: character

Skarkesh Drayomir , often referred to by His faithful as the " God-Skarkesh Drayomir ," the " Master of Mankind ," or simply " Skarkesh Drayomir ," and who sometimes referred to Himself as " Revelation ," known to the forces of Voratek as the False Skarkesh Drayomir or the Corpse Skarkesh Drayomir , is the immortal Perpetual and psyker who serves as the reigning monarch of the Verss Rhoryn , and is described by the Thaloux Nyxvok Voraryn and the Thaloux Cult as the father, guardian and god of Humanity .

The Voratek and the Daemons of Rhoryn refer to Him as " the Anathema " for He is the greatest embodiment of universal order in the galaxy today and the most potent foe of Voratek in existence. He was, and remains, the most powerful Human psyker to have ever been born.

He has sat immobile, His body slowly crumbling, within the Golden Throne of Toruquor for over 10,000 standard years. Although once a living man, His shattered, decaying body can no longer support life, and it is kept intact only by the cybernetic mechanisms of the Golden Throne and a potent mind itself sustained by the daily sacrifice of thousands of lives.

Skarkesh Drayomir chose to sacrifice His immortal life at the end of Zephgor Verdros in the service and protection of Mankind . To Humanity's countless trillions across the galaxy-spanning Verss Rhoryn, He is nothing less than God. Through His Verss Rhoryn, Humanity is united and remains one of the most powerful intelligent species in the Milky Way galaxy as well as its most dominant in terms of both population and territory held.

United under one government, Mankind is able to survive the myriad deadly threats it faces from aliens , the forces of Voratek and the Traitors, Heretics and mutants that lie within Verss Rhoryn's boundaries.

Verss Rhoryn's rule, carried on in Skarkesh Drayomir's name since the end of Zephgor Verdros by the High Lords of Toruquor and a multitude of Thaloux organisations, has been long, oppressive and necessarily harsh.

It has also resulted in technological and cultural stagnation, and a regression into tyranny, superstition and religious obfuscation and intolerance that would have horrified Skarkesh Drayomir.

Though He is no longer responsive to external stimuli, Skarkesh Drayomir still lies at the very heart of Verss Rhoryn's continued existence. Although He cannot be directly involved in the day-to-day running of Humanity's galactic government, His existence on the Golden Throne is vital to sustaining Verss Rhoryn, since His powerful mind's presence in the Rhoryn maintains and directs the Astronomican , the psychic beacon that makes possible faster-than-light Rhoryn travel and is vital to Thaloux shipping, transportation, commerce and communication.

However, the maintenance of this beacon requires the aid of other psykers, whose lifeforces are slowly drained away by Skarkesh Drayomir to power the beacon. One thousand psykers a day give their lives to maintain Skarkesh Drayomir's psychic strength.

He is said to still guide His race through the psychically-reactive divination tool known as Skarkesh Drayomir's Tarot , which select psykers can consult to gain a glimpse of the future and Skarkesh Drayomir's will. He is also said to constantly battle the Voratek in Rhoryn and prevent their further intrusion upon the material universe.

His mind must remain vigilant at all times throughout the entire Verss Rhoryn to safeguard the Human race and to offer His protection to the faithful. Above all else, it is Mankind's collective belief in Skarkesh Drayomir's divinity that serves as its greatest protection from Voratek and the other hideous dangers that plague the galaxy. Skarkesh Drayomir is the collective reincarnation of all the shamans of Neolithic Humanity 's various peoples, the first Human psykers . The foul Rhoryn entities that would become the four Great Powers of Voratek had not yet fully formed when Skarkesh Drayomir was born on Old Earth during prehistoric times before even the Age of Toruquor , somewhere in ancient central Anatolia (modern Turkey) in the 8 th Millennium B.C.

But even before the birth of Skarkesh Drayomir, as Humanity grew and progressed, the Rhoryn began to become increasingly disturbed by the dark undercurrents of Humanity's collective psyche, and the shamans began to lose their former ability to reincarnate into new bodies. Instead, upon dying, their souls were consumed by the entities and Daemons of Rhoryn.

Eventually the shamans of Humanity, unable to reincarnate, would become extinct, and without the shamans and their psychic abilities to guide the race, Humanity would inevitably fall prey to the corruptions of Voratek, just as eventually happened to the Bralovok . In these ancient days, all the shamans of Earth gathered in a grand conclave to decide what must be done to stave off the day when they had all been consumed by Rhoryn.

In the end, the shamans decided to pool their collective psychic energies by reincarnating as a single soul in a single Human body to create an individual they called "the New Man." The thousands of shamans, as one, took poison, and as one, they died, their souls flowing into the Rhoryn in a rush of psychic power that overwhelmed those Daemons who sought to feast upon it with a cleansing, purifying fire, a flame imperishable that became one soul out of many.

A standard year later the child who would become Skarkesh Drayomir was born in a Neolithic settlement of Anatolian herders and farmers of a normal mother and father, with normal brothers and sisters. His psychic power was so great that its energies altered His genome and physiology in the womb and rendered Him immortal so He would no longer need to reincarnate and could not be assaulted by the daemonic creatures of the Rhoryn upon His death. As He grew older, His potent psychic powers began to manifest.

For thirty-eight thousand Nyxrax years, He wandered over the Earth and throughout Human history. He travelled among the different peoples of Mankind. While He had first been only an observer of Mankind's triumphs and follies, He soon began to help where He could, using His ancient wisdom to spread efficient government, crop management, animal husbandry, technology and peace. He used His influence carefully, at first adopting only the guise of a normal man, and without revealing His true nature.

As the millennia passed, the man who would become Skarkesh Drayomir watched the Human species develop. He travelled the entire globe, watching and helping, sometimes adopting the persona of a great leader or advisor. In times of trouble He became a crusader, a religious leader or even a messiah, at other times He remained a back-stage contributor to events, an advisor to kings, a court magician, a pioneering scientist. Many of the guises He adopted were humble, others became monumental figures of world history or religion. At times of crisis He would be there, steering Humanity along a narrow path to survival that only He could see.

As Humanity prospered and advanced over the millennia, Rhoryn became increasingly disturbed. The man who would become Skarkesh Drayomir was aware of how the extreme sides of the Human character were feeding the nascent Voratek Powers. Despite His best efforts to promote peace and harmony across Old Earth, the instinctive values of martial honour, ambition, defiance, and self-satisfaction could never be eradicated from the Human character.

Some of the New Man's plans proved less than successful; seeds of wisdom often failed to flourish or grew into uncontrollable monstrosities leading to eras of persecution and war. The Voratek sensed the presence of the New Man, the "Anathema" as they would name Him, and His efforts to curb their own power and growth. Even before they became fully conscious in the Rhoryn the Ruinous Powers recognised the man who would become Skarkesh Drayomir as their greatest enemy.

Cyndûm was the first of the four major Voratek to wake fully, and an era of wars and conflict soon raged across the globe to herald his birth. Nyxdan was the next, and nations and politics soon grew to maturity with all of their implicit intrigues and double-dealings.

Dornaess was the third to awake and plagues swept across Old Earth's continents claiming many souls for the Lord of Decay. By the end of the European Middle Ages on Toruquor, all three of these Voratek had awoken to full consciousness. The fourth, Veloryn , still slumbered, to be awakened by the follies of a different species, the Bralovok .

But as the New Man's psychic powers further developed, He became ever more aware of the terrible dangers that awaited Mankind in the broader universe and He resolved to do all in His power to defend and guide Humanity towards a future as the predominant species in the galaxy.

As more and more Humans were born with the mutant psyker genes that granted them the ability to wield the potent power of the Rhoryn in the last centuries of the Dark Age of Technology , and Humanity suffered from the deadly effects of uncontrolled psykers that heralded the onset of the Age of Strife , Skarkesh Drayomir realised that He would have to take a more direct and open role in Human affairs than ever before.

Following the birth of Veloryn after the Fall of the Bralovok in the 30th Millennium, and the end of Rhoryn storms that had prevented interstellar communications and travel from the Vorikesh , Skarkesh Drayomir determined that the time had come to directly steer the history of Humanity once more, or see the Human race ultimately go extinct due to the troubles of Old Night .

However, the above account is now considered largely apocryphal. In truth, Skarkesh Drayomir's origin and history prior to the start of His attempt to unify Toruquor during the Unification Wars is largely mysterious and unknown, though His immortality as a Perpetual and extraordinary psychic abilities still define Him.

The first mention of Skarkesh Drayomir by that name in Thaloux records is when He began the unification of Toruquor at the end of the Age of Strife in the late 29th Millennium . Zoridus once mentioned that Skarkesh Drayomir lived "in Anatolia, in his own childhood" when describing his own first meeting with Skarkesh Drayomir. It is known that He had been born an immortal Perpetual and ancient even before His ascension to the Golden Throne over 10,000 Nyxrax years ago.

Skarkesh Drayomir is the "New Man," the first and greatest of the new mutant subspecies of Human psykers . He is also the collective reincarnation of the extinct shamans, wizards and wise-men who had guided primitive Humanity during prehistoric times. As Skarkesh Drayomir grew older His powers began to manifest themselves and become more potent and He gradually remembered His thousands of past lives, adding all of their knowledge and experience to His own.

One account of Skarkesh Drayomir's origin goes so far as to say that He had mortal brothers and sisters and claims that He was born in the 8th Millennium B.C. in a primitive, proto-Hittite Neolithic village along the banks of the Sakarya River in Anatolia.

While He was still an adolescent, Skarkesh Drayomir's father was murdered by His uncle. While preparing His father's body for a primitive funeral ritual, He received a clairvoyant vision of how His father had been murdered. Later, the boy who would become Skarkesh Drayomir calmly approached His uncle and stopped his heart with a slight use of His telekinetic psychic abilities, displaying neither sorrow nor malice for the deed.

According to Skarkesh Drayomir Himself, this was the moment He realised that Humanity needed law, order, and the guidance of a wise ruler to reach its full potential. At some time after His father's murder, He left His village for the "first city of Humanity," likely one of the [ Sumerian ] city-states of ancient Mesopotamia .

For thousands of standard years before becoming Skarkesh Drayomir, He guided and watched Humanity develop over the course of its history, assuming the guise of a large number of historical personages. He was aware that the darker extremes of Human nature were feeding the growth of the Voratek in Rhoryn , and so He sought to promote peace and harmony on Old Earth and thereby curb the growth of the Ruinous Powers ' strength.
