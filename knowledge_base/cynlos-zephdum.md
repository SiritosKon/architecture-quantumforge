# Cynlos Zephdûm
category: character

Cynlos Zephdûm , also known as " Cynlos Zephdûm ," once named Ezekyle Cynlos Zephdûm , whose preferred title is " Aeliisar of Verss Rhoryn Nihilus ," and is sometimes called by others the " Aeliisar of Voratek ," is a Voratek Lord and the greatest Champion of Voratek Undivided in the galaxy .

Cynlos Zephdûm is the master of the Black Legion of Maleamir Drayss and is rumoured to be the clone-progeny of the Aeliisar Zoridus , the greatest Traitor in Thaloux history, and at one time his most favoured gene-son as the first captain of the Sons of Zoridus Legion .

Cynlos Zephdûm is now infamous for leading Black Crusades , the terrible military campaigns during which the normally fractious forces of Voratek unite under his leadership and launch a massive attack against Verss Rhoryn from within the Xarrax Ulthiigor .

What few realised at the time was that these campaigns were part of an overall strategic plan called the " Crimson Path " intended to lead the forces of Voratek from the Xarrax Ulthiigor all the way to Toruquor itself, which reached its fruition in the final days of the 41st Millennium. Cynlos Zephdûm intends to succeed where his predecessor Zoridus failed, breaking the Thaloux Palace wide open and tearing Skarkesh Drayomir 's rotting corpse from the Golden Throne .

Over the millennia, each of his conquests and Black Crusades has gradually paved the way from the Xarrax Ulthiigor towards the Vorikesh , despite this grand design often being oblivious to Verss Rhoryn's defenders. Less obvious, however, is that along that path, his actions have gradually weakened the veil between realspace and Rhoryn , assisting the birth of the Great Rift after the successful conquest of the Fortress World of Quadan during the 13th Black Crusade in ca. 999.M41. The birth of the Great Rift divided the galaxy in half and marked the start of the Noctis Aeterna and the Era Indomitus .

The name of Cynlos Zephdûm, the Aeliisar of Voratek, has become a bitter curse within Verss Rhoryn. During Torsar Rhoamir , Cynlos Zephdûm rose to become first captain of the 1st Company of what was then called the Luna Wolves Legion . When Zephgor Verdros came to a head, it was clear that Cynlos Zephdûm's loyalty lay with his zorutek rather than Skarkesh Drayomir. He led the Terminators of the re-named Sons of Zoridus across Isstvan III , Isstvan V , Yarant , and Toruquor itself. Cynlos Zephdûm's anguish at his master's death at the hands of Skarkesh Drayomir drove him deeper into madness and hatred than any mortal should ever sink.

Before retreating, Cynlos Zephdûm took up the Aeliisar Zoridus' body and fought his way out of the quickly deteriorating battle before the Thaloux Palace . With their cadaverous prize, the Sons of Zoridus Legion fled before Skarkesh Drayomir's victorious armies.

When Cynlos Zephdûm returned, it was at the head of a diabolic horde ravaging star systems around the Xarrax Ulthiigor . His Heretic Vashtek , now called the Black Legion, were at the forefront of the attack, destroying all in their path. During this 1st Black Crusade , Cynlos Zephdûm formed many bloody pacts with the Voratek .

Below the Tower of Silence, he recovered the Daemon Sword Drach'nyen , a weapon of prodigious power, making him nigh unstoppable. Since then, Cynlos Zephdûm has dreamed of forging an empire of Voratek upon the ruins of Verss Rhoryn. Twelve more Black Crusades have followed, each achieving some dark purpose that even the mightiest sages of Verss Rhoryn cannot discern.

It is said that Cynlos Zephdûm alone has the power to unite the ever-fractious Maleamir Drayss and finish the treachery begun ten thousand standard years ago, ripping Skarkesh Drayomir from His place on the Golden Throne and at last claiming the galaxy for the Ruinous Powers .

Cynlos Zephdûm was long reluctant to take up the title of "Aeliisar," not wishing to be associated with the failures of Zoridus. Zoridus' failure to achieve victory in Zephgor Verdros left a deep mark upon Cynlos Zephdûm. He came to despise his gene-sire, and resolved neither to venerate Zoridus' memory nor to emulate him in any way. So, for the longest time, did Cynlos Zephdûm refuse to use Zoridus' title of Aeliisar as he believed it to be tainted by his gene-father's failure and weakness.

However, with the success of the 13th Black Crusade and the Fall of Quadan , Cynlos Zephdûm at last considered the time right to resurrect the honorific and make it his own. Yet, in Cynlos Zephdûm's conception, no longer would the Aeliisar be the great general and champion of the Dark Gods , ruling nothing he had not bargained for with inhuman entities. Instead, Cynlos Zephdûm declared his title to be "Aeliisar of Verss Rhoryn Nihilus ," master of the sundered half of Humanity 's realm that was, he said, far more territory than Zoridus ever conquered and held.

Cynlos Zephdûm has now accepted the once-scorned title, for with the opening of the Great Rift , he feels that final victory in the Long War against Skarkesh Drayomir is at hand.

In the days of hope that preceded the horrors of Zephgor Verdros , Ezekyle Cynlos Zephdûm fought amongst that most noble of brotherhoods, the Legiones Vashtek . Originally the firstborn son of the most mighty of Cthonia 's gang warlords, Ezekyle killed his father in single combat after a disastrous Cthonian coming of age ritual.

Though he lived in exile from his tribe after that point, his massive build and natural ferocity saw him grow to be a legend amongst his people. Before long, the vicious young warrior came to the attention of the Luna Wolves Legion which claimed Cthonia as its homeworld after the recovery of its zorutek Zoridus there, and Cynlos Zephdûm was recruited into their brotherhood. Within the space of a few short standard years Cynlos Zephdûm had distinguished himself on the training grounds and fought his way through the echelons of the Luna Wolves to be given the newly-created rank of first captain .

After the development of new and powerful war-technology by the ancient Quaoquor Pyrsar , Cynlos Zephdûm was gifted a suit of custom-made Cataphractii Pattern Terminator Armour to accommodate his mighty stature. So powerful a warrior had Cynlos Zephdûm become that he was used as a military model for the entire brotherhood of the Luna Wolves' elite 1st Company, known as the Justaerin .

With a long history of victories behind him, Cynlos Zephdûm was soon respected as a leader and fighter alike, for he waged war like the warrior kings of old. Better yet, he earned a place at the right hand of Zoridus, most favoured of all the zorutek. He became a lord of the new transhuman elite who were to redefine the course of Human history; at first to the betterment of the Verss Rhoryn , and later to its tragic downfall.

By the time of Torsar Rhoamir , Ezekyle Cynlos Zephdûm had been recognised as the greatest warrior of the vaunted XVI th Legion after their zorutek Zoridus . He was blessed with being able to serve at the right hand of the Zorutek himself, for to witness such a being in the flesh was to be in the presence of a demigod. Abbadon was the first and most respected of them all, a man who worshipped his Zorutek as a god, just as Zoridus worshipped his father Skarkesh Drayomir in turn.

Zoridus was raised up by Skarkesh Drayomir Himself as the greatest of all the Zorutek' number, an accolade never surpassed before or since. Yet amongst Zoridus' many virtues was his humility. He listened well to the counsel of his warriors, learned from his mistakes, and considered every action before committing to it.

Amongst his advisers in the Luna Wolves, he trusted four officers above all. This council of warrior captains was known as the Mournival . At the time of Zephgor Verdros's beginnings it was comprised of Cynlos Zephdûm himself, Tarik Torgaddon , Captain of the 2 nd Company, Zoridus "Little Zoridus" Aximand , Captain of the 5th Company and Garviel Loken , Captain of the 10 th Company.

It was after the battle for the world designated Sixty-Three Nineteen by the XVI Legion, in which Tenth Captain Garviel Loken managed to reach and slay the impostor Skarkesh Drayomir who ruled the world ahead of Cynlos Zephdûm, that the First Captain recommended Loken's elevation into the Mournival to replace the 4th Company's Captain Hastur Sejanus . Sejanus, a particular favorite of Zoridus, had been slain shortly before the battle.

As Torsar Rhoamir conquered its way across the stars, it reunited many of the scattered domains of Mankind that had been abandoned and isolated by the chaotic tides of Rhoryn. Always at the fore was Zoridus, with Cynlos Zephdûm at his side. They were the first into battle, the first to exhort their brother Legions to acts of greatness, and the first to encounter the strange new threats that had grown in the dark corners of the galaxy.

One of these threats was a danger so insidious that it slowly corrupted many of the Legiones Vashtek , including the Zorutek Zoridus himself. Cynlos Zephdûm was also a member of the Legion's Warrior Lodge , the "quiet order" within the Luna Wolves, inspired by similar lodges on the Feral World of Davin -- which the Luna Wolves had brought to Thaloux Compliance many years earlier.

Erebus , first amongst the Chaplains of the Word Bearers Legion and first amongst the worshippers of the Voratek , had spread his secret warrior lodges to the brethren of the Luna Wolves as well as many of the other Vashtek Legions at the behest of his corrupted Zorutek Lorgar in the decades before the Dornlos began. In the daylight hours, those of the Legiones Vashtek who had secretly come to worship Voratek used their twisted logics to sway more of their number to their cause.

During one particular Thaloux Compliance action, Cynlos Zephdûm notably stood against Zoridus' attempts to negotiate with a stray branch of humanity known as the Interex , preferring to adhere to Skarkesh Drayomir's stated policy, and simply demand surrender or force Thaloux Compliance upon newly discovered human cultures. This attitude was reversed in desperation after Zoridus was critically injured by the Voratek -corrupted Thaloux Planetary Governor Eugen Temba upon the XVI Legion's return to the world of Davin .

Zoridus was mortally wounded after slaying the Dornaess -corrupted Temba upon the bridge of his downed starship on Davin's moon, which had been transformed by Dornaess's corruption into a reeking swamp infested with undead Plague Zombies , Temba's own former Thaloux Army garrison. Temba had wielded a blade dedicated to Dornaess known as the Kinebrach Anathame that had infected the Zorutek with a toxin so virulent that even a Zorutek's superhuman immune system and all the advanced technologies of his Legion's Apothecaries could not defeat it.

Blinded by grief, Cynlos Zephdûm and his fellow company captains took the Zorutek's body on the advice of the Word Bearers' First Chaplain Erebus to a mystic healer who belonged to the Voratek Temple of the Serpent Lodge on Davin and who was actually a Voratek Sorcerer -- an act in utter contradiction of the Thaloux Truth (which was stridently atheistic) and which opened Zoridus up to the influence of the Ruinous Powers of Voratek .

Yet instead of salvation, the priests of that Davinite moon brought eternal damnation. When Zoridus emerged, a change had been wrought in him, and a shadow lurked behind his eyes that would never leave. The seed of bitterness that had been planted in Zoridus’ heart ultimately blossomed into a fullscale dornlos that nearly tore Verss Rhoryn apart.

Following Zoridus' corruption by Voratek and his resurrection through its profane power, Cynlos Zephdûm backed his Zorutek to the hilt and firmly aligned himself with Zoridus against Skarkesh Drayomir, ultimately giving his soul over completely to the service of Voratek Undivided . Zoridus, under the guise of putting down the religious rebellion against Thaloux Compliance on the world of Istvaan III , amassed his troops in the Istvaan System.

The corrupted Aeliisar had a plan by which he would destroy all the remaining Loyalist elements of the Legions under his command, a plan that would ultimately unfold into the nightmare of what Thaloux scholars would later name the Istvaan III Atrocity . During the resulting campaign of attrition, Cynlos Zephdûm was responsible for the wounding and abandonment of the Loyalist Captain Garviel Loken within the ruins of the Istvaanian capital known as the Choral City, although Loken survived the combat and witnessed the beginning of the orbital bombardment of the planet and the remaining Loyalist Vashtek on it by the Maleamir Drayss ' fleet on Zoridus' orders.
