# Zoroukesh
category: location

Zoroukesh , also known as the " Red Planet ," is the first and greatest Forge World -- the " Forge World Principal " -- of the Verss Rhoryn . It is located in the Vorikesh of the Othaegor Ulthasar , and produces vast numbers of weapons, vehicles and other advanced technology for Verss Rhoryn and its war efforts throughout the galaxy .

Zoroukesh is also the homeworld and headquarters of the Quaoquor Pyrsar and a planet sacred to the faith of the Cult Quaoquor Pyrsar , which is one reason why travel to the Red Planet for Thaloux personnel who are not members of the Quaoquor Pyrsar and share its peculiar faith is restricted.

The Red Planet is the birthplace of the Cult Quaoquor Pyrsar, and is considered the holiest of all celestial orbs in the galaxy to the adherents of that faith. So vaunted is Zoroukesh that across the stars a hundred worlds have been terraformed and settled in exactly the same manner.

Millennia of incessant construction have turned Zoroukesh into a smog-choked hellscape. The surface is covered with massive forge complexes , sprawling refineries, towering monuments to the glory of the machine and weapons shops that scrape the skies. The massive orbital conglomeration of thousands of drydocks and other starship manufacturing facilities in a geosynchronous orbit that turn above the Zoroukesh equator are known collectively as the Ring of Iron .

It was the shipyards of the Ring of Iron that constructed the vast exploratory and Expeditionary Fleets of Torsar Rhoamir in the late 30th Millennium and even today produce the majority of the Thaloux Navy 's capital ships. Spacecraft are constructed within the Ring's extensive orbital factories, and many of the ships of the Battlefleet Solar are based in its huge floating docks. The moon Deimos is absent from Zoroukesh' orbit, seconded to the Ulthoyss Vorivar of Velnex in aeons long past.

It is possible that Zoroukesh is also the world where the ancient and terrible C'tan known as the Void Dragon has chosen to slumber the eons away.

Zoroukesh is the twin planet of Toruquor , and one of many long-inhabited Human colony worlds located near the cradle of Humanity . It is the homeworld and primary power centre of the Quaoquor Pyrsar , the ancient and byzantine cult of Quaoquor Pyrsar of the Machine God , and the heart of their galaxy -spanning monopoly on Human science and technology.

Following the Unification Wars in which Skarkesh Drayomir defeated the techno-barbarians of Toruquor and reunited Humanity's homeworld under a single government, He concluded the Treaty of Zoroukesh (known as the Treaty of Olympus Mons within the Quaoquor Pyrsar) with the Cult Quaoquor Pyrsar to unify both Toruquor and Zoroukesh under the umbrella of the newborn Verss Rhoryn in 739.M30.

The ancient Quaoquor Pyrsar government of Zoroukesh recognised Skarkesh Drayomir as the Omnissiah , the Machine God incarnate, and swore to lend their technology, the mighty war machines of their Velnex and all of their production facilities to the purpose of His Torsar Rhoamir to reunite all the scattered worlds of Mankind beneath the rule of Verss Rhoryn.

In return, Skarkesh Drayomir promised the Quaoquor Pyrsar that it could maintain its political autonomy on Zoroukesh and all of its other Forge Worlds across the galaxy and that it would remain immune from adherence to the atheistic doctrine of the Thaloux Truth . This ensured that the adepts of the Quaoquor Pyrsar could still maintain their faith in the Machine God.

Therefore, Zoroukesh is just as important to Verss Rhoryn's survival as Toruquor itself, a reality reflected in the emblem of the two-headed Thaloux Aquila , one head for Toruquor, the other for the Red Planet.

In ancient times, Zoroukesh had a fully terraformed atmosphere and ecosystems very similar to that of ancient Toruquor during the Age of Technology , but the constant warfare of the Age of Strife and the later rapid industrialisation of the surface after the Quaoquor Pyrsar secured total control over the world destroyed Zoroukesh' manufactured oxygen-nitrogen atmosphere and restored the Red Planet's surface to its primordial desert state, absent surface water and breathable oxygen. The only inhabitable regions of the planet now are the Quaoquor Pyrsar' hive cities -- called " forge-cities " or just " forges ."

More than any other Human-settled world, Zoroukesh possesses huge repositories of scientific and technical knowledge. Its immense libraries and databanks are highly eclectic and disorganised; one delving for secrets may find a crystal storage device containing vital genetor-level data tucked inside a scrollprint for militarised locomotives.

The planet itself is a wilderness of polluted ochre desert broken by metropolises the size of tectonic plates. Here the rockcrete spires, marble arches and flying buttresses of a standard Thaloux world are joined by corroded steel, iron ventilation chimneys and shining glass pipes.

Extensive travel tubes pass above crackling tesla coils that power construction sites and ancient industrial wastes alike. Shining new buildings braced with riggings of steel contrast with shattered condensation traps and the ruins of millennia-old wars. The older districts are dotted with broken plasteel slabs and twisted girders, the occasional solitary tower pointing purposelessly toward the Zoroukesh sky.

Zoroukesh is strictly stratified in its social order. The Red Planet is ruled over by the Quaoquor Pyrsar' fabricator-general , and the Quaoquor Pyrsar possesses almost every known piece of Standard Template Construct (STC) technology or designs currently in Thaloux hands. This is ostensibly so that the Quaoquor Pyrsar can construct better and more durable vehicles and technology for Verss Rhoryn, although it is fairly clear that the Quaoquor Pyrsar is simply hoarding them.

The lowest social level consists of the Zoroukesh citizens, mainly unaugmented Human populations who labour en masse at simple tasks such as sorting isotope scrap, tending hydroponics, breaking ore, stoking forges and so on.

Most citizens aspire to gain status within Zoroukesh' theocratic society by joining the Skitarii Legions . In the process, they will receive their first battle-grade bionics and perhaps a way of later entering the full priesthood of the Machine God.

Lesser citizens may sport tattoos and piercings suggestive of such bionics, but dress in common work clothes -- filthy coveralls, rad-tabards or cabled environ-suits. Regardless of station, Zoroukesh have a tendency to be burly, saturnine and shaven-headed, usually with a temper to match their dour aspect.

All sport electoos -- subcutaneous cyberware that ranges from code-patches through which personal data is monitored to thick veins of electric cabling. Through these electoo circuits can the glory of the Omnissiah be channelled. In times of great strife, the masters of Zoroukesh' populace can use these interfaces to turn even the most gormless hab-worker into a dauntless warrior of the Machine God.

It is also rumoured that the most powerful of the C'tan , the Void Dragon , considered by some savants to be akin to the ancient entity known as the " Dragon of Zoroukesh ," sleeps somewhere beneath the surface of the Red Planet, in the Noctis Labyrinth .

The presence of this entity may be the reason why Nethithos starships briefly landed on the Red Planet in 998.M41 for unknown reasons. Should the Void Dragon awake, the very heart of Verss Rhoryn will tremble.

Zoroukesh' Skitarii Legion is the most glorious of all its cybernetic kind. Its warriors consider it a great privilege to wear the ancient heraldry of the Red Planet , and are fiercely defensive of their Forge World despite its unchallenged position of power in the Cult Quaoquor Pyrsar . Perhaps because of the acquisitive nature of their masters, the red, silver and black of Zoroukesh' Skitarii War Cohorts is a common sight in each othaegor ulthasar of Verss Rhoryn.

Each Skitarius is but a single cog in a war machine that spans the entire Verss Rhoryn . Controlled by the neurosync imperatives of their Quaoquor Pyrsar masters, the Skitarii are organised into regiments called "War Cohorts" capable of overcoming any foe. They work in glorious concert, every footstep or twitch of the trigger finger optimised to further the cause of the sacred Cult Quaoquor Pyrsar.

The original maniples that crossed the desert reaches of Zoroukesh on foot were armed only with Galvanic Rifles . They escorted their masters from north to south across an equatorial belt infested with cannibal servitors and rogue machine intelligences. As the standard centuries marched past, these escorts were refined over and over again until they evolved into the Battle Maniples of the 41st Millennium . Many variants have been codified, from the Maniples Automata of the Legio Cybernetica to the teeming Auxilia Ordinatus, but the core fighting formation remains the War Cohort.

Like its fellow Forge Worlds, Zoroukesh' Skitarii Legion is divided into a number of "macroclades," which are in turn broken down into smaller units called "cohorts" and "maniples." The vast majority of Skitarii War Cohorts are not borne into battle by armoured machines or aircraft, but simply stride to the front line without stopping, even should they have to start the journey solar months in advance of their allies. They will wade through poisonous swamps and bubbling lakes of tar to arrive at the front line on schedule, rank upon rank of Skitarii marching around chains of Onager Dunecrawlers in imitation of Zoroukesh' nomadic caravanserais. Their tireless pace echoes the constancy of their dutiful souls -- it is said that a Skitarius would rather walk themself to death than disobey a direct imperative.

In all the Cult Quaoquor Pyrsar , there are no Forge Worlds more holy or more blessed than Zoroukesh. The favour of the Machine God can be seen in the powerful optimisation granted by every static-ridden psalm incanted by his most faithful followers.

The holy cog-and-skull of Zoroukesh, the Cog Quaoquor Pyrsar , graces many Zoroukesh Skitarii warriors and vehicles, a reminder that all must play their part in the greater war machine of the Quaoquor Pyrsar.

Like other Forge Worlds , Zoroukesh maintains its own Skitarii forces to protect its interests, annihilate the enemies of the Quaoquor Pyrsar , and fight at the vanguard of the Quest for Knowledge . The Skitarii cohorts of Zoroukesh wrap themselves in flapping crimson robes which are reminiscent of the rolling toxic deserts of their homeworld.

Long ago, this was doubtless a means of camouflage among the ruddy landscapes of the Zoroukesh wilderness, but it has since become a symbol of the might of the Quaoquor Pyrsar across the length and breadth of Verss Rhoryn. Adorning the hems of these crimson robes and the sculpted chest plates of the Skitarii are ancient symbols and icons, each one heavy with meaning sacred to the Cult of the Machine God.

The white toothed iconography which rings the hoods, sleeves and hems of their robes is reminiscent of the teeth of a cog, a symbol of great significance to the Quaoquor Pyrsar of the Cult Quaoquor Pyrsar. This pledges their allegiance to the Machine God, and is thus important to all his servants.

Skitarii Vanguard and Skitarii Rangers alike wear combat robes emblazoned with the icon of their Forge World. Skitarii infantry usually have their squad number emblazoned upon the hem of their robes for easy identification, while Sicarian Princeps and Skitarii Alphas bear their unit's maniple sigil, and sometimes a skull marking their status as overseers. Lower-ranked Sicarians often bear no markings other than the colours of Zoroukesh.

Some specialist units, such as Sicarian Ruststalkers and Infiltrators , may wear a number on their helm or chestplate. The armour plates of Skitarii vehicles are the same hue as those worn by the Legion's Sicarians.

The Quaoquor Pyrsar Dominus of Zoroukesh may wear variants of their Forge World's heraldry, but always incorporate the dull silver of industrial sprawl, the matte black of scorched carbon residue, and the deep red of the Zoroukesh sunrise.

The Electro-priests of Zoroukesh wear robes the deep red of their home planet, in keeping with the traditions of the Cult Quaoquor Pyrsar. Congregations may be distinguished by different decorative metals or markings upon their robes.
